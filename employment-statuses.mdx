In this guide you'll learn what employment statuses are, what they mean, and how they work – so you always know where your employees are in their lifecycle.

## What is an employment status?

It's a status that tells you where an employee is in their lifecycle. The employment status is shown under the employee's profile picture and is automatically determined based on dates that are set in their employments.

Employment statuses make it easier to filter and get an overview of your employees in Hailey. They also help you decide who should see which employees. If you don't want to show employees that have left, or haven't started yet for example - you can manage that through **Roles & rights**.

<Note>
  As an **Admin**, you always have access to all employees. **Managers** and **Employees** may need additional rights to see employees with certain employment statuses.
</Note>

## What do the employment statuses mean?

Hailey uses different employment statuses to reflect an employee's current employment situation.

- **Unknown** - The employee does not have an employment on their profile.
- **Being hired** - The employee is currently going through the hiring process and has not yet started their employment.
- **Will join** - The employee's start date is in the future.
- **In service** - The employee's start date has passed, and the employment is currently active.
- **Leaving** - The employee has an end date that is today or in the future.
- **Garden leave** - The employee's last working day has passed, but their employment remains active until the employment end date.
- **Has left** - The employee's employment end date has passed.

## How to update an employment status

In most cases, the employment status updates automatically based on the dates set on the employee's employment – the status will change the day when the set date arrives – meaning you don't need to do anything manually.

However, there are a few exceptions where dates aren't the only thing determining the status:

- **Being hired:** We always recommend completing the hiring process. If that's not possible, you can cancel it by going to the employee's profile and clicking **Actions** - **Cancel hiring**.
- **Has left:** The employee needs to be **Rehired or re-activated** to get a new status. This is done via the employees profile.
- **Unknown:** This status can only be changed by clicking **Actions** - **Add employment** from the employee profile making sure to add an employment with a start date.

<Tip>
  Read more about **Rehiring and re-activating** employees [**HERE**](/rehiring-wizard), and click [**THIS**](/employment-wizard) link to learn how to **Add employment** to an already existing employee.
</Tip>

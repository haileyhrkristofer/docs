## What does candidate management mean?

Candidate management describes how you work with candidates throughout the recruitment process - from when an application is submitted until a candidate is hired.

It helps you stay organized, follow each candidate’s journey through the process, and ensure a clear and structured recruitment experience.

## Before you get started

To receive applications from candidates, you first need to create a **Career page**, a **Recruitment**, and a **Job Ad**.

- Read more about how to create your **Career page** in [**THIS**](/career-page) guide.
- Read more about how to create your **Recruitment** in [**THIS**](/start-and-manage-recruitment) guide.
- Read more about how to create your **Job Ad** in [**THIS**](/create-and-manage-job-ads) guide.

## Enable notifications

To ensure that you are notified whenever new applications are received, you can **Enable notifications for new applicants**.

This setting can be configured by navigating to **Recruitment** in the main menu, selecting the relevant recruitment process, and then clicking the number next to the **New Applications** stage. Next, click **Enable notifications for new applicants** to update your notification settings.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-11-27-01-8687-AM.png?width=670&height=379&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-11-27-01-8687-AM.png)

Once you start communicating with your candidates, you can also enable notifications for selected candidates when they send you a **Message**. This setting can be enabled by clicking the **Bell icon** at the top of the candidate's profile.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-48-45-8774-AM.png?width=670&height=377&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-48-45-8774-AM.png)

When the setting is enabled, the recruiter will receive an email containing a link to the candidate's profile whenever the candidate sends a message. This is a personal setting, which means it is not automatically enabled for all recruiters when someone chooses to activate it.

Read more about how to communicate with your candidates later in this guide.

## How do you manage candidate applications?

Once the **Job Ad** has been published and the recruitment process is underway, applications will hopefully start coming in.

Navigate to **Recruitment** in the main menu and click on the active recruitment process to view all new applications.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-30-44-8082-PM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-30-44-8082-PM.png)

If needed, you can also add applications manually. To do this, click **Manage** - **Add applicant manually**. When adding an application manually, two fields are required:

- **First Name**
- **Last Name**

You can also optionally provide the candidate’s Email Address, Phone Number, and upload a CV.

Both candidates who have applied themselves and those added manually will appear on the Kanban board with their first and last names displayed. To view more information about a candidate, click on their name.

For each candidate, you can view their contact details and complete application, including their CV, cover letter, and responses to any custom questions that were included in the application process.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-07-49-53-3543-AM.png?width=670&height=381&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-07-49-53-3543-AM.png)

In the first section, you can also view a **CV Summary** **from** **Peep**. Peep is an AI-powered tool that summarizes key information from a candidate’s CV and presents it in a clear and easy-to-read format.

Please note that Peep may make mistakes, so always verify the information against the original documents. We also encourage you to provide feedback on the CV Summary by clicking the **thumbs up** or **thumbs down** icon. Your feedback will be shared with Hailey to help improve Peep.

In your **GDPR settings**, you can decide how long candidate data should be stored in Hailey. Read more about this in [**THIS**](/gdpr) guide.

### Filter candidates based on their application responses

You can easily filter candidates based on the questions you have added to the application form. To filter responses in a candidate’s application, click the **filter icon**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-07-54-15-2273-AM.png?width=670&height=383&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-07-54-15-2273-AM.png)

Filtering is only available for **Yes/No**, **Single Choice**, and **Multiple Choice** question types, but you can apply filters to either a single question or multiple questions at the same time.

Select the responses you would like to filter by and then click **Apply**. Only candidates whose responses match the selected criteria will be displayed, while all other candidates will be hidden from the list. To display all candidates again, simply click **Clear Filters**.

Read more about how to create questions in the **Application form** in [**THIS**](/create-and-manage-job-ads) guide

## How do you add notes to a candidate profile?

You can add **internal notes** about each candidate under the **Notes** tab. These notes are not visible to candidates; they can only be viewed by users who are involved in the recruitment process. To add a note, click **Add**.

You can also **Edit** or **Delete** existing notes by clicking the three dots to the right of the specific note.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-38-01-0138-AM.png?width=670&height=381&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-38-01-0138-AM.png)

## How do you communicate with candidates?

Communication with candidates takes place directly from the candidate’s profile under the **Messages** tab. You can either write a custom message or select one of your saved **Message Templates** by clicking the plus icon. The message will be sent directly to the candidate’s email address.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-40-53-9237-AM.png?width=670&height=384&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-40-53-9237-AM.png)

How do you create **Message Templates**? Read more about it in [**THIS**](/message-templates) guide

To communicate with multiple candidates at the same time, click on the candidates’ initials. A **lightning bolt icon** will then appear in the lower-right corner. Click it and select the message icon.

From this view, you can also write a custom message or choose one of your saved **Message Templates.** When composing a custom message from this view, you can use placeholders to make the message more personalized.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-14-2026-06-27-12-9358-AM.png?width=670&height=354&name=image-png-Jul-14-2026-06-27-12-9358-AM.png)

When a candidate replies, their response is automatically added to the **Messages** tab on their profile. In addition, a notification will appear for the entire recruitment process, as well as within the specific recruitment where the message was received.

## Managing candidates through the recruitment process

From a candidate’s profile, below their name, you can see which stage of the recruitment process they are currently in. To move a candidate to a different stage, click **Actions** → **Move Application**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-42-39-1291-AM.png?width=670&height=380&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-09-42-39-1291-AM.png)

You can also move a candidate directly from the Kanban board by dragging and dropping them into the desired stage.

To move multiple candidates at the same time, select their initials from the overview page. Then click the **lightning bolt icon -** **arrow icon** and move them to the appropriate stage.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-14-2026-06-24-06-5993-AM.png?width=670&height=357&name=image-png-Jul-14-2026-06-24-06-5993-AM.png)

### References

When it is time to conduct reference checks, move the candidate to the **References** stage on the Kanban board. Read more about **Reference Checks** in [**THIS**](/template-new-article-0-0) guide.

### To be hired

Once you have found the perfect match, you will naturally want to hire them. This is done by moving the candidate to the **To be hired** stage. When moving a candidate to this stage, you will have the option to **Initiate hire**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-14-2026-06-22-00-5120-AM.png?width=670&height=326&name=image-png-Jul-14-2026-06-22-00-5120-AM.png)

Read more about how to **Initiate hire** in [**THIS**](/iniate-hire) guide.

### Rejected

Candidates who will not be offered the role should be moved to the **Rejected** stage on the Kanban board. These candidates will remain in the recruitment process, making it possible to move one or more of them back into the process if needed.

## Rights

As an Admin in Hailey, you automatically have the access to **Recruitments** right. Read more about **Roles & right** in [**THIS**](/roles-and-rights) guide.

- **None** - The Recruitment section is **hidden** from the main menu.
- **View** - Allows the user to **view** all content within the **Recruitment section** in the main menu, including **Career Pages, Recruitments,** and **Job ads.**
- **View & Edit** - Gives the user **full access** to manage the **Recruitment module**, including the ability to create, edit, and delete **Recruitments, Career Pages, and Job Ads.**

<Note>
  Do you have hiring managers or employees who should only have access to specific recruitments? Read more about this in [**THIS**](/start-and-manage-recruitment#set_your_rights) guide.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn’t click Initiate hire when I moved the candidate to the To be hired stage, and now the option no longer appears. What should I do?">
    No problem. Simply move the candidate back to a previous stage and then move them again to the To be hired stage. The Initiate hire wizard will then appear again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I accidentally deleted a candidate’s application. How can I restore it?">
    Unfortunately, it is not possible to recover a deleted application, as the action is permanent. For this reason, we recommend moving candidates who will not be offered the position to the Rejected stage instead of deleting their applications.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I update a candidate’s contact information after their application has been submitted?">
    You can edit a candidate’s contact details by clicking on the candidate in the Kanban board and then clicking on the candidate’s phone number or email address displayed below their full name.

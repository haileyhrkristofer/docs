Build a safer workplace with **HSE Deviations**. In this guide, you'll learn how to enable the feature, manage permissions, report deviations, and follow up on submitted cases. You'll also get an overview of categories, responsibilities, and how to ensure an effective deviation management process.

## What is HSE Deviations?

**HSE Deviations** help organizations report, investigate, and follow up on incidents related to health, safety, and the work environment. By recording deviations, near misses, and risk observations, organizations can identify root causes, take corrective actions, and proactively improve workplace safety.

## How do you set up HSE Deviation?

To start reporting **HSE Deviations**, you first need to activate the feature. Navigate to **Incidents** from the home screen and click the **Settings icon** in the top-right corner. When the settings panel opens, enable the feature using the toggle. You then need to assign a handler for each category.

Categories available includes:

- **Stress & Wellbeing**
- **Ergonomics & Physical strain**
- **Unsafe behaviour & Procedure Violation**
- **Tools & Equipment**
- **Personal injury**
- **Near miss**
- **Workplace risk**
- **Work enviroment**
- **Environmental deviation**
- **Other**

## How do you report deviations from the Hailey app?

You report a deviation from your **Dashboard** in the **Hailey app** by clicking on the **plus sign** in the upper right corner - **Report HSE Deviation**.

<Note>
  Note: You can only report HSE deviations through the mobile app.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-44-48-4324-AM.png?width=430&height=896&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-44-48-4324-AM.png)

Next step is to choose a **category** for your **Deviation**. Then you describe the incident in writing.

You can also **add pictures** by clicking on the **plus sign** by **Attachments**. The last step is to choose if you want to be **anonymous** in your deviation report. If you want to be anonymous you need to toogle on the button. Your name and organizational belonging will then not be visible.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-48-51-4198-AM.png?width=420&height=875&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-48-51-4198-AM.png)

You will be able to follow your reported deviation from your **Dashboard** in the mobile app. The **Deviation** can have three statuses:

- **Unhandled** - no Handler has taken action to your reported deviation
- **Being handled** - one or several handlers has taken action to your reported deviation
- **Closed**- a Handler has updated the deviation and closed it.

## How do you handle a reported Deviation?

When an employee has reported a deviation, **all** **Handlers** for the chosen category is given an email notification. Click on **Go to Hailey** and we will take you to **Incidents - HSE Deviations** where you can take action.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-58-21-3005-AM.png?width=670&height=422&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-58-21-3005-AM.png)

Next step is to click on the relevant **deviation** and you will se more detailed information and being able to take action! Give the deviation **Handler(s)** and set a **Deadline** for when it should be resolved.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-00-23-2459-AM.png?width=670&height=330&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-00-23-2459-AM.png)

By clicking on **Notes**, you can easily follow up on your work during the process.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-03-37-8674-AM.png?width=670&height=266&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-03-37-8674-AM.png)

You can also change the category by clicking on **More - Change category.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-04-45-1381-AM.png?width=670&height=321&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-04-45-1381-AM.png)

When the **Deviation** is resolved, click on **More** and **Close the deviation**. You are required to give a **closing message** to the reporting employee so they understand what was done and why the issue is resolved.

<Note>
  Once you close the deviation, the reporting employee will see your closing notes and the deviation will be marked as closed. You can't reopen it after this.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-05-45-8337-AM.png?width=670&height=328&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-05-45-8337-AM.png)

<Note>
  By closing the deviation, the reporting employee will get at a push notification and the update card will have status **Closed** with a **closing** **comment**.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-07-25-7279-AM.png?width=430&height=898&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-09-07-25-7279-AM.png)

## Rights

The rights listed below are relevant to access **HSE Deviations**. As an **admin,** you do **not** automatically have these rights. You must manually add these rights to a role that you are a member of.

- Enable **Manage deviations.**
- The right to **Manage deviations** allows users to manage all **HSE deviations**, regardless of organizational affiliation or where in the organization the deviation was reported.
- Remember to assign the employee as the responsible person for a category. Only assigned users can manage deviations within the categories they are responsible for.

## What is a survey in Hailey?

Surveys help you capture employee's opinions, experiences, and feedback. By tracking responses and results in Hailey, you can gain valuable insights that support your organization's development!

<Note>
  To send out a survey, you first need to create a survey template. Read more about how to do this [**HERE**](/surveys-in-hailey).
</Note>

## How do you send a survey?

- To send out a survey, mark the employees you want to send the survey to in the employee list.
- When the employees are marked in the list, click **Action** and **Send out a survey.**

<Note>
  Remember to make sure everyone you want to send the survey to are included in the list!
</Note>

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/01.png)

- Choose which one of your templates you want to send and name the survey.
- Set a deadline for when the employees must have answered the survey. It is possible to answer it even if the deadline has passed.
- Click Send to send the survey.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/02.png)

<Note>
  Surveys are delivered as tasks in the system and end up on the employees' dashboards. An email is also sent informing them that a survey has been sent to them, the employee can click here to go to the survey.
</Note>

## How do employees answer a survey?

When you have a survey to answer, an email is being sent and a task appears on your dashboard. It's possible to answer the survey from both ways.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/03.png)

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/04.png)

When you click the task in Hailey or on the link in the email, you will be sent to a new tab in your browser. Here you will find your company logo, a text about the anonymity, a potential description of the survey, and the deadline. Click Answer survey to start answer.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/05.png)

The survey is completed section by section, with the questions included in each respective section. Once all questions have been answered, click Send.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/06.png)

When you have sent your answer, you'll get a confirmation that your answer has been submitted. The survey task will also disappear from your dashboard.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/07.png)

## View and analyze the results from your survey

The results of a survey are found under the tab **Performance** in the main menu. Click on **Surveys** to view the results. It is required that at least 3-10 answers from employees have been received, depending on which setting you have set in the template used. This is to maintain anonymity.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/08.png)

The answers in a survey are presented question by question together with a percentage of how many of the employees that have chosen each answer alternative.

In the left-hand column, you can filter the results based on organizational belonging, as well gender and whether the employee is a manager. The filtering is based on the information that was valid at the time the survey was distributed. To maintain anonymity, the selected filter must include at least the minimum number of responses defined in the survey template before results can be displayed.

<Note>
  Participants with associated profile picture/initials do not represent who has answered, but who has been invited to the survey.
</Note>

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/09.png)

The option to send reminders is available here, which means that if you click on **Actions** - **Send** **Reminder**, an email will be sent to the employees who have not yet responded to the survey, urging them to do so as soon as possible. Reminders can only be sent once per day, and the date of the last reminder is shown.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/10.png)

## End your survey

Surveys can be deleted or ended through the three dots in the corner. You can also end the survey by **Actions - End survey.**

A deleted survey is completely deleted and an ended survey will be saved under the status **Done** to the left and will not take in new answers. Once a survey has been ended, it is no longer possible to send reminders.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/11.png)

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/12.png)

## Generate an AI summary from Peep

If AI settings for Surveys are enabled in your account, you can generate an AI summary of survey results. When a survey is closed for additional responses (once the anonymity threshold has been reached), you will be given the option to create an AI-generated summary. When you close your survey, you will be given the option to **Generate Peep-summary**.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/13.png)

It is also possible to generate an AI summary for surveys that have already been closed. Click **Actions - Generate summary from Peep**.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/14.png)

Peep will generate a summary from each section in your company language. You can then provide feedback on the summary by clicking the **thumbs-up** or **thumbs-down button** and leaving a comment.

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/15.png)

<Note>
  Please note that if you apply filters to the survey results, the summary will be hidden.
</Note>

## Export the result from the survey

If you want to export the raw data from your employee surveys, go to the survey you want to download the data from. You then click on **Actions** in the top right corner and you will then get the following alternatives:

- Download to **Excel (xlsx)**
- Download to **Powerpoint**

<Note>
  If filtering has been applied when retrieving the results, it will be included in the export. This makes it easy to retrieve results per department, for example, which can then be shared with relevant individuals without revealing the entire survey's results.
</Note>

![](/images/start-and-follow-your-surveys/16.png)

## Rights

If you would like someone other than Admin in your account to be able to send and manage surveys, the following rights are relevant:

- **Send surveys** - **Enable** allows the right to send surveys from the employee list
- **View all surveys** - **Enable** allows the right to view all surveys within the account
- **View my surveys** - **Enable** allows the right to view surveys that they have sent themselves.

<Note>
  Read more about Roles & rights [**HERE**](/roles-and-rights)!
</Note>

## FAQ's

**Question 1:** I want to see how all managers have responded and then share the results with them, without giving them access to the responses from the entire survey. How can I do that?

In the left-hand panel, under **Other filters**, select **Yes** in the **Is manager** field. This will filter the results so that only responses from managers are shown.

Once the filter has been applied, you can export the results to **Excel (.xlsx)** or **PowerPoint** and share them with your managers. This allows them to view the filtered results without gaining access to the responses from the entire survey!

**Question 2:** I want to filter the results by **Team**, but when I click the field it says **No data**. Why?

When creating a survey template, you define how many responses are required before results can be displayed. This setting also applies when filtering results.

For example, if you have chosen that at least **5 responses** are required to display results, there must be at least 5 responses within the team you are filtering by. If the team has fewer responses than the required threshold, **No data** will be displayed. This is done to ensure anonymity.

Read more about this [**HERE**](/surveys-in-hailey)!

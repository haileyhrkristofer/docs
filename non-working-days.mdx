title: "Non-working days"
---

Does your organization have non-working days? By setting them up in Hailey, employees will find it easier to report their worked hours accurately.

## What is Non-working days?

When your employees are reporting time in Hailey, you can specify which days that are non-working days. When this is applied on the employees profile, the non-working days will be shown when navigating to the **Time tab** on the employees profile.

**Non-working days** are marked as gray in the time overview and they are also marked with a pink box with a description for each employee. When you add non-working days for an employee, the employee **can not register time or time off on these days**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-20-2026-03-34-57-8314-PM.png?width=665&height=384&name=image-png-Apr-20-2026-03-34-57-8314-PM.png)

## How do you create Non-working days?

Get started by clicking on **Settings - Time & time off - Add new - Non-working days**. You can choose a predefined calendar containing national holidays and choose whether Saturdays and/or Sundays should be considered non-working days.

You also have the option to add custom days as non-working days, such as a bridge day or Christmas Eve. Specify the date for the day in question along with a description. Then click **Save**.

    No, the system does not support configuring half-days as non-working days. Only full non-working days can be configured.
  </Accordion>

## What does rehire and re-activate mean?

When a former employee returns to your organisation, you can easily **rehire** them directly from their employee card. This starts the hiring process and lets you create a new employment on the existing employee profile. You can then register the new employment details, configure a contract and collect any required information from the employee through the candidate- and preboardingportals - all in one seamless process.

If an employee was accidentally terminated, you can instead **reactivate** their employment directly from the employee profile. This restores the employee's previous employment, allowing you to easily reverse the termination and avoid going through the rehire process.

## How do you rehire an employee?

For employees that have left your company and have the employment status **Has left,** you can rehire them from their profile. The employee that has left will have the **Rehire** option visible below their name.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Feb-17-2026-12-05-24-0328-PM.png?width=670&height=274&name=image-png-Feb-17-2026-12-05-24-0328-PM.png)

Click on **Rehire**, and the rehiring wizard will guide through the rehiring process. The wizard follows the same steps as when you initiate a hire in Hailey where you fill out the employment terms, collect information about the employee and sign a contract.

![Skärmavbild 2026-07-27 kl. 15.36.40](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-27%20kl.%2015.36.40.png?width=670&height=506&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-27%20kl.%2015.36.40.png)

<Note>
  You can find the guide for the hiring process in Hailey [HERE](/iniate-hire#initiate_hire).
</Note>

When rehiring a person who previously worked for you and had an account in Hailey, consider the following:

- If the account is locked, remember to unlock the account for the person so they can log in again. You can do this either by selecting the person from the employee list and clicking **Action** - **Unlock employees Hailey account**, or by opening the person’s employee card and clicking **Action - Unlock account.**
- Go through the data that remains on the person's employee card and what data has been deleted due to your GDPR settings. Learn more about the GPPR settings [HERE](/gdpr).

## How do you re-activate an employee?

If an employment has been ended by mistake, you can re-activate the employee directly from the employee card. The former employee will then have a **Re-activate employee** button under their name, which you can use to restore their previous employment without going through the rehire process.

![Skärmavbild 2026-07-27 kl. 15.33.51](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-27%20kl.%2015.33.51.png?width=670&height=413&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-27%20kl.%2015.33.51.png)

<Note>
  Note that when re-activating an employee, **all GDPR-cleared fields must be filled in again** on the employee card.
</Note>

When you re-activate an employee, the following will happen:

- The **End date** and **Last working day** fields of the most recent employment will be cleared.
- If there are multiple employments with the same end date, the end date will be cleared for **all of them**.
- The GDPR data deletion of personal information will be stopped.

## Rights

To rehire and re-activate, the following rights are required from others than admin:

- **Initiate hire**
- **View** **employees that have left**

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A former colleague is returning to our organization. Should I reactivate or rehire the employee?">
    **Rehire** - it allows you to create a new employment with updated employment details, generate a new employment contract, and collect the employee's current information – all in one seamless workflow.

    **Question**: I reactivated an employee, but some information is still missing. Why?

    When an employee is terminated, the GDPR data deletion process starts immediately and removes data according to your data retention policy that can be found in **Settings - Security - GDPR**.

    If any information has already been deleted, it needs to be filled in again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I rehired an employee, but they can't log in to Hailey. Why?">
    The employee's account may have been locked when their previous employment was terminated. A locked account prevents the employee from logging in.

    To resolve this, unlock the account by either:

    - selecting the employee in the **Employee List** and clicking **Actions - Unlock Hailey account**
    - opening the employee card and clicking **Actions - Unlock account**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How AI works in Hailey

Hailey offers AI-powered features that help with information retrieval, content generation, and automation. AI can be used in the Peep chatbot, to create contract templates, job advertisements, and survey templates, as well as to enhance bot functionality in platforms such as Teams and Slack. AI usage is optional, disabled by default, and only has access to information available within Hailey and its approved data sources.

**Hailey, as a system, is not responsible for the content generated by the AI.**

- The AI is based on Google's open model Gemma and is provided by the European service provider Scaleway S.A.S.
- It's important to note that using AI is entirely optional and not mandatory. The AI settings is per default inactivated in Hailey.
- The responsibility for informing employees about how AI is utilized within Hailey lies with you.

If you're interested in finding out more about the providers of the AI capabilities that Hailey utilizes, you can find more information here:

- [Google Gemma](https://ai.google.dev/gemma/docs/core)
- [Scaleway Managed Inference](https://www.scaleway.com/en/inference/)

You can find Hailey's AI settings under Settings - Security and AI. This is where you configure your AI preferences. Below, you'll find more information about each specific function.

<Note>
  Please note that AI-settings can only be edited by Admin-users in Hailey.
</Note>

## Peep - AI Chatbot

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-13-39-4259-PM.png?width=670&height=312&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-13-39-4259-PM.png)

Peep is available to all employees in the mobile app. It helps users quickly find information about colleagues, answer questions, and generate employee headcount and summaries based on available employee data, such as departments or locations.

**What can Peep do?**

- Search for information from the employee list
- Retrieve information about an individual employee
- Count and summarize **employee headcount**
- Search and list **articles** from Company Corner
- Answer questions based on the content of **Company corner articles**

**What information does Peep have access to?**

- All information available in the employee directory, except salary information
- All published **articles** in Company corner

<Note>
  Peep cannot retrieve or summarize employee salary data, access the content of uploaded documents in Company corner, or answer questions that require information outside the data sources it has access to.
</Note>

## Contract

If you allow AI in contract, it means that when creating contract templates, your team can utilize AI to assist in drafting the contract content.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-13-49-0159-PM.png?width=670&height=312&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-13-49-0159-PM.png)

You can access this by going to **Settings** - **Contract** **repository**, clicking **Add** **new** and choosing **AI Generate**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-37-28-0359-PM.png?width=670&height=360&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-37-28-0359-PM.png)

The user then needs to provide the information needed in order to create the template wanted. When clicking **Generate**, the template will be created.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-02-49-11-8142-PM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-02-49-11-8142-PM.png)

When generated, a preview of the generated template will appear. Here, the possibility to choose between cancelling, editing or saving is shown.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-02-50-46-2443-PM.png?width=670&height=355&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-02-50-46-2443-PM.png)

If the user choose to edit, it's possible to adjust the template by, for example, adding codes and input fields. From this view the user can edit the template just the same as when creating it from scratch.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-02-51-49-2038-PM.png?width=670&height=352&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-02-51-49-2038-PM.png)

## Bot

If you activate our Bot, it means that employees can access in-depth information from your Teams or Slack related to your organization. Click on **Bot** to enable the AI setting.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-14-02-0071-PM.png?width=670&height=311&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-14-02-0071-PM.png)

When the Bot is activated, in addition to the preset information, it is possible to obtain additional details from your Teams or Slack in the form of contact information for employees' colleagues. The Bot can provide work phone numbers and company emails, and below you can see an example of how this appears in Teams.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-28-2024-09-01-27-8953-AM.png?width=670&height=304&name=image-png-May-28-2024-09-01-27-8953-AM.png)

<Note>
  Note that the integration for each respective system must be activated for the bot to be usable.

  Please read more about the Hailey Teams bot [**HERE**](/sv/integration-with-teams) and Hailey Slack bot **[HERE](/sv/integration-with-slack)!**
</Note>

## Recruitment

If you choose to activate AI for recruitment, it means that employees using the recruitment functionality will be able to create detailed job descriptions as well as generating a CV summary from Peep with the help of AI. Click on **Recruitment** to activate the AI setting.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-13-49-0159-PM.png?width=670&height=312&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-13-49-0159-PM.png)

### Generate a job description

When the setting is activated, it allows employees with access to recruitment in Hailey to create creative and detailed job advertisements. The AI assistance option is available from the Create Job Ad view, and users can click on AI assistance to generate a job advertisement.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-04-05-4720-PM.png?width=638&height=337&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-04-05-4720-PM.png)

Users then need to enter the required information to create the desired job advertisement. When the user clicks **Generate**, the job advertisement will be created.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-04-53-0032-PM.png?width=642&height=339&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-04-53-0032-PM.png)

When the template is generated, a draft of the generated job description will be displayed. Here, you have the option to **Cancel**, **Re-generate**, or **Insert** **job** **description**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-06-28-2755-PM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-06-28-2755-PM.png)

If you choose to **Insert** the job description, it can be edited from this view in the same way as when it is created from scratch. Read more about creating job ads **[HERE](/create-and-manage-job-ads)!**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-08-39-2545-PM.png?width=665&height=350&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-08-39-2545-PM.png)

### Generate a CV summary from Peep

When the setting is activated, Peep will generate a CV summary from candidates in a recruitment process. CV summaries a generated for both candidates who apply through the recuritment process and candidates who are added manually.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-34-43-1941-PM.png?width=670&height=358&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-34-43-1941-PM.png)

<Note>
  Peep only generates summaries for documents uploaded through fields named **CV**, **Resume**, or **Curriculum Vitae** (or similar). Documents uploaded through fields with other names, such as **Passport Copy**, will not be summarized.
</Note>

## Surveys

If you choose to activate AI for Surveys, you will be able to use AI to generate survey templates and create Peep summaries based on your survey results. Click on **Surveys** to activate this.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-14-24-2355-PM.png?width=670&height=312&name=image-png-Jul-15-2026-12-14-24-2355-PM.png)

### Generate a survey template

When the setting is activated, it allows all users with the right to View & edit Survey templates to generate them with AI. When adding new survey templates, you will then get the option to generate with AI.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-09-57-9263-PM.png?width=669&height=352&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-09-57-9263-PM.png)

First, you need to select the number of answers required before you can see the results of your survey. Then, fill in your prompt with the necessary information to create the survey template you want. When you click **Generate**, your template will be created. This can take up to a minute.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-10-37-8776-PM.png?width=665&height=352&name=image-png-Sep-11-2026-03-10-37-8776-PM.png)

Once the template is generated, you can choose to **Cancel**, **Edit** or **Save** the template. Once saved, the template will be selectable when sending out a survey through the employee list. Read more about starting Surveys **[HERE](/start-and-follow-your-surveys)!**

### Generate a Peep summary for your results

When the setting is activated, you will be given the option **Generate Peep-summary** for your survey results once you close your survey.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-03-2026-02-36-55-6282-PM.png?width=670&height=352&name=image-png-Jul-03-2026-02-36-55-6282-PM.png)

It is also possible to generate an AI summary for surveys that have already been closed. Click **Actions - Generate summary from Peep**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-03-2026-02-36-25-1040-PM.png?width=670&height=349&name=image-png-Jul-03-2026-02-36-25-1040-PM.png)

Peep will generate a summary from each section in your company language. You can then provide feedback on the summary by clicking the **thumbs-up** or **thumbs-down button** and leaving a comment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-03-2026-02-37-42-5308-PM.png?width=670&height=327&name=image-png-Jul-03-2026-02-37-42-5308-PM.png)

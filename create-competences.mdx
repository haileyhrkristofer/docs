This guide helps you get started with competences! Using competences, you’ll gain a clear structure for identifying, developing, and tracking the skills your organisation needs to grow.

## What is competences?

Competencies in Hailey give you a clear and structured overview of what your organization is capable of today and what you need going forward. They enable you to work more effectively with development, recruitment, and planning, while creating better opportunities for employees to grow. This helps you make more informed decisions and take a more strategic approach to managing your organization’s capabilities.

## How do you create competences?

To create a compentence, navigate to **Settings** - **Skills**, click **Add new** and choose **Competence**. Set the name, description and a target raise of the competence.

Then choose whether you want this competence to be added to employees at a specific **Location**, **Business Area**, **Department**, **Team** or **Title**. This means that the competence will appear under **Competences** on their Employee card. Click on **Save**.

<Note>
  If you enter values in multiple fields for which the competency is for, e.g. one value in the Location field and one in the Department field, the employee must belong to both the Location **and** the Department for the competence to appear under Competences on the employee card.
</Note>

## Rights

- **View** on your own profile makes it possible to see all information under tab **Competences** on your own profile.
- **View** on other employees makes it possible to see all information under tab **Competences** on the employee profiles + access to **Skills - Competences** in the main menu for employees who is scoped.
- **View & edit** competences gives you the right to view, add and delete competences.
- **Settings - Create** competences gives full access to create new and manage already added competences under **Settings**.

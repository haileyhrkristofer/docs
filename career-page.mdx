## What is a Career page?

A career page is a webpage where you showcase your open positions and share information about what it's like to work at your company. This is where potential candidates come to learn more about your organization and apply for jobs.

To be able to receive applications through Hailey’s recruitment module, you need to create at least one career page.

## How do you create a Career page?

To create your career page, select **Recruitment** in the main menu. Then click on **Actions - Create career page.**

<Note>
  Are you a company with **multiple Legal entities?** No worries! Hailey will allow you to create multiple career pages - all for the ease of your future colleagues.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-01-58-16-9996-PM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-01-58-16-9996-PM.png)

Start by changing the logo for the career page if needed. Hailey will automatically select your **default logo.** Then **name** the career page and choose which **language** the page should be displayed in. This can easily be changed later if needed or desired.

If you want to direct your candidates to a specific privacy policy, enter the URL in the Privacy Policy field. If the field is left empty, Hailey’s own Privacy Policy will be available for the candidate to review.

This is also where the subdomain for the career page is configured. Please note that the domain name must be unique and therefore cannot be reused for any other career pages you may create.

<Warning>
  Note that once the **subdomain** has been saved, it **cannot** be edited or renamed.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The language setting determines which language the career page is displayed in when candidates use it.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-01-59-09-9914-PM.png?width=670&height=357&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-01-59-09-9914-PM.png)

The first step in creating the **Career page** is to create the **Home page.** Hailey will initially select your default cover image. You can easily change it by clicking on the image and selecting another file. You can also add **a title** and an **optional subtitle** for the career page.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-01-11-9708-PM.png?width=670&height=355&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-01-11-9708-PM.png)

<Note>
  For cover images, images, and logos to be available for selection, they must be uploaded under **Settings - Branding**. Learn more about this [**HERE.**](/admin-get-started#Branding)
</Note>

On the second step, an **About us** page can be created. This is optional and needs to be activated using the **Active button** in the right-hand side menu in order for it to be visible on the career page.

In this step, you can create different blocks for cover images, text boxes, images and even videos to present the company and its various departments. Once the blocks have been created, you can use drag and drop to move them around as you wish.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-10-36-26-7493-AM.png?width=670&height=338&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-10-36-26-7493-AM.png)

In the third step, you can choose whether **departments** should be included **on the career page,** along with an accompanying text and image.

For a department to be displayed, it needs to be added by selecting it in the right-hand sidebar. Once a department has been added, you can click on it to **add an image and text**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-10-37-33-4666-AM.png?width=670&height=320&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-10-37-33-4666-AM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-10-38-01-9220-AM.png?width=670&height=281&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-10-38-01-9220-AM.png)

Once this is done and the career page is saved, the structure of the career page is complete, but it **can be changed at any time** if necessary or when you want to make changes. We will now move on to making the **career page public!**

To make the career page visible to applicants, go to **Publish** in the top right corner. Here you can approve **making the career page visible to applicants.**

<img src="https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-02-49-8088-PM.png?width=670&height=355&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-02-49-8088-PM.png" alt="" /> Once you have saved, you can view the career page by clicking on **View Career page** in the top right-hand corner.

<Note>
  To give applicants access to your career page, we recommend embedding the link on your own website.
</Note>

Once your Career Page is created, the next step is to start a Recruitment. After that, you can create and link Job Ads to the Recruitment. Read more about how to **Start a Recruitment** [HERE.](/start-and-manage-recruitment)

## How do you edit the Career page?

If necessary, you can of course also **edit** a career page by clicking on the settings icon in the top right-hand corner. To do this, navigate to the career page in question. Click on the page you want to change. The following can be edited:

- Title
- Subtitle
- Language (is changed by clicking on the settings icon in the top right corner)
- Name of the page in Hailey (is changed by clicking on the settings icon in the top right corner)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-11-43-7866-PM.png?width=670&height=354&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-11-43-7866-PM.png)

One can also delete a career page. This is done from the career page overview. Click on the three dots to the right and then on **Delete**.

<Note>
  Deleting the career page cannot be undone! If you delete the entire career page, any job ads published on the page will also be unpublished.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-06-30-5437-PM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-Aug-10-2026-02-06-30-5437-PM.png)

## Rights

As an Admin in Hailey, you automatically have the access to **Recruitments** right, including the **Career Page**. Read more about **Roles & right** in [**THIS**](/roles-and-rights) guide.

- **None** - The Recruitment section is **hidden** from the main menu.
- **View** - Allows the user to **view** all content within the **Recruitment section** in the main menu, including **Career Pages, Recruitments,** and **Job ads.**
- **View & Edit** - Gives the user **full access** to manage the **Recruitment module**, including the ability to create, edit, and delete **Recruitments, Career Pages, and Job Ads.**

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a career page to publish job ads in Hailey?">
    Yes, in order to create job ads in Hailey and receive applications from candidates, you need to have a career page set up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which URL should I use to embed the Career Page on our website?">
    You should use the URL for the subdomain you have selected for your Career Page.

    You can find it by going to **Recruitment - Career Pages**. Once you are on the Career Page, click the gear icon to see which subdomain you have selected. To find the URL you should use, click **View Career Page**.

    ![cp](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/cp.png?width=670&height=387&name=cp.png)

    Then, simply copy the URL from the browser’s address bar. You can use this URL to hyperlink to or embed the Career Page on your website.

    ![url](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/url.png?width=670&height=46&name=url.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="We operate in multiple countries, how should the language settings on our career pages be configured?">
    Each career page you create has its own language setting, which determines the language candidates see when visiting the page. If you recruit candidates in multiple countries, we recommend creating separate career pages for each language. Please note that the language setting **does not** automatically translate your content on your career page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

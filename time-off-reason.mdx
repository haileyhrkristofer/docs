## What is time off reasons?

When your employees are reporting time off, they need to choose a reason for their time off. A time off reasons can for example be Holiday, Medical appointment or Care for child. These reasons you set up will become selectable options when employees are reporting their time off.

## How do you create Time off reasons?

Get started by clicking on **Settings -Time & time off - Add new - Time off reasons**. Name the reason and categorize it to either **Holiday**, **Sick leave** or **Other**.

You can choose whether a time off reason should be able to become a **Long term leave** after a certain amount of days. Read mot about Long-term leave [HERE](https://app-eu1.hubspot.com/knowledge-article/8690880/editor/425606628585/content?redirectUrl=%2Fknowledge%2F8690880%2F188995994644%2Farticles%2Fstate%2Fall).

It is also possible to choose if a time off reason should reduce the employees **Comp time balance**. Read more about Comp time balance [HERE](/time-comp-time-balance).

## Constraints

A time off reason can have **constraints**, which means that you can limit how much employees report time off with this specific time off reason within a period.

- **Limit days with time off during a specific period -** Toggle on if you want to restrict the usage of the time off reson on **days**. **For example** 3 days off within the period of 365 days.
- **Limit time off occasions during a specific period.** **For example** 12 instances of sick leave within the period of 365 days.

## Scope

It is also possible to **Scope** a time off reason. This means that only the employees within the scope can choose this time off reason when reporting time off. When scoping, choose between **Legal entity, Location, Department, Business area, Team** and **Title.**

## Translations

Does your organization have employees with multiple languages? You can add **translations** for each time‑off reason. When an employee uses Hailey in their preferred language, the time‑off reason title will automatically be shown in the users chosen language.

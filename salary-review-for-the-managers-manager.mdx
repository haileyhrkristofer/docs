What is final approval and finalizing a salary review?

Final approval and finalizing are the last steps in a salary review. Final approvers review the completed salary review and approve the salary changes. Once the salary review has received final approval, it must also be finalized before salary changes are registered and become visible on employee profiles.

How do I final approve a salary review?

As the final step in the salary review, **the final approver(s) will approve the entire salary review**. If you are one of these people, you will find the salary review under **My Updates** on the start page. There you will see a task called _Manage salary review_ and “Waiting for final approval” if all salary changes have been approved.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-44-19-8510-PM.png?width=670&height=92&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-44-19-8510-PM.png)

By clicking on the update card, you will be taken to the view for giving **final approval of the entire salary review.** A turquoise checkmark will appear once the proposer and approverhas submitted their proposals. For the salary change to take effect, both the proposer and the approver must be marked with a turquoise checkmark.

Want to see which employees have received adjustments and the amount per employee? Simply scroll to the right in the view!

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-45-00-9962-PM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-45-00-9962-PM.png)

If there are salary proposals that have not been approved by the proposer's manager, a yellow hourglass will appear under the **Status** column. As the final approver, you have the option to approve these salary changes on behalf of the managers. To approve the salary proposals, click **Manage - Approve all unapproved salary changes,** then click Approve in the box that appears.

<Warning>
  If a salary change is not approved by the proposer's manager, the salary will not be automatically updated, so it is **important that salary changes are approved** before the entire salary review is approved.
</Warning>

To approve the whole salary review - simply press Action and then **Approve salary review** in the top right corner. When approving the full salary review, a warning message will appear informing you that **once the review is approved, it will be locked.**

**No new proposals or approvals can be made after all final approvers have given their approval.**

If there are employees in the review with proposed salaries **that haven’t been approved**, or employees **without any proposed salaries**, a warning message will appear. If you proceed by clicking “**Yes, I am sure,”** the salaries will not be updated on the employees' profiles.

How do I finalize a salary review?

Once all final approvers have clicked **Approve salary review**, there is one last step remaining – completing the salary review. One of the final approvers must **Finalize** the entire salary review for the salary changes to be registered on the employee card. This is done under the Actions-button and then **Finalize.**

<Warning>
  **As soon as the review is finalized, the salaries will be visible on the employees' profiles. Regardless of the effective date.**
</Warning>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-50-33-9607-PM.png?width=670&height=160&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-50-33-9607-PM.png)

When finalizing, you can choose whether to send an email notifying employees of their salary changes. If this option is not selected, the salary changes will still be visible to employees on their employee cards once the review is completed.

<Note>
  The salary review will remain under **My updates** until salaries have been updated.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-50-11-0864-PM.png?width=670&height=250&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-50-11-0864-PM.png)

What is required for salary changes to be registered?

<Warning>
  **For the salary changes to be registered, the following conditions must be met:**

  - The immediate manager has submitted the proposals
  - The manager’s manager, or all managers above, have approved the salaries
  - The final approvers have given their approval
  - One of the final approvers has completed the salary review

  If any of these steps are missed, the new salary changes will not be registered. Hailey will also issue a warning during the final approval if this is about to happen.

Key terms

Here you will find brief explanations of terms that may be helpful when giving final approval and finalizing a salary review.

- **Final approver:** An employee responsible for giving final approval to the salary review. Once all final approvers have approved the review, no new salary proposals or approvals can be made.
- **Finalize:** The final step of the salary review process. Finalizing the review registers the approved salary changes and makes them visible on employee profiles.
- **Company target:** The target salary increase set for the company during the salary review. It is displayed as both a percentage and an amount and provides a benchmark against which proposed salary increases can be compared.
- **Proposed**: The proposed salary increase based on the salary changes included in the review. It is displayed as both a percentage and an amount and updates based on the approved proposals included in the review.
- **Status:** Shows whether salary proposals have been submitted and approved. Unapproved proposals are marked with a yellow hourglass, while approved proposals are marked with a turquoise checkmark.
- **Approve all unapproved salary changes:** An action that allows final approvers to approve salary changes that have not yet been approved by a manager. This helps ensure that salary changes are included before the salary review is approved.
- **Approve salary review:** The action used to approve the entire salary review. Once all final approvers have approved the review, no new proposals or approvals can be made.
- **Finalize:** The final step of the salary review process. Finalizing the review registers the approved salary changes on employee profiles.
- **Effective date:** The date on which salary changes are intended to take effect. Salary changes become visible on employee profiles as soon as the salary review is finalized, regardless of the effective date.

FAQ

**Question:** Can I approve salary changes that have not been approved by a manager?

Yes. If salary changes have not been approved by the proposer's manager, you can approve them on behalf of the managers by selecting  **Manage → Approve all unapproved salary changes**. This helps ensure that salary changes are included before the salary review is approved.

**Question**: Can I finalize a salary review before it has been final approved?

No. The salary review must first be approved by all final approvers before it can be finalized. Once all final approvers have approved the review, one final approver can complete the process by selecting **Finalize**.

**Question**: When do salary changes become visible on employee profiles?

Salary changes become visible on employee profiles as soon as the salary review has been finalized. This applies regardless of the effective date configured for the salary review.

**Question**: Can I make changes after the salary review has been approved?

No. Once all final approvers have approved the salary review, the review is locked and no new proposals or approvals can be made.

**Question**: Do employees need to be notified when the salary review is finalized?

No. When finalizing the salary review, you can choose whether or not to send an email notification to employees. Even if no email is sent, salary changes will still be visible on employee profiles once the review has been finalized.

## What is a holiday plan?

A **holiday plan** provides a shared overview of registered time off during a selected period, for example, ahead of the summer or Christmas holidays. Employees included in the same holiday plan can easily see each other's absences without having to access the time overview.

You can create multiple holiday plans for different groups or parts of your organization.

<Note>
  Only the employees included in the same holiday plan can see each other's time off in the overview. You can create separate holiday plans for different groups of employees within the organization.
</Note>

<Tip>
  The holiday plan itself cannot be exported. Instead, use our Time off -report, where you can get an overview and export all registered time off.
</Tip>

## How do you start a holiday plan?

To get started with a holiday plan, begin by selecting the employees you want to include in the plan.

1. Go to the **Employee list**.
2. Select the employees you want to include.
3. Click **Action**.
4. Select **Start holiday plan**.
5. Enter:

- A name for the holiday plan
- The holiday period
- A deadline to indicate when the time off _should_ be registered
- An optional comment

6. Click **Send**.

## How do you use the holiday plan?

When the holiday plan has been created, a card will appear under **My Updates** for both the person who created the plan and the employees included. The card remains visible until the holiday period has ended.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-29-2026-11-53-51-1844-AM.png?width=670&height=207&name=image-png-Jul-29-2026-11-53-51-1844-AM.png)

In the overview, you can see:

- all registered time off during the chosen period
- only that a person is marked as **Absent** (not the specific time off reason or type)

You can also filter by:

- Employees
- Attendance
- Organizational filters

If you have enabled **non-working days**, employees will not be counted as present on those days. Employees with registered working hours on their profile will also not be shown as present on days without scheduled working time.

<Tip>
  Tip! Filters can be saved as views and reused in other holiday plans.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Employees who are on long term leave are not included in the holiday plan.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-29-2026-11-44-19-0662-AM.png?width=670&height=441&name=image-png-Jul-29-2026-11-44-19-0662-AM.png)

<Tip>
  Curious about who else is working during the Christmas or summer holidays? Use the **Attendance**-filter to quickly get an overview! If someone works even one day during the current period, they will appear under the filters Working this week and Working next week.
</Tip>

How do you add time off in the holiday plan?

To register time off during an active holiday plan, **you apply for time off in the same way as usual**.

You can do this from **Add time off** on the homepage **,** via **Manage - Add time off** on your profile, or directly from the active holiday plan by clicking **Add time off** in the top right corner.

**To do this:**

1. Click **Add time off** directly from the holiday plan, from the homepage, or via your profile.
2. Select the period you want to apply for.
3. Specify the reason and extent of the time off:

- If reporting in percentage, enter the extent as a percentage.
- If reporting in hours, you can use the **Full day** option (if working hours are registered in your profile) or enter the number of hours manually.

4. Add a comment if needed.
5. To register multiple periods in the same request, click **Add another time off**.
6. Click **Send to approval** when you are finished.

<Tip>
  Read more about how to add time off [HERE](/time-off-submit). For more information on how to approve time off, click [HERE](/time-off-approve-and-change).
</Tip>

## How do you update the holiday plan?

If you need to **make any changes** to the holiday plan, the person who **started the holiday plan** can go to the overview via the dashbord, click on **Update** on the top right corner, and make the adjustments. What is possible to update is:

- Which employees should be included/excluded in the plan
- Title
- From and To Date
- Deadline
- Comment

To delete the holiday plan, click **Delete**. No time off registered **during that period will disappear**, and it will be available in the time overview in the main menu. Read more about the time overview [HERE](/time-off-approve-and-change#overview).

<Tip>
  Did a new employee start after the holiday plan was created? You can always add employees afterwards by updating the holiday plan!
</Tip>

## Rights

The following right is required from others than admin to start a holiday plan:

- **Time & Time Off - Start holiday plan:** allows the user to create and start holiday plans (only No or Yes).

## Common questions

**Question**: Why can't I start a holiday plan?

To start a holiday plan, you need right to do so.

An admin can give you this right by updating your role under **Settings - Security - Roles & rights**. Under the role's **Company rights** section, enable **Start holiday plan** under **Time & Time Off**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="If I include the entire organization in a holiday plan, will everyone be able to see each other's time-off reason during that period?">
    No. Everyone will be able to see **that someone is absent**, but **not the reason** for their time off. The absence will simply be **marked in purple** on the calendar, without revealing why the person is away.

    **Question**: Can I add or remove employees from an existing holiday plan?

    Yes, the person who created the holiday plan can update which employees are included.

    Open the holiday plan and click **Update** to add or remove employees, update the period, deadline, title, or comment.

    **Question**: Why can't I see an employee in the holiday plan?

    Employees will only appear in a holiday plan if they are included when the plan is created or added later through an update. Keep in mind that employees on long-term leave are not included in the holiday plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I export a holiday plan?">
    No, holiday plans cannot be exported.

    However, users with the appropriate rights (such as Admins or users with access) can export time off data under **Reports - Time off**. Simply select the desired date range and export the data. The report will be downloaded in **CSV format**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

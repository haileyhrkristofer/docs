## How do you apply for a job?

Once you have created and published your **Job Ad**, it is displayed on your selected **Career page**. When applicants visit the **Career page**, they can view all **Open positions** and filter them based on your **Locations** and **Departments**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-12-2026-12-01-38-4882-PM.png?width=670&height=387&name=image-png-Aug-12-2026-12-01-38-4882-PM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-12-2026-12-07-29-9957-PM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-12-2026-12-07-29-9957-PM.png)

When an applicant clicks on an open position, they can review all the information provided in the job ad. After reading the advertisement, the applicant proceeds to the **Application**, where the **Contact person** for the recruitment process is presented. The applicant then completes the requested information and uploads any documents you have chosen to require.

Each job ad includes a link to Hailey's **Privacy Policy**, allowing applicants to learn more about how their personal data is processed. If you have linked your own **Privacy Policy** on your **Career Page**, it will also be available for candidates to read.

Once all required fields have been completed, the applicant clicks **Submit application.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-35-11-1607-AM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-35-11-1607-AM.png)

## What happens after an application has been submitted?

When the applicant clicks **Submit application**, they automatically receive a confirmation email stating that their application has been received and that you look forward to reviewing it. This email is sent in the language selected for your **Career page**.

The application is then added to the **Recruitment** process associated with the job ad. To learn more about managing applicant submissions and the next steps in the recruitment process, please read more in [**THIS**](/candidate-management) guide.

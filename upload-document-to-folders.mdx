In this guide, you'll learn how to upload documents to employee folders in Hailey. Using folders is a way of organize and making it easier to store, find, and manage documents efficiently.

## Why upload documents to folders?

Folders help keep employee documents organized and easy to manage. By storing documents in dedicated folders, you can ensure information is structured, easy to find, and accessible to the right people.

## How do you upload a document to a folder?

To upload a document:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the employee profile">
    Go to the employee profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Docs tab">
    Open the **Docs** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click on the folder where you want to store the document">
    Click on the folder where you want to store the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click inside the upload area or drag and drop one or more fi">
    Click inside the upload area or drag and drop one or more files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The document will be uploaded and stored in the selected fol">
    The document will be uploaded and stored in the selected folder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can upload a single file or up to 10 files at the same time. Supported file formats are PDF, PNG, JPEG, PPT, PPTX, XLS, XLSX, DOC, and DOCX. The maximum file size is 30 MB per file.
</Note>

## How do you manage uploaded documents?

Once uploaded, the document will appear in the selected folder on the employee's Docs tab.

To manage a document, click the **three-dot menu** next to the document. Depending on your access settings, you can either:

- **Download** the document to your device
- **Edit** the document details
- **Delete** the document

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-31-37-7145-AM.png?width=670&height=326&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-31-37-7145-AM.png)

<Note>
  The available actions depend on the permissions configured for the folder. If you cannot edit or delete a document, contact an administrator to verify your access level.
</Note>

When editing, you are allowed to **change the name** of the file and **change which folder** the file should be stored in. When switching to another folder as storage for a file, the disposal rules of the new folder are the ones that will effect the file. This from the date of change.

## Rights and access

Rights to folders is controlled through the **Access** you set up for each folder. Depending on the access settings, employees may be able to **View**, **Upload**, **Edit**, or **Delete** documents.

<Note>
  Folder access is configured in **Settings - Customization**. Read more about it [**HERE.**](/employee-folders)
</Note>

## Common questions

Below you'll find answers to frequently asked questions about uploading and managing documents in folders.

**Question 1:** Why can't I upload a document?

Your access level for the folder may not include the ability to upload documents. Folder permissions are configured by an administrator.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="2: Who can access documents in a folder?">
    Only users who have access to the folder can view or manage documents stored within it. Available actions depend on the permissions assigned to each user role.

    **Question 3:** Can I upload multiple documents at once?

    Yes. You can upload a single file or up to 10 files at the same time.

    **Question 4:** Why can't I see a specific folder?

    Folders are only visible to users who have been granted access through the folder's access settings. Contact an administrator if you believe you should have access.

    **Question 5:** I can't find the **Docs** tab at an employees profile. Where is it?

    If the **Docs** tab isn't visible to you, it's because you don't have the right **Access** to the **Employee folders** on that specific employee (_this also applies to admins_). Once the access settings have been updated you'll be able to find the tab.

    Use [**THIS**](/employee-folders) guide to learn how to manage and update access to employee folders.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

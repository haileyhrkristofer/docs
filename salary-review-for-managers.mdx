What is a salary proposal for a manager?

A salary proposal is a manager's recommendation for an employee's new salary during a salary review. Managers can review the salaries of employees included in the review, make adjustments, add comments, and submit proposals for approval.

The feature provides a clear overview of salary changes and helps ensure that proposals follow the approval process before salaries are finalized.

Getting started with salary proposals

When the salary review has started, you as the manager will receive an email with information that it’s time to propose salary changes for the employees that reports to you and are included in the review.

On your homepage in Hailey, under **My updates** you’ll find the update card _Propose salary changes._

**When you click on the update card, you will be taken to the salary review.** Here, you will be guided by the instructions written for your salary review, along with a brief summary on the following steps of the salary review in Hailey. If you close the instructions, you can easily reopen them by clicking the small information icon **(i)** next to the salary review title.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-26-14-6457-PM.png?width=670&height=299&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-26-14-6457-PM.png)

In the salary review, you will see a list of the employees who report to you, their salaries before and after the salary changes in total, the percentage increase based on the average target, and the proposed salaries as a percentage.

<Note>
  Salaries in currencies other than the reference currency are converted to the reference currency in the salary summary.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-28-24-0206-PM.png?width=670&height=338&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-28-24-0206-PM.png)

<Note>
  Salaries expressed as annual salaries are divided by 12 and included in the calculations for both the increase and the salary summary.

  Hourly wages are **excluded from the calculations** and will therefore not affect either the increase or the total salary amount.
</Note>

Review and submit salary proposals

By **clicking on each employee individually**, you can make salary adjustments. The salary already has a proposed raise based on the target raise, but each salary can be adjusted as needed. **Adjustments can be made in either percentage or amount.**

In the modal that opens when making adjustments, there is also an option to add additional adjustments beyond the review. _Whether this is available depends on whether it has been added to your salary review._

<Warning>
  Please note that **Hailey does not round to whole numbers**. Therefore, keep in mind to round manually if you adjust salaries using a percentage increase.
</Warning>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-29-40-9066-PM.png?width=670&height=585&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-29-40-9066-PM.png)

<Tip>
  The information displayed under **Additional information** depends on how the salary review has been configured. **Current scope percentage** is included by default, while additional employee fields can be added when creating the salary review. In this guide, **Assessed performance level** is shown as an example.
</Tip>

You **can also add comments for the proposed salary change**. These comments will be visible to yourself, your manager (i.e., the manager’s manager), the final approver, and the person who initiated the review.

When/if you receive a reply to your comment, for example from your manager, an email will be sent to you. Under the heading _Latest commen_t, you can see the date when the latest comment was posted, which allows you to see if your comment has been replied to.

If an employee has several different salary types, a plus sign appears in front of the person's name. By clicking on the relevant employee, you can see the salaries that the employee has.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-30-54-6680-PM.png?width=670&height=161&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-30-54-6680-PM.png)

You can **choose to exclude** any salary or salaries that should not be part of the review. Click on the salary and toggle the **Exclude from salary review** button. The excluded salary will then be grayed out and will not affect the salary summary. **If a salary has been excluded by mistake,** simply repeat the process and toggle off the “Exclude from salary review” button.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-31-34-0633-PM.png?width=670&height=678&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-31-34-0633-PM.png)

You don’t need to complete all proposals at once – feel free to work through a few and then click **Save as draft** in the top right corner of the review. The task to suggest changes will remain under **My updates** on the dashboard and your progress will be saved for the next time you return.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-33-31-5922-PM.png?width=670&height=152&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-33-31-5922-PM.png)

Once you’ve reviewed all salaries and are satisfied with your proposals, click **Propose salary changes** in the top right corner.

Your manager will receive an email and find the proposed salary changes under **My Updates** in Hailey. From there, they can review and approve the proposals. If the immediate manager doesn’t report to anyone, they will also be responsible for approving the proposals.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-34-16-6196-PM.png?width=670&height=160&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-34-16-6196-PM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-34-41-9993-PM.png?width=670&height=491&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-34-41-9993-PM.png)

<Note>
  Do you, as the immediate manager, get feedback to revise the proposals?

  Under **My Updates**, the update card for _Propose salary changes_ **will remain until the salary review process is completed.** The task stays available in case you need to make any corrections to the salary proposals.
</Note>

Key Terms

Here you will find brief explanations of terms that may be helpful when proposing salary changes as a manager.

- **Average target:** The average salary increase target for the employees included in your salary review. It is displayed as both a percentage and an amount, allowing you to compare your proposals against the review target.
- **Proposed**: The average salary increase based on the salary proposals you have entered. It is displayed as both a percentage and an amount and updates as you make changes to employee salaries.
- **Additional adjustment:** An additional salary adjustment that can be added alongside the salary review proposal. Whether this option is available depends on how the salary review has been configured.
- **Exclude from salary review:** An option used to remove a salary from the salary review. Excluded salaries are not included in the salary calculations or salary summary and can be included again if needed.
- **Save as draft:** An option that allows managers to save their progress without submitting salary proposals. Saved drafts can be revisited and updated later before the proposals are submitted.
- **Propose salary changes:** The action used to submit salary proposals for employees included in the salary review. Once submitted, the proposals are sent to the next approver for review and approval.

FAQ

**Question:** How do I exclude a salary from a salary review?

A salary can be excluded from the salary review without removing the employee from the review. To exclude a salary, open the employee's salary details and enable **Exclude from salary review** for the relevant salary. The salary will then be greyed out and will no longer be included in the salary calculations or salary summary. If a salary has been excluded by mistake, simply disable **Exclude from salary review** to include it again.

**Question**: What happens if an employee changes manager during an ongoing salary review?

The salary review is based on the manager specified in the employee's **Reports to** field. If the **Reports to** field is updated during an ongoing salary review, the proposing manager in the salary review will also be updated.

**Question**: Can a substitute manager submit salary proposals during a salary review?

The salary review is based on the manager specified in the employee's **Reports to** field. A substitute manager does not automatically take over the role of the proposing manager. If the regular manager is still specified in **Reports to**, the responsibility for submitting salary proposals remains with that person.

**Question**: Why do I see multiple update cards for the same salary review under My Updates?

You may see multiple update cards for the same salary review under **My Updates** if you have more than one role in the salary review process. For example, you may be responsible for proposing salary changes for employees who report to you while also approving salary proposals submitted by managers who report to you. **As a result, multiple update cards may be displayed for the same salary review.**

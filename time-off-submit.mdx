## What is time off?

The **Time off** feature in Hailey lets you register time off, such as vacation or other leave.

Once you submit a request, **it is automatically sent to the approver**. You can also follow the status of your requests directly in Hailey.

<Tip>
  To learn how to approve and change time off, click [HERE](/time-off-approve-and-change).
</Tip>

## Before you get started

Before you can register time off, make sure that:

- You have **reporting policy** in your employment.
- The relevant **time off reasons** have been set up by your administrator.

Read more about how to assign a reporting policy for employees [HERE](/update-time-and-time-off-settings-on-employee).

How do you add time off?

You can register time off in Hailey for today, a future date, or a date that has already passed. Once you submit your request, it is automatically sent an approver.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your Dashboard and click Add time off">
    Go to your **Dashboard** and click **Add time off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the date range for your time off">
    Select the **date range** for your time off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the reason for your absence">
    Choose the **reason** for your absence.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Specify the scope of your time off">
    Specify the **scope** of your time off. - You can report time off as either a **percentage** or in **hours**. - If you report your time off in hours and have working hours configured in your profile, you can enable **All day?** to register a full day automatically. Disable **All day?** if you want to enter a specific number of hours instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a comment if you want to provide additional information">
    Add a **comment** if you want to provide additional information (optional).
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you need to register multiple periods of time off in the">
    If you need to register multiple periods of time off in the same request, click **Add another time off**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send to approver to submit your request">
    Click **Send to approver** to submit your request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Time off cannot be submitted on a non-working day.

  If time off is submitted for **a period that includes non-working days**, no time off will be registered on the non-working days.
</Note>

## How do you change or delete your time off?

If your time off request is still **pending approval**, you can update or remove it if needed.

If you notice a mistake, such as selecting the wrong reason, you can **simply delete the request** and submit a new, corrected one.

To delete a submitted request:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to your employee card and open the Time tab">
    Go to your employee card and open the **Time** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the trash can icon in the top right corner">
    Click the **trash** **can icon** in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the days you want to remove and confirm">
    Select the days you want to remove and **confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once deleted, you can create a new time off request with the correct details.

<Note>
  Time off that have already been **approved** need to be **removed by your admin or manager with the required rights.** Read more [HERE](/time-off-approve-and-change).
</Note>

## How do you follow your time off?

After you’ve submitted a time off request, you can easily follow its status from your dashboard.

Any request that is still relevant (upcoming or awaiting approval) will appear in an update card under **My updates**. There, you can see whether your request has been approved or not.

To view a full overview of your time off, including past entries, go to the **Time** tab on your employee card.

<Note>
  You will receive an **email** when your submitted time off request has been approved or rejected.
</Note>

## Rights

The following rights are useful when adding time off:

- **Time & time off - View:** gives access to the **Time** tab on the employee profile
- **Time & time off - View & edit:** gives the right to add automatically approved time off delete approved time off.
- **Time & time off - Edit approved time cycle:** gives the right to add or delete time off within an approved month.

If you want to read more about how Roles & rights work in Hailey, click [HERE](/roles-and-rights)!

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I want to add time off, but I can't find the Add time off -button. What should I do?">
    If the **Add time off** button is missing, it's most likely because there is no **reporting policy** connected to your employment.

    To enable time reporting, admin or someone with the right can update your **Time & time off settings** and assign the correct reporting policy.

    Read more about how to update an employee's time & time off settings [HERE](/update-time-and-time-off-settings-on-employee).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I see my approved time off?">
    You can view your approved time off in the **Time** tab of your profile. Your registered time off will be displayed in the calendar.

    Upcoming time off requests will also appear as an update card under **My updates**. There, you can see whether your request has been approved or is still pending.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my time off request still pending?">
    Your time off request remains pending until it has been reviewed and approved by your assigned **Time & time off approver**.

    If you are unsure who your approver is, you can check with your manager or administrator.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I request time off on certain days?">
    Some days may be unavailable due to company settings, closed periods, or restrictions related to the time off reason.

    If a day is **gray** in your calendar, it is a non-working day. Time off cannot be registered on a non-working day.

    Are you seeing a message when you try to submit a time off request? If so, you may have reached the maximum number of days you can take using that time-off reason.

    If you believe something is incorrect, contact your Hailey admin.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I add or delete time off for in a previous month?">
    If you're using **time cycles**, the month may already have been approved. Once a time cycle has been approved, only users with the **Edit approved time cycle** right can make changes to that month's time off. Read more about time cycles [**HERE**](/time-cycles).

    Even if you're **not** using time cycles but your time off has been **approved**, you need specific rights to be able to delete approved time off.

    If you're **not** using time cycles you should be able to add time off for a previous month.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add time off for multiple employees at once?">
    No, it is not possible to add time off for multiple employees at the same time.

    To add time off for an employee, go to their **employee profile** and click **Actions - Add time off**. You can also add time off directly from the **Time overview** by clicking a day in the employee's row. You can register either a single day or a longer period.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

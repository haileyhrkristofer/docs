---
title: "Sign in with Google SSO"
---

With Google Single Sign-On, you can sign in to Hailey using the same credentials you use for your work email and other Google sevices.

## Enable Google SSO

If you'd like employees to sign in with their Google accounts, Google SSO must first be enabled in Hailey. Go to **Settings** - **Security** - **Log in**, open the Google card, and activate the sign-in method by turning on the **Active** toggle.

<Note>
  Multiple sign-in methods can be enabled at the same time. Before **disabling your current sign-in method**, we strongly recommend testing the new option first. This helps prevent accidentally locking yourself out of the system.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-03-2026-01-15-48-9626-PM.png?width=670&height=346&name=image-png-Jun-03-2026-01-15-48-9626-PM.png)

## How to sign in with Google

Once Google SSO has been enabled, users can sign in to Hailey by clicking the **Google** button on the login page.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-17-2025-11-22-10-7448-AM.png?width=670&height=357&name=image-png-Jul-17-2025-11-22-10-7448-AM.png)

The first time a user signs in with Google, they will be asked to authenticate using their Google account.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-05-2024-06-43-11-2794-AM.png?width=666&height=355&name=image-png-Mar-05-2024-06-43-11-2794-AM.png)

If your organization requires **two-factor authentication,** you'll also need to complete an additional verification step. The exact process may vary depending on your organization's security settings.

After the initial setup, signing in is simple. Users can access Hailey by clicking the Google button on the login page and following the standard Google sign-in process.

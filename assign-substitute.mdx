## What is Assign substitute?

Assign Substitute allows you to temporarily assign a substitute for an employee who is absent. Using this feature, the substitute will temporarily inherit the rights and approval principles of the employee they are covering for.

## Before you get started

To temporarily transfer rights to the substitute employee, you can use **Assign Substitute** via the **Actions** button on the employee card.

You can also **Add an** **employment** to preserve historical records and visualize the substitute employment after the substitute period has ended. When adding a substitute employment, a **Substitute reason** must be specified. These reasons need to be configured before a substitute employment can be added for an employee.

You can read more about how to create substitute reasons [**HERE.**](/template-new-article-7-0-0)

## How to assign a substitute?

To Assign a substitute, follow these steps:

- Go to the employee card **of the employee who will be absent.**
- Click **Actions**
- Select **Assign substitute**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-07-57-54-4024-AM.png)

Then select **Who should be the substitute** and specify the **From** **and To dates** of the substitute period. Once you are satisfied, click Save.

<Note>
  By assigning a substitute, the absent employee’s **Roles & rights** and **Approval principles** are transferred to the substitute employee for the duration of the substitution period.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-08-05-05-2937-AM.png)

Once the selections have been saved, this will be clearly indicated on both the absent employee's and the substitute's employee cards. This is shown with **a box under each profile picture**, where the start and end dates are also displayed. All employees can see the box in the employee profiles.

<Note>
  For the employee acting as the substitute, this will only be visible on their profile **once the substitution period begins.**
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-08-02-02-2335-AM.png)

Need to **edit or remove the substitution?** Go to the absent employee's profile, click on the box under their profile picture, and make the necessary changes.

<Note>
  If needed, one employee can have multiple substitutes. This is achieved by repeating the above process.
</Note>

<Note>
  When a substitution period ends, **the rights will automatically revert to the employee who was absent**. The box under the profile pictures will disappear after the last day of the substitution.
</Note>

<Note>
  If you need to **change the substitute,** the current substitution must first be removed. Then, add the new substitute as described above.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hubfs/image-png-Nov-18-2025-08-00-21-1049-AM.png)

## How to add a substitute employment?

**Add a new employment** to preserve historical records and visualize the substitution after the substitution period has ended.

Follow the instructions [**HERE**](/employment-wizard) to add employment.

To create a substitute employment, you must fill in certain fields under **Employment information** in the wizard:

- Under **Employment type,** select **Fixed term**. Then select **Temporary substitute** as the **Fixed term type.**
- Enter an end date for the fixed term.
- Select **who the employee is substituting for** and a **Substitute reason.**

After filling in the employment information, continue through the wizard as usual.

## Rights

As an admin in Hailey, you can always assign a substitute. You can give the right to assign substitutes to other roles in **Roles and rights.**

- **Assign substitute through the action button**: Actions - Assign substitute - Yes.

<Note>
  [**HERE**](/roles-and-rights) you can read more about **Roles & rights**
</Note>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I see the the box with information about the substitution in the employee's profile?">
    No worries. For the employee acting as the substitute, the box **only becomes visible** **once the substitution period starts.** On the absent employee’s profile, the box is displayed immediately after the substitution has been assigned.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I select a substitute reason when adding a new employment for an employee?">
    This is likely because no substitution reasons have been configured yet. To select a reason from the dropdown list, the reasons must first be created in Hailey.

    Create substitute reasons by following the instructions [**HERE.**](/template-new-article-7-0-0)
  </Accordion>

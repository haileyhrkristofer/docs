In this guide, you'll find an overview of the features available in the Hailey mobile app and learn how employees and managers can use the app to stay connected and complete tasks on the go.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Picture%201-png-1.png?width=670&height=358&name=Picture%201-png-1.png)

## Log in

You can log in to Hailey using the SSO options utilised by your company or with your email credentials sent to you in your email invitation to Hailey.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-11-2025-12-39-53-9696-PM.png?width=670&height=311&name=image-png-Jun-11-2025-12-39-53-9696-PM.png)

## Home

When you log into Hailey, you will always be taken to the **Home** tab. Here you will find any **news** posted by your company, as well as **who's away** in your department or team in the current and upcoming week. From here, you are also able to do the **face quiz**, and **report time** or **time off** by pressing the **\+** in the top right corner.

If you are a part of a holiday plan, this will be shown on this page as well.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-27-2025-10-51-47-8483-AM.png?width=230&height=471&name=image-png-Oct-27-2025-10-51-47-8483-AM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-27-2025-10-51-52-6517-AM.png?width=230&height=471&name=image-png-Oct-27-2025-10-51-52-6517-AM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-27-2025-10-52-08-8572-AM.png?width=230&height=471&name=image-png-Oct-27-2025-10-52-08-8572-AM.png)

Above the **\+** sign you can access the **Tasks**-tab. Here, you will get an overview of the tasks assigned to you in Hailey. Some tasks can be **marked as complete** in the app, while other tasks need to be completed by logging into haileyhr.com in a web browser instead of the app.

<Note>
  The tasks that can be completed in the app (if you have the rights to do so) include:

  - Tasks in workflows
  - Signing documents
  - Approving time off
  - Add, remove, and verify certifications
  - Add and delete goals
  - Answering questions before a one-to-one
  - Answer questions for 360° reviews
  - Answer surveys and pulse surveys
  - Sign off feedback
  - Submit and approve time cycle
  - Approve or deny new salary, salary change, end salary
</Note>

## News

From the home page, you can view the latest published news. Click on any news item to open and read it. If you want to explore more articles, select **Show all** to browse through previously published news. In the full news list, you can also use the search field to quickly find specific articles.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-03-2026-10-17-55-6724-AM.png?width=670&height=457&name=image-png-Mar-03-2026-10-17-55-6724-AM.png)

You can also react to a post. By clicking **Like**, you give the post a thumbs up. If you press and hold the thumbs‑up icon, additional reaction options will appear for you to choose from.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-03-2026-10-22-34-8763-AM.png?width=365&height=754&name=image-png-Mar-03-2026-10-22-34-8763-AM.png)

## Time

If you have the right to see time in Hailey, you will find the **Time** tab at the bottom of the dashboard. Here, you will get a seamless **overview of your time** and/or **time** **off** as well as **non-working days**.

In the list below the calendar, you can see your scheduled time, time entries, time-off events, and non-working days logged for each specific day. If you’ve reported time off, you’ll also see whether it has been **approved** or is still **waiting for approval**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-28-2025-07-20-08-3607-AM.png?width=585&height=606&name=image-png-Oct-28-2025-07-20-08-3607-AM.png)

<Note>
  You can add and delete time off from the three dots in the top right corner in the **Time** tab.
</Note>

## Report time

You can report time by going to the home page. Then click the **plus icon in the top-right corner** and select **report time**. Next, choose the date or dates you want to report time for and enter the number of hours worked. Then press **submit**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-04-2026-12-18-40-0414-PM.png?width=670&height=460&name=image-png-Mar-04-2026-12-18-40-0414-PM.png)

## Clock in and Clock out

To clock in, click the plus sign (+) on your **Dashboard,** then select **Clock In.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-30-59-9339-AM.png?width=256&height=524&name=image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-30-59-9339-AM.png)

Once you’re clocked in, you’ll have the option to either **Clock Out** or **Take a Break.** If you choose to take a break, a timer will start automatically. To resume your shift, click **End Break.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-31-59-5101-AM.png?width=278&height=570&name=image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-31-59-5101-AM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-32-43-4226-AM.png?width=278&height=568&name=image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-32-43-4226-AM.png)

When your shift is complete, click **Clock Out.** A summary of your shift will then be displayed. If anything needs to be corrected, for example if you forgot to end your break, you can adjust the reported time by clicking **Adjust**, making the necessary changes, and then clicking **Save.** If everything looks correct, simply click **Close.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-59-49-6864-AM.png?width=278&height=569&name=image-png-Oct-23-2025-11-59-49-6864-AM.png)

## Add time off

You can add time off by clicking the **plus icon in the top-right corner** of the home page and selecting **Add time off**. Then fill in the details of your absence, including the dates and the reason.

## View colleagues’ time off

If you have permission to view time off for other employees, you can do so through their profile. Go to Colleagues in the main menu and click the employee whose time off you want to view. Once you are on the employee’s profile, click Time off. There, you can see time off for the upcoming year as well as all time off.

## Approve time off

To approve time off, go to your home page and select **Tasks**. In this view, you will see a task called **Approve time off**. Click the task and then choose **Approve** to complete the approval.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-04-2026-10-10-25-9107-AM.png?width=670&height=462&name=image-png-Mar-04-2026-10-10-25-9107-AM.png)

## Company

At the bottom, you will also find the **Company**-tab, which puts your entire organisation at your fingertips. You are able to filter on **my departments**, **my teams** and, if you are a manger, **reporting to me**. By using the search bar, you can search for any employee in your company. If you have employees reporting to you, you will also be able to view your manager's agenda here.

From here, you can enter a colleagues profile and find all the information about them that you have the rights to see. For instance, you can see **who they report to** and if they have **anyone reporting to them**.

By pressing the button **Contact me** you will be able to **contact your colleagues directly from the app.** If you click on **Show in org**, you can see who the employee reports to and who reports to them.

<Note>
  The amount of information you see on a colleagues profile depends on the **roles and rights** you have in Hailey. As a manager, you might be able to see more information about the employees who report to you than other employees.
</Note>

Through this tab, you can also access the **Company corner**, where you can find company-specific information posted here.

## Profile

By pressing the **Profile**-tab at the bottom of the screen, you are led to your employee card in Hailey. Here, you can view all the **information about yourself and your employment**.

**Your company admin configures what you can see on your profile.** But this could include information about your current and previous employments at your company, change your profile picture, your personal information, compensation and salary information, documents such as your contract, upcoming or previous feedback sessions and a timeline of events and happenings during your time at your company.

Here is a list of the information you can find in this tab, if you have the rights to do so:

- Under **Job**, you will find information relating to your current employment. You will also be able to find information about your previous employments, if you have had any within your organisation. If you have the rights to do so, you can edit the informations in sections General and Other in the app.
- Under **Personal**, you will find all your personal data including your sensitive information, personal contact information and emergency contact in this section. If you have the rights to do so, you can edit the information in sections General, Sensitive Information, Contact information and Emergency contact.
- Under **Compensation**, all information related to your salary is shown, including bank details, benefits and other terms relating to your compensation. By clicking on your salary card, you can see your salary history. If you have rights to do so, you can edit the information in sections General, Bank details and Benefits in the app.
- Under **Documents**, you can find your documents, such as your contract and other files shared with you. If your company uses folders, these may be organized into different folders. You can open and download documents if needed, or upload a new document by clicking the plus icon in the top-right corner.
- Under **Timeline**, you can view events that have occurred during your employment.
- Under **Feedback**, you can access your one-to-one sessions and 360 **°** s. Here you can see the title, host, date and status of each specific session. By clicking one of the sessions, you can see the notes and answers from the specific session.
- Under **Educations,** you can see any educations that you have participated in that are registered in Hailey.
- Under **Goals,** you can view, add, and delete the goals created for you in Hailey, depending on your access rights.
- Under **Competences**, you can see any competences that have been given to you in Hailey.

## Competences

Go to the employee’s profile and select **Competences**.

- Under **Auto assigned** tab, you will see the competences that have been automatically assigned to the employee based on their organisational belonging, such as Location, Business area, Department, Team or Title.
- Under the **Additional** tab, you’ll see competences that have been added directly to the employee’s profile and are not linked to their organisational belonging.

Click on the **plus sign (+)** to add an **additional competence**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2026-10-07-10-8902-AM.png?width=670&height=463&name=image-png-Sep-24-2026-10-07-10-8902-AM.png)

The competences added to the employee’s profile can be rated. **Click on the competence** to view more information about it under **Description**, or to rate it under **Rating**. Select a rating and add a comment if you want. Then click **Submit Rating**. Previous ratings and comments are displayed under **History**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2026-10-06-48-7668-AM.png?width=670&height=470&name=image-png-Sep-24-2026-10-06-48-7668-AM.png)

You can remove a competence directly in the mobile app. Start by opening the employee profile. Then go to **Competences** and click on the competence you wish to remove from the employee. In the top-right corner, there are three dots – tap on them and select **Remove competence**. Confirm your choice by tapping Remove competence again.

You can also remove a historical rating in the mobile app. Tap the **competence** you wish to edit, then tap History. In the top right corner of the historical rating, there are three dots – tap them and select **Remove rating**. Then confirm your choice by tapping Remove rating again.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Sep-24-2026-10-05-55-4960-AM.png?width=550&height=568&name=image-png-Sep-24-2026-10-05-55-4960-AM.png)

## Certifications

Go to the employee’s profile and select **Certifications.** You can click on a certificate to view more information.

- Required tab—Certificates required for the employee based on organizational belonging.
- Added tab—The employee's other added certificates that are not required.

Click on the **plus icon (+)** to add a certification.

Fill in the **Issued date** and **Expiration date**. You can add a file or a URL under **Proof of certification**, and write a **comment** if needed. Finish by clicking **Send for verification**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-09-24%20kl-%2009-05-04-png.png?width=670&height=455&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-09-24%20kl-%2009-05-04-png.png)

When you click Send for verification, you will see who will be assigned the task of verifying the certificate. Who verifies the certificate is determined by your approval principles. Click [**HERE**](/approval-principles) to learn how to set it up.

The verifier will then receive a task under **Tasks** to verify the certificate. Once verified, the certificate will be marked as **Valid** in the employee’s profile.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-09-24%20kl-%2009-06-10-png-1.png?width=670&height=459&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-09-24%20kl-%2009-06-10-png-1.png)

You can also remove certificates directly in the mobile app. Start by opening the employee card for the person whose certificate you want to delete. Then go to the **Certifications** tab and select the certificate you wish to remove. In the top right corner, tap the three dots and choose **Delete certificate**. Confirm the deletion by tapping **Yes, I’m sure.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-09-24%20kl-%2009-06-26-png.png?width=550&height=562&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-09-24%20kl-%2009-06-26-png.png)

## Manager notes

To add manager notes in the mobile app, start by opening the employee card for the person you want to write a note about. Tap **Manager notes** to access the correct tab. To create a new note, tap the plus icon in the top right corner. Write your note in the text field, and when you're done, tap the checkmark in the top right corner to save. Your note is now saved and will appear under the **Manager notes** tab.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-28-2025-07-59-09-2304-AM.png?width=670&height=428&name=image-png-Oct-28-2025-07-59-09-2304-AM.png)

## Update employee fields

You can update employee fields directly in the mobile app, provided that you have the rights required to update employee fields. Read more about roles and rights [**here**](/roles-and-rights). Start by tapping **Profile**. Then select the **category** you want to update. Tap the **Pencil** icon where you want to edit the fields.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-23-2025-10-01-36-3314-AM.png?width=670&height=472&name=image-png-Dec-23-2025-10-01-36-3314-AM.png)

Fill in the information you want to update and tap **Save**. Your fields are now updated.

## Complete tasks

You can easily manage tasks directly in the mobile app. Go to **Home - Tasks** to see tasks assigned to you. Open a task to complete it or to view what needs to be done. If the task needs to be completed outside of Hailey, you can click **Mark as done** once it’s finished, and it will be removed from your task list.

## Answer questions before one-to-one

You can answer questions ahead of a one-to-one meeting in Hailey. You’ll find your questions under **Home - Tasks**. Open the task **Answer questions before one-to-one** and fill in your responses. Click **Save** when you’re done. You can return and update your answers until the meeting starts.

## Report HSE deviations

Reporting HSE incidents is a simple way to contribute to a safer workplace. Safety happens when people report what they see and your organization actually listens. Read more about HSE incidents and how to report them [here.](/template-new-article-4)

## Settings

By pressing the three dots in the top right corner on your profile, you will access settings. From here, you can choose the **language** in the app and what theme you want. You can choose between **light** and **dark mode** or **system** which will use the theme used in your phones system settings.

Furthermore, you can access our **F.A.Q.** or **change your password**.

## What are workflows?

Workflows in Hailey are automated processes that help manage HR tasks and activities. They consist of steps and tasks that can be assigned to employees, managers or teams. Workflows can be triggered manually, by specific and recurring dates or time-off events. They help automate processes such as onboarding, offboarding, and reminders, ensuring tasks are completed at the right time. Workflows can also use custom fields and scoping to make sure the right tasks reach the right people.

There are four types of **triggers for workflows**:

- Date-related
- Manual
- Recurring
- Time off

The **automated and recurring workflows start automatically** based on the time setting you have chosen. Time off workflows start based on the trigger you have chosen. The **manual ones are started only via the employee list**.

## Start workflows manually

When you want to manually start a workflow, go to the **Employe list** in the main menu. Select the employee(s) for whom you wish to start a workflow. Click on **Action - Run workflow**.

If you want to run a workflow for a single employee you can go to the employees profile. Click **Action - Run workflow** and select the workflow you wish to start.

<Warning>
  Haven't created any **Workflows** yet? Use [THIS](/create-workflows) guide to get started!
</Warning>

## Manage and claim tasks

Do you have many tasks on your dashboard? Click on **View all tasks** in order to get a full list of all your tasks.

The view **groups different types of tasks**, allows you to search for specific tasks, or select those that have passed their deadline. Additionally, you can **sort by**:

- Newest
- Oldest
- Most urgent
- Least urgent

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-14-20-3968-AM.png?width=650&height=331&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-14-20-3968-AM.png)

Click on a task to get a more detailed view. Here, you can see more detailed information about the task.

To mark a task as completed, click on **Mark as done** in the corner. Some tasks will also disappear by completing the action required, such as signing a contract or proposing new salaries in a salary review.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-15-50-0607-AM.png?width=650&height=332&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-15-50-0607-AM.png)

### Claim tasks

Do you want to take ownership of a task that is assigned to your team or taskforce? When a task is not assigned to a specific person, anyone in the team or task force can claim it.

To claim a task, open the task from the **My tasks** on your dashboard to read the details about the task - click on **Claim task.** Once this is done, members of the taskforce or team will be able to see that you have claimed the task when they open it from their dashboard.

Claiming a task does not lock it. Other team members can still claim the task or mark it as done if needed.

## Reassign and complete other's tasks

Do you want to track the progress of specific tasks or tasks of specific employees? Go to **HR Hub - Tasks.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-16-25-3302-AM.png?width=650&height=334&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-16-25-3302-AM.png)

Here you get **an overview of all active tasks**, including those not part of a workflow. In the left column, you have the option to filter tasks based on a range of predefined options.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-17-01-2355-AM.png?width=650&height=334&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-17-01-2355-AM.png)

People with extended rights can also **complete** **tasks**, that originate from your custom workflows, **for others from here**. Click on the selected task and mark it as done.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-20-2026-11-27-43-6901-AM.png?width=650&height=360&name=image-png-Aug-20-2026-11-27-43-6901-AM.png)

Tasks that originate from workflows can also be reassigned. This is done by clicking a task and then click on the **pencil-icon** in the top right corner. Once the task has been reassigned, an automatic email notification will be sent to the employee who has been assigned the task. To do this you need the related rights, read more about that [H4ERE](#Rights).

<Note>
  This is useful when a specific employee who has been assigned a task is absent, but we still want to ensure that the task is completed.
</Note>

## Follow and manage workflows

You can also track entire workflows via the **HR-hub - Workflows.** Here, **an overview** of your workflows is displayed. The overview shows who the workflow is running for and when it started. In the left menu, you can filter based on a range of predefined options.

**Click on a workflow to get a more detailed view**. Light grey tasks are either completed or in upcoming steps. Active tasks appear sharper.

The Deadline column shows the status of the assigned person in relation to the set timeframe. The Completed column indicates whether the task is marked as done. Hover over a completed task to see when it was completed and by whom.

<Note>
  Reminders are automatically sent to employees when a task passes its deadline. If you want to send an additional reminder, this can be done through your internal channels.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-14-2026-09-38-20-9187-AM.png?width=650&height=333&name=image-png-Aug-14-2026-09-38-20-9187-AM.png)

When **all tasks in a workflow are completed**, the person who started the workflow receives an email. The workflow is then marked as **Done**.

<Note>
  When a workflow starts automatically, no email about it being completed will be sent.
</Note>

Need to delete a workflow that is running? Click on the three dots to the right of it and select **Delete**. The workflow and all its tasks will then disappear.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-14-2026-09-40-03-5665-AM.png?width=650&height=333&name=image-png-Aug-14-2026-09-40-03-5665-AM.png)

## Rights

There are various rights related to workflows in Hailey. All rights are configured via Settings - Security - Roles & Rights. Below, we outline the rights that affect workflows:

- **Start Workflows**
- **Follow Workflows**
- **View and Complete Tasks for Others**

<Note>
  Would you like to learn more about **Roles and rights** in Hailey? Click [HERE](/roles-and-rights)
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I start an automated workflow manually?">
    **Answer:** Yes, automated workflows can also be started manually via the employee list or employees profile. If this is done, the workflow will not start automatically for the same person again. Hailey understands that the workflow has already started and helps you avoid duplicates.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I complete all tasks in the HR-Hub?">
    **Answer:** Tasks originating from the workflows you have built in Hailey can only be completed by the assigned person who should complete the actual task in Hailey. This may include tasks such as answering questions before one-to-ones or approving time off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know I have received a task?">
    **Answer:** When an employee receives a task via a workflow, they are **notified by email**. Tasks that start simultaneously will come in the same email. When a new step with new tasks starts, a new email will be sent.

    The tasks also appear under the employee's **My tasks** on the Hailey dashboard. Via the email, you can click on HAILEY to be taken directly to Hailey and see your new task.

    The email can look like this:

    ![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-17-2025-09-53-39-0145-AM.png?width=467&height=261&name=image-png-Oct-17-2025-09-53-39-0145-AM.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can I not see completed workflows in the HR-Hub?">
    **Answer:** When you open the **Workflow** tab in the HR-Hub, Hailey will apply the filter **Running** as the default when you view this tab. If you want to see completed workflows, you can remove the default filter
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When will the workflow start if I have an automatic trigger for my workflow?">
    **Answer:** The automated and recurring workflows start automatically based on the time setting you have chosen. Time off workflows start based on the trigger you have chosen. There can be, up to, an 8-hour delay if registered on the same day.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

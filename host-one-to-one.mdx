## What is a one-to-one?

A one-to-one is a structured conversation designed to create a recurring dialogue about well-being, performance, development and goals. It enables increased transparency, strengthens the relationship between employee, manager, and team lead. It also gives both parties in the conversation space to raise questions or needs that might otherwise get lost in daily routines.

By using Hailey’s one-to-one functionality you receive support in keeping conversations consistent, well-prepared, and documented. It becomes easy to follow up on what has been discussed previously, see the status of planned meetings, and create a shared understanding of next steps.

## Before you get started

Before you get started make sure you have set up your one-to-one templates. You can read more how to set them up **[HERE](/using-feedback-sessions)**.

## How to host a one-to-one

You can either host a spontaneous one-to-one or a prepared one-to-one.

If you want to hold a **spontaneous one-to-one** without preparing questions, go directly to the employee’s profile, press **Action** - **Hold or prepare one-to-one** - **Hold one-to-one now**.

Give your one-to-one a title, set a date, choose a one-to-one type and choose which feedback template you want to use. Using a template is not mandatory, you can take all notes during the one-to-one in the **notes** field. From here you can also view a previous one-to-one by pressing on **View previous One-to-one**.

![](/images/host-one-to-one/01.png)

When you want to **prepare a one-to-one**, meaning send out questions beforehand, you can either send your one-to-ones in bulk or send a one-to-one to a specific employee.

- If you want to send out one-to-one by a bulk update, you navigate to the **employee list** and **select** those for whom you want to prepare a one-to-one. Press on **Action** and select **Prepare a one-to-one.**
- If you only want to send a one-to-one to a specific employee, go to the **employee’s profile**, press **Action** - **Hold or prepare one-to-one** - **Prepare one-to-one**

Give the one-to-one session a name, select the colleague who is hosting the session, select the one-to-one template and choose a deadline for when you want the employees to have answered their preparing questions. You can also choose to preview the template before sending it to the employees. Then press **Start**.

![](/images/host-one-to-one/02.png)

The employee and/or the colleague will now get an email and a task where they can answer the preparing questions.

You can answer the questions and **save as a draft** to continue at a later time, or **submit your response**. When you submit your response, the task will disappear from the dashboard. If you forget to click **Save as draft**, the latest text you wrote will still be saved.

<Note>
  Please note that once the conversation is started neither the employee or the colleague will be able to answer or further edit any preparing questions.
</Note>

When it’s time to hold the prepared one-to-one in Hailey, navigate to the employee’s profile and press on the **Feedback** tab. Press on the three dots to the right of the one-to-one and select **Start**.

![](/images/host-one-to-one/03.png)

A side panel now opens showing the answers to the preparing questions, along with the option to make additional notes. If you want to view answers from a previous one-to-one, you can click **Show previous one-to-one** and review responses from earlier conversations side by side. It is also possible to filter on one-to-one types to easier sort out which previous one-to-one you want to view.

![](/images/host-one-to-one/04.png)

Click **Save** when your one-to-one is finished. To go back and edit, click the three dots on the right and select **Edit**.

In order to finalize a one-to-one you can either do that by requesting sign-off or by finalizing immediately.

![](/images/host-one-to-one/05.png)

When a sign-off is requested, both the colleague and the employee will get a task containing the meeting notes and the possibility to approve the meeting notes. Once the one-to-one has been approved by both parties, it can no longer be edited.

By immediately finalizing a one-to-one you go past the step of requesting sign-off. The one-to-one is approved immediately.

## One-to-one overview

In the main menu under **Performance** you will find an overview of the one-to-one sessions in Hailey. Here you can keep track of your sessions and whether or not there is something you need to take action on.

![](/images/host-one-to-one/06.png)

The overview shows you the employee the one-to-one regards, the title of the feedback session, one-to-one types, the status, meeting date and the hosting colleague. It is also possible to filter the list to easily find e.g. a specific employee, status or colleague.

If you want to update your one-to-one/s to a different one-to-one type, you can select the relevant one-to-ones, click on **Action** in the top-right corner, and then choose **Edit type.** Select the type you want to assign the conversation to, then click **Save.**

![](/images/host-one-to-one/07.png)

## Key terms

Here you will find explanations of concepts and features that may be useful to have a little extra insight into:

- **Colleague:** The person leading the one-to-one.
- **Employee:** The person the one-to-one is about and held with.
- **Preparing questions:** Questions that are sent out before the one-to-one is held.

## Rights

If for example a manager, employee, team lead, or you as an admin are going to have the right to start and host a one-to-one you must have the necessary rights to access this type of conversation. You can read more about how you set this up **[HERE](/using-feedback-sessions)**.

Here we list the permissions that may be relevant for you to enable if you want to work with one-to-ones in Hailey.

- **View** gives the right to view one-to-ones and answer preparing questions.
- **Manage** gives the above right and also to start, edit, request approval and finalize one-to-ones.
- **Full** **access** gives all of the above and also the right to delete one-to-ones.
- **Edit one-to-one types** gives the right to change the one-to-one type for already held sessions.

## Common questions

  <Accordion title="I'm not seeing any templates when I try to send out the preparing questions. How come?">
  The rights for one-to-ones are now managed from **Settings - Feedback** under one-to-one types. Make sure that you at least have the right **Manage** either through being an employee, teamlead, manager or a manager above, or by being added through a task force.

  If you don't have the right to edit the rights for one-to-one types contact the person who is a Hailey admin in your system.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="As a manager, can I view previous sessions that the former manager has had with employees?">
  If the rights are unchanged compared to when the previous manager worked, you will be able to see already held one-to-ones. If you don't see any previous one-to-ones contact the person who is a Hailey admin in your system.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I want to see the answers from an employee's prepared questions before we hold the session. How can I do that?">
  If the preparing questions already are answered you can start the sessions by navigating to the **Employee's profile - Feedback** and there clicking on the three dots on the right side of the one-to-one and clicking **Start**. Once the one-to-one is started you will be able to see all the prepared answers. You can always come back to the sessions later.
  </Accordion>

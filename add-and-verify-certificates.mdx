This guide shows you how to add and verify certificates in Hailey!

## What is a certificate?

Certificates are uploaded documents that shows that an employee has a certain competence, education, or authorization. They can work as proof that the individual meets specific requirements for, for example a title.

## Before you start

Before you start adding and verifying certificates on your employees, make sure you have created your certificates under **Settings** - **Skills** - **Certificates**. Read more about how to do so i the guide [HERE.](/certifications)

## How do you add certificates?

Navigate to the employees' profile and click on the Certificate tab. This is an overview of all **Required** and **Additional** certificates.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-45-45-6346-AM.png?width=670&height=380&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-45-45-6346-AM.png)

When adding a certificate that is **required** for the employee, click on the **three dots** to the right - **Add new.** Follow the wizard!

When adding a certificate that is **not** required for the employee, click on **Action - Add certificate.** Follow the wizard further down!

Following steps shows you how to add a certificate step by step!

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-46-15-8043-AM.png?width=670&height=378&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-46-15-8043-AM.png)

This description is made in the settings. Learn more about how to create certificates [HERE](/certifications).

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-46-40-0128-AM.png?width=668&height=377&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-46-40-0128-AM.png)

In the next step, you have the possibility to set dates for the validity of the certificate. <img src="https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-47-07-3116-AM.png?width=668&height=379&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-47-07-3116-AM.png" alt="" />

Add documented proof of the certificate. You cant upload a file and/or an URL.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-47-37-2612-AM.png?width=670&height=380&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-47-37-2612-AM.png)

The last step shows you who the verifier is and you can also make a comment to the verifier.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-48-33-0493-AM.png?width=670&height=379&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-48-33-0493-AM.png)

The certification is now added on the profile with status **Pending**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-49-12-1135-AM.png?width=669&height=353&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-49-12-1135-AM.png)

When you click on the certificate, you get more detailed information.

## How do you renew certificates?

When a certificate is about to **expire** and need to be renewed, the verifier gets an email and a task on their **Dashboard**. To renew the certificate, click on the task and follow the wizard.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-02-01-26-5390-PM.png?width=670&height=388&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-02-01-26-5390-PM.png)

## How do you verify certificates?

As the verifier of certificates, you get a **task** to your **Dashboard**. Click on the task and your view will show all information for this certificate, for example the documented proof.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-50-21-7693-AM.png?width=670&height=362&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-50-21-7693-AM.png)

**Verify** or **Reject** the certificate. If you **verify**, the certificate will get status **Certified** on the employees profile.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-01-59-57-5139-PM.png?width=665&height=381&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-01-59-57-5139-PM.png)

If you **reject** the certificate, you will need to add a reasons for reject. An email will now be sent to the employee who added the certificate, including the rejection comment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-52-23-6844-AM.png?width=666&height=379&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-06-52-23-6844-AM.png)

## Overview of certificates

If you have the rights to view certificates on other employees than yourself, you will get an overview of those certificates by clicking on **Skills - Certifications** in the main menu.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Mar-04-2026-12-20-35-6544-PM.png?width=670&height=378&name=image-png-Mar-04-2026-12-20-35-6544-PM.png)

The overview views the employees and their certifications. The columns shows the status, type and the expiration date. You can **Export** the overview list by clicking **Export** in the top right corner.

## Rights

- **View** on your own profile makes it possible to see all information under tab **Certificates** on your own profile.
- **View** on other employees makes it possible to see all information under tab **Certification** on the employee profiles + access to **Skills - Certification** in the main menu for employees who is scoped.
- **View & edit** certificates gives you the right to view, add and delete certificates.
- **Settings - Create** certificates gives full access to create new and manage already added certificates under **Settings**.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The employee’s certificate has the status **Expired**, but I haven’t received any notification. Why is that?">
    Notifications are sent based on your settings. Go to **Settings – Skills – Certificates** to ensure that the function **Notify before expiration date** is enabled, and double-check who is listed in the field **Who should be notified?**

    **Please note** that the notification function only applies to **Required certificates** (not Other certificates).

    ![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-03-06-58-1287-PM.png?width=670&height=338&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-03-06-58-1287-PM.png)

    You can also use the overview in **HR Hub – Tasks** to search and see whether the task **Renew certificate** has been sent out to the employee in question.

    ![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2026-03-06-27-1564-PM.png?width=670&height=227&name=image-png-Jul-01-2026-03-06-27-1564-PM.png)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between a **Required** and an **Other** certificate?">
    Required certificates are based on the employee’s organizational belonging. This means that there are certain certificates the employee is expected to have because they belong to a specific part of the organization. Required certificates can, for example, be determined by **Legal entity,** **Team**, or **Title**.

    An _Other_ certificate is independent of the employee’s organizational affiliation and can essentially be added to any employee. The certificate is not a requirement, but something the employee has in addition to what is expected.

    Read more about how to create certificates in **THIS guide!**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What do the different statuses mean, and when does a status change?">
    A certificate can have one of the following statuses:

    - No certificate
    - Expired
    - Certified
    - Pending
    - Expiring soon

    **No certificate** is shown when an employee is expected to have a certificate, but none has been added to their profile. If a certificate has been added but not yet verified, it appears as **Pending**.

    Once a certificate has been verified, it can have the status **Certified** or **Expiring soon**, depending on your certificate settings.

    When a certificate’s expiration date has passed, it is given the status **Expired**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

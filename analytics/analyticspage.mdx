Understand how Hailey presents data and calculates analytics, and learn how to create custom dashboards to visualize and analyze the metrics that matter most to your organization.

## What are dashboards?

In Hailey, four standard analytics dashboards are available: **Company Overview**, **Headcount**, **Monthly Salary**, and **Absence**. These analytics provide a summary or average of the data based on the filters you apply. All analytics are generated from information stored in employee profiles.

In addition to the standard dashboards, you can create your own **Custom Dashboards** from scratch without using Hailey’s predefined templates. This allows you to tailor visualizations and metrics to your organization's specific needs and priorities.

Custom Dashboards are created and managed through the **Creator's Corner**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-15-2026-12-34-25-4385-PM.png?width=656&height=316&name=image-png-May-15-2026-12-34-25-4385-PM.png)

## Why use custom dashboards?

Would you like deeper insights into how your organization is developing over time? With Hailey’s analytics tools, you can gain a clear overview of key HR metrics such as employee turnover, organizational changes, and other important workforce trends.

All data is automatically compiled from information already stored in employee profiles, providing you with up-to-date and easily accessible insights to support decision-making. This enables you to identify trends, make more data-driven decisions, and proactively support the development of your organization.

With **Custom Dashboards**, you can tailor analytics and visualizations to focus on the metrics that matter most to your business, ensuring that stakeholders have quick access to the information they need.

## Create your own dashboard

You can build custom dashboards by selecting the analytics and metrics that are most relevant to your organization. Once created, dashboards can be shared with everyone in the organization or with specific groups of users, depending on your needs and preferences.

All created dashboards are displayed in **Creator’s Corner** for administrators and employees who have permission to manage dashboards.

For more information about access and dashboard management permissions, please refer to the **Roles and Permissions** section below.

Click **Create Dashboard** to get started. You can choose from a selection of ready-made templates, which can either be used as they are or customized to suit your needs. Alternatively, you can build a dashboard entirely from scratch.

To create a new dashboard from scratch, select **Build Your Own**. Start by giving the dashboard a name and adding a description. You will then be able to choose which data and analytics should be included in the dashboard.

You can also select an emoji to appear next to the dashboard name. This emoji will be displayed as the dashboard’s cover icon in **Creator’s Corner**, making it easier to identify and distinguish from other dashboards.

**Should the dashboard only contain specific data?** Select the organizational units you would like to include. If you leave this field blank, the dashboard will display data for all employees.

Once you have made your selections, click **Save** before continuing.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-15-2026-12-36-45-4594-PM.png?width=432&height=779&name=image-png-May-15-2026-12-36-45-4594-PM.png)

<Tip>
  You can always update the dashboard’s **name**, **description**, **visibility settings**, and the **organizational units included** in the dashboard through the **Settings** menu located in the top-right corner.
</Tip>

In the next step, you can create widgets to visualize data based on your organization’s specific needs and preferences.

Start by giving your widget a name, for example **“Number of Employees”**, and then select the metric you would like to display.

Next, choose how you want the data to be visualized. Depending on the type of data selected, you can display it as a number, bar chart, line chart, pie chart or table.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-15-2026-12-29-30-6802-PM.png?width=626&height=571&name=image-png-May-15-2026-12-29-30-6802-PM.png)

The final step is to decide which data should be displayed in the widget. Would you like to include all employees, or only a specific part of the organization?

If you would like the widget to display data for a specific group, select **Filtered**. You can then choose to include data based on:

**Legal Entity,** **Workplace,** **Department,** **Cost Center,** **Team,** **Job Title** or Employees who **report to a specific manager**

To add more widgets to your dashboard, click the **plus (+) icon**. You can then arrange your dashboard according to your preferences by moving widgets, changing their position, minimizing them, or resizing them as needed.

Start by naming the Dashboard and give it a description. After that, you will be able to choose which data should be included in the ongoing  Dashboard. You also have the option to select an emoji to the left of the dashboard name. This will be visible in **Creators Corner** as the cover image.

Should the Dashboard only contain certain data? Select which organizational units should be included. If you leave the field empty, the dashboard will display data for all employees. Click **Start building** to proceed.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-15-2026-12-36-45-4594-PM.png?width=432&height=779&name=image-png-May-15-2026-12-36-45-4594-PM.png)

<Tip>
  You can always change the name, description, add who the dashboard should be visible for, and modify the organizational unit included in the dashboard via **Settings** in the top right corner.
</Tip>

In the next view, you will have the opportunity to create widgets with visualized data based on your needs and preferences. Start by naming your widget, for example “number of employees” and then choose which metric you want to visualize.

In the next step, you select how you want the data to be displayed. The options are to show the data as a **number**, **bar chart**, **line chart**, **pie chart** or **table**, depending on the data you have chosen to include.

Last but not least, choose which data should be displayed in the widget. Should everyone be included, or do you only want to include parts of the organization on which the visualized data will be based? If the latter applies, click on **Filtered**. Here you can choose to include data from a specific **Legal entity**, **Workplace**, **Department**, **Cost center**, **Team**, **Title**, or **Employees** **reporting to a specific manager**.

Add additional widgets by clicking the **plus sign** in the middle. Move, rearrange, minimize, or enlarge your created views according to your preferences.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-15-2026-12-26-48-0220-PM.png?width=628&height=335&name=image-png-May-15-2026-12-26-48-0220-PM.png)

Once you are satisfied with your dashboard, click **Save** in the top-right corner to store your changes.

You can access all created dashboards by returning to **Creator’s Corner**, provided that you are an administrator or have permission to manage dashboards.

If you would like to share a dashboard without granting management permissions, open the dashboard **Settings** and add selected employees under **Visibility**. This allows specific users to view the dashboard while restricting their ability to modify or manage it.

## Managing dashboards

Use the **Filters** panel on the left-hand side of the dashboard to narrow down and analyze specific parts of your organization.

You can also filter data for a specific time period by using the **calendar** in the upper-right corner. Simply select the desired date range to update the dashboard and view analytics for that period.

These filtering options make it easy to focus on the data that is most relevant to your analysis and reporting needs.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-24-2025-04-04-15-8910-PM.png?width=669&height=348&name=image-png-Nov-24-2025-04-04-15-8910-PM.png)

## Roles & rights

As an administrator, you have access to all **Analytics** in Hailey. If you would like other users to access Analytics, you must grant the appropriate permissions through **Roles & rights**.

If additional employees should be able to **create** and **manage dashboards**, this permission must also be assigned in **Roles & rights**. Select the role, other than Administrator, that should be allowed to build and manage dashboards.

To do this, go to **Roles & rights,** select the role you want to update, click the **three dots** and choose **Edit**. Scroll down to Company rights and then Analytics to enable the relevant dashboad and analytics permissions.

For example, if you would like your managers to create and maintain their own dashboards, you can configure those permissions here.

For more information about roles and rights, please see the documentation available [HERE](/roles-and-rights).

## What does the integration do?

The Microsoft Teams integration consists of two components: **Hailey's Teams Bot** and incoming **Webhooks**. You can choose to enable either one of them or both, depending on your needs.

**Hailey's Teams Bot** allows employees to report time off directly in Teams, see which colleagues are currently absent, and receive notifications in Teams when they have new tasks in Hailey's To-do list (you can find the included tasks [**HERE**](#what-data-s-included)).

If **incoming webhooks** are enabled, notifications for birthdays, work anniversaries, and employees' first day of employment can also be posted to a selected Teams channel, making it easier to celebrate and recognize important events together

## How does the integration work?

Once **Hailey’s Teams Bot** has been enabled, employees will automatically receive notifications in Teams whenever they are assigned new tasks in Hailey. The bot can also be used at any time to report time-off or to see which colleagues are absent on a specific day.

If **webhooks** are enabled, notifications are automatically sent once per day to the Teams channel selected during setup. These notifications include relevant events for the day, such as birthdays, work anniversaries, and the first working day of new employees.

## What data is included in the integration?

### Hailey’s Teams Bot

- Report your own sick leave for today (provided this has been enabled in the setup)
- Access your To-do list
- Retrieve contact information
- View which colleagues are absent today

Whether or not the bot is actively used, employees will automatically receive task notifications in Teams as long as the integration is enabled. The tasks that are automatically sent through Teams relate to:

- Salary
- One-to-ones
- Workflows
- Contract signings
- 360° Reviews

### Webhooks

You can choose to send notifications for the following events:

- Birthdays
- Work Anniversaries
- New joiners

## What is required to be included in the integration?

### Hailey’s Teams Bot

To use **Hailey’s Teams Bot**, each employee must activate the bot in Microsoft Teams. Instructions on how to complete the activation can be found [**HERE**.](#employees-setup)

### Webhooks

By default, all employees are included in webhook notifications. If you would like to exclude specific employees or certain types of events from these notifications, this can easily be configured in Hailey. Instructions on how to exclude employees or specific events can be found [**HERE**](#excluding-employees-from-webbhooks).

Now it is time to **Create a flow**. Enter a **Flow name** and click **Next** to proceed.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-25-2025-09-25-32-2602-AM.png?width=670&height=362&name=image-png-Apr-25-2025-09-25-32-2602-AM.png)

In the next step it is time to choose in which **Team** and **Channel** the notification card should be posted. After you’ve selected **Team** and **Channel** – click **Create flow**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-25-2025-09-26-43-1603-AM.png?width=670&height=357&name=image-png-Apr-25-2025-09-26-43-1603-AM.png)

## Excluding employees from Webhooks

If you have employees who prefer not to be recognized publicly, you can exclude them from webhook notifications. Similarly, you can choose to exclude **New Colleagues**, **Work Anniversaries**, or **Birthdays** if you do not want one or more of these events to generate notifications in Teams.

To review and manage these settings, navigate to **Settings - General - Culture Corner**.

Employees who do not wish to be included in any notifications should be added under **Exclude Specific Employees**.

From this page, you can also decide which types of notifications are relevant for your organization and control which events are published to your selected Teams channel.

<Note>
  These settings also control what is displayed in the Culture Corner section on Hailey’s homepage.
</Note>

## Common questions

**Question 1:** Employees cannot find the Bot in Teams?

For employees to use the **Hailey Teams Bot**, they must activate the bot themselves in Microsoft Teams. The setup process is quick and easy, employees simply need to follow the instructions [**HERE**](#employees-setup).

**Question 2**: Why are webhook notifications not being sent for certain employees?

First, check whether the employee has been excluded from **Culture Corner**, as employees excluded there are also excluded from Teams webhook notifications. In addition, the employee's employment status may affect whether they are included in notifications. Employees with the employment statuses **Has left** or **Garden leave** will not be included in webhook notifications.

**Question 3:** Will employees be able to access personal data in the Teams Bot that they normally cannot see in Hailey?

No. **Hailey’s Teams Bot** follows the same **Roles and rights** settings that you have configured in Hailey. As a result, the bot will only display information that the user is authorized to access and will not expose personal data or other information that the user would not normally be able to view in Hailey

**Question 4**: An employee has not activated the Teams Bot but is still receiving tasks from It. Is that expected?

Yes. Any tasks that are automatically sent through the Teams Bot will be delivered regardless of whether the employee has activated the bot in Microsoft Teams. However, if an employee wants to interact with the bot directly by chatting with it, reporting time-off they must first activate the bot in their Teams account.

**Question 5:** Can we set up different Webhooks for different Teams channels?

No. At the moment, it is only possible to select **one Teams channel** to receive notifications from **Culture Corner.**

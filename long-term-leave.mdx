## What is long term leave?

When an employee is absent for a longer period, it can be helpful to group and manage these absences separately and add their time off as **long term leave**.

**Long-term leave features:**

- Is managed as one continuous period instead of separate days.
- Ability to trigger workflows based on the start and end dates of long term leave. Please read more about how that works [HERE](/create-workflows#Time-off-and-Workflows).

## Before you get started

Before you get started using long term leave, it is important you have the correct time and time off settings. Read more about how to set this up [HERE.](/settings-for-longterm-leave)

<Note>
  Note that if you register **time off** in **hours**, make sure employees have **work hours** registered on their profiles for the long term leave to be activated once registered. Read more about work hours [HERE](/work-hours).
</Note>

## How do you add long term leave?

Adding long term leave follows the same process as adding time off as usual. Read more about how to add time off in the guide [HERE](/time-off-submit).

<Note>
  Long term leave is also registered on non-working days.
</Note>

## How do you approve Long term leave?

The time off request will follow your approval principals settings. You can read more about how to set up your approval principles for time off [HERE.](/create-reporting-policy)

The approver gets a task on their dashboard with information and option to **Reject** or **Accept**.

<Note>
  Long-term leave **only** applies to 100 percent absence.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-07-2026-08-58-32-3015-AM.png?width=670&height=345&name=image-png-Aug-07-2026-08-58-32-3015-AM.png)

When the employees long term leave if approved, this will be displayed below their profile picture. Go to the **employee** **profile - Time tab** and **hover over the leave period** in the overview to see more detailed information.

## Overview of long term leave

Get an overview of employees on long term leave in the **Time overview**. Use the filter function  to see who is on longterm leave using the Time & time off filters.

## Time Cycles During Long Term Leave

If an employee is **partially** on long term leave during a month, an email and a task will be sent to them to approve their time cycle. If an employee is on long term leave for an **entire** month, neither an email nor a task will be sent.

## Common questions

**Question**: Why doesn't long term leave register on the employee?

The long term leave registers on the employee profile if it completes one of the following requirements:

- The time off is registered in **Percentage**
- The time off is registered in **Hours** and the employee has **Work hours** registered.

<Note>
  **Questions**: I don't want those employees to be visible for others.

  Employees on long-term leave are hidden from **“Who’s away”**, **“Manager agenda”**, and the **Holiday plan**.
</Note>

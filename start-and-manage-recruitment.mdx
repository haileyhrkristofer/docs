## What is recruitment?

A recruitment in Hailey is a digital workflow for managing an open position, where you use ATS (Applicant Tracking System) features to advertise, receive applications, communicate with candidates, and track the process step by step. Everything is centralized within the system - from first contact to hiring.

## Before you get started

To start a **Recruitment**, you first need to create a **Career Page**.

If you have not created a Career Page yet, follow the instructions in this guide:

- Read more about how to **Create a Career Page** in [**THIS**](/career-page) guide.

Once the **Career Page** has been created, you can proceed with starting your **Recruitment.**

How do you start a recruitment?

To start your recruitment, select **Recruitment** from the main menu - **Actions - Start recruitment.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-42-39-8743-AM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-42-39-8743-AM.png)

When starting a **Recruitment**, the first step is to give the recruitment a **Name** and assign it to a **Location** and a **Department**. You can also specify a **Salary** by choosing either an **Initial salary** or a **Salary range**.

The next part of the setup is deciding which steps should be included in your recruitment process. Three steps are mandatory: **New applications**, **To be hired**, and **Rejected**.

Hailey will suggest steps which can be removed from the kanban board, and you can add your own steps by clicking the **Add step** button. Once you are satisfied with the setup, click **Start recruitment**.

In addition to the suggested steps, customers also use the following steps:

- Application Screening
- Assessments
- Work Sample Review
- Case Interview
- Group Interview
- Background / Security Screening
- Offer & Contract

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-47-25-2831-AM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-47-25-2831-AM.png)

Your draft has now been created! You will now be taken to an empty **Kanban board**, where applications will start coming in.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-48-32-8995-AM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-48-32-8995-AM.png)

<Note>
  You can choose later whether the Location, Department, and Salary should be displayed in the **Job ad**. Read more about how to **Create and manage job ads** in [**THIS**](/create-and-manage-job-ads) guide.
</Note>

## How do you edit a recruitment?

If you want to change any of the settings for an existing recruitment, click **Manage** - **Edit recruitment**.

From there, you can update the recruitment **Name**, change the **Location** and **Department**, add or remove **Steps**, adjust the assigned **Recruiters**, or **Delete** the recruitment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-54-41-5047-AM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-11-54-41-5047-AM.png)

<Note>
  If you remove a step that currently contains applicants, they will automatically be moved to the **New Applications** step. Read more about how to **Manage your candidates**, and therefore your applications, in **[/candidate-management](/candidate-management)THIS** guide.
</Note>

## How do you finalize a recruitment?

To finalize your recruitment, go to **Recruitment** in the main menu, select the relevant recruitment, and then click **Actions** in the upper-right corner. From there, click **Finalize recruitment.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-01-24-7912-PM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-01-24-7912-PM.png)

When you click **Finalize recruitment**, the recruitment process is marked as completed, indicating that the hiring process has been concluded. The recruitment will then have the status **Finalized**, but you can still make any necessary changes to candidate information, the job ad, or other details.

We recommend reading the message that appears on the screen carefully before clicking **Yes, I'm sure**. Once you have confirmed the action, it cannot be undone.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-03-09-3532-PM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-03-09-3532-PM.png)

<Note>
  When you finalize a recruitment, you will likely also want to unpublish the related **Job Ad**. Read more about **Job Ads** and how to do this in [**/create-and-manage-job-adsTHIS**](/create-and-manage-job-ads) guide.
</Note>

## Recruitment overview

All your created recruitments are displayed when you click **Recruitment** in the main menu. Recruitments are shown in the order they were created, with the most recently created recruitment appearing at the top of the list.

You can also filter recruitments by **Ongoing** and **Finalized**.

<Note>
  When you open a **Finalized** recruitment, you can access all candidate information, including applications, uploaded files, notes, and messages. You can also configure your recruitment data retention settings. Read more about this in [**/gdprTHIS**](/gdpr) guide.
</Note>

The number of applicants is displayed for each step in the recruitment process, along with information about who created the recruitment, when it was created, and whether a job ad is currently published.

It is also possible to **Sort** recruitments by creation date, number of applications, or name. In addition, you can sort by **Location** and **Department**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-11-48-6621-PM.png?width=670&height=390&name=image-png-Aug-21-2026-12-11-48-6621-PM.png)

## Rights

As an Admin in Hailey, you automatically have the access to **Recruitments** right. Read more about **Roles & right** in [**THIS**](/roles-and-rights) guide.

- **None** - The Recruitment section is **hidden** from the main menu.
- **View** - Allows the user to **view** all content within the **Recruitment section** in the main menu, including **Career Pages, Recruitments,** and **Job ads.**
- **View & Edit** - Gives the user **full access** to manage the **Recruitment module**, including the ability to create, edit, and delete **Recruitments, Career Pages, and Job Ads.**

Do you have hiring managers or employees who should only be given access to specific recruitments?

If so, you can add one or more recruiters in the **Recruiters** field when you start your recruitment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-14-2026-11-34-46-1041-AM.png?width=670&height=389&name=image-png-Aug-14-2026-11-34-46-1041-AM.png)

It is also possible to add one or more recruiters after the recruitment has been started. To do this, click on the specific recruitment - **Actions** - **Edit Recruitment**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-14-2026-11-36-11-2646-AM.png?width=670&height=384&name=image-png-Aug-14-2026-11-36-11-2646-AM.png)

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What rights should I assign to a recruiter who should not have access to all employee data?">
    To ensure that an employee can initiate hires in Hailey and send out employment contracts, we recommend creating a new role in Settings and assigning the following rights:

    - Right to view employment information for all employees
    - Right to initiate hire
    - Right to act as the company signatory (if the recruiter should sign the contracts)
    - Right to view people in the hiring process
    - Right to view upcoming employees

    This setup allows the employee responsible for recruitment to manage the hiring process, while still preventing access to sensitive.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I see new messages in recruitment chats?">
    New messages can be found under **Recruitment** next to the relevant recruitment. **A red dot** will also appear on the conversation icon within the specific recruitment when new messages are available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I receive notifications for new candidates in recruitment?">
    Yes, you can enable notifications for new candidates if you want to receive updates. When activated, the recruiter will receive an email whenever a new candidate or application is submitted.

    You can find this setting in the **Recruitment ATS** under the first step, **New applications**. Click the three dots on the side and choose whether you want to **select all in line** or enable/disable notifications for new applicants.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I accidentally finalized a recruitment, but it was a mistake. How can I undo it?">
    Once a recruitment has been Finalized, the action cannot be undone. However, even though the recruitment has been finalized, it is still possible to receive applications from candidates, provided that the Job ad has not been unpublished.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

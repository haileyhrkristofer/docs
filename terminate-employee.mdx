## What does it mean to terminate an employment?

When it's time to say goodbye to a colleague, you can easily manage the entire termination process in Hailey. Here, you register the employee's last day of employment and last working day, and choose whether the employee's salaries should be ended automatically on the same date. You can also create and send contracts, as well as initiate a workflow if needed. This gives you a smooth and structured offboarding process, with all the important steps managed in one place.

## How do you terminate an employment?

To start the process, navigate to the employee card in question and click **Actions - Terminate**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-44-12-0076-PM.png?width=670&height=429&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-44-12-0076-PM.png)

A wizard will now guide you through the process.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-44-27-1946-PM.png?width=670&height=419&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-44-27-1946-PM.png)

Proceed to the first step and start off by entering the last date of employment, along with the last working day. In this step you also get the option to **end salary/-ies** automatically at last day of employment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-45-00-1226-PM.png?width=670&height=417&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-45-00-1226-PM.png)

<Note>
  **Last working day:**

  Entering a last working day is useful if the employee, for any reson, will stop working earlier than the actual date for end of employment. The employee will then have the employment status **Garden leave** during the period between the last working day and the last day of employment.

  ![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-45-12-0591-PM.png?width=670&height=133&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-45-12-0591-PM.png)
</Note>

The next step, you can choose whether or not to sign a contract of the termination.

- **Proceed without a contract** – No contract will be created, and the employment termination will be completed immediately after the final step of the wizard.
- **Configure a contract** – Select a contract template and follow the steps to create the contract.

<Note>
  Please note that if you choose to continue without creating a contract, **the termination of employment will be registered immediately**. The employment will then end on the end date you specified in the first step.
</Note>

![Skärmavbild 2026-07-24 kl. 14.45.35](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2014.45.35.png?width=670&height=417&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2014.45.35.png)

If you want to sign a contract of the termination, select **Configure a contract** and choose a template from the drop-down menu.

<Note>
  Please note that if you choose to use a contract, the employment **will not be terminated until the signing process has been completed**.
</Note>

![Skärmavbild 2026-07-24 kl. 14.50.37](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2014.50.37.png?width=670&height=423&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2014.50.37.png)

<Note>
  To be able to choose a contract from the drop-down menu, a contract template must be created. How to create the template is explained **[HERE](/create-contract-templates).** Only templates that are registered as **terminating one employee** will be visiable.
</Note>

In the following step, if the contract template contains optional sections and/or optional attachments, select the ones you want to include in the contract. If the contract has any **input fields**, fill them in here as well.

![Skärmavbild 2026-07-24 kl. 15.04.15](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2015.04.15.png?width=670&height=420&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2015.04.15.png)

Finally, you create the signing process.

- Under **Signing Method**, choose how the contract will be signed, either with BankID or digital autograph.
- Under the **Review step**, you can assign a reviewer to review the contract before it is sent for signing. The reviewer can either approve or reject the contract with a comment. If the contract is approved, it will be sent for signing. If it is rejected, you will receive the reviewer's comment so you can make the necessary changes before sending the contract again.
- Under **Signing step 1**, the **employee** and the **manager** are included by default. If you want to adjust the signing steps or the signing order, click **Add Signing Step** and add the number of signing steps you want, as well as which employee(s) should sign in each step.
- The signing process follows the order of the steps. If there are multiple signatories in the same step, they will all receive the contract at the same time.

![Skärmavbild 2026-07-24 kl. 15.23.14](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2015.23.14.png?width=670&height=498&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-07-24%20kl.%2015.23.14.png)

In the final step you are provided with a **Contract Summary**, where you see an overview of

- the employee whose employment is ending
- the final dates
- which signers are involved in the process and also the signing order

Click **Preview** to see the entire contract and the green arrow to send the contract. The selected signing order determines whether the contract is sent out to all parties directly or if there are multiple steps.

![Skärmavbild 2026-05-11 kl. 15.08.27](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-05-11%20kl.%2015.08.27.png?width=670&height=482&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-05-11%20kl.%2015.08.27.png)

<Warning>
  The termination will **not be done** until the signing is **finalized**.
</Warning>

In the last step of the termination wizard you get the option to start a custom workflow on the employee. This is not mandatory, and any automated workflows regarding e.g. offboarding will still be activated accordingly if they are not started from the wizard.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-55-42-1858-PM.png?width=670&height=232&name=image-png-Nov-17-2025-02-55-42-1858-PM.png)

While the signing is still in progress, the pending termination will remain visible on the employee’s profile.

![Skärmavbild 2026-04-17 kl. 09.05.39](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-04-17%20kl.%2009.05.39.png?width=670&height=208&name=Sk%C3%A4rmavbild%202026-04-17%20kl.%2009.05.39.png)

When the contract has been signed by all parties the termination is complete - the employee will receive the status **Leaving** and the end date will be visiable .

## Rights

As an Admin in Hailey, you will always be able to terminate an employee. Should anyone else have the right to do this, you will need to assign them the right to **Terminate**.

<Note>
  Read more about roles and rights [HERE](/roles-and-rights).
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the employment terminated immediately?">
    No. The employment is not terminated immediately. It will end on the end date you set in the first step of the process. Until then, the employee will have the status **Leaving**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the difference between deleting an employee and terminating an employment?">
    When you terminate an employment, the employee's profile remains in the system while their data is removed according to your GDPR settings. This is beneficial for maintaining statistics and historical records, and it also makes it easy to rehire the employee in the future.

    When you delete an employee instead, the entire profile and all associated data are permanently removed from the system. This option is therefore used less frequently and is primarily intended for situations where a profile was created by mistake or should no longer exist for any reason.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to send a contract when terminating an employment?">
    No. You can terminate an employment without sending a contract. Simply select **Continue without contract** in the wizard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I ended an employee's salary by mistake (or too early) during the termination process. What should I do?">
    You can easily reactivate the salary from the employee's profile. Navigate to the **Compensation** tab, open the relevant salary, click **Actions**, and select **Reactivate salary**. This will remove the salary's end date and restore its status to **Active**. You can then end the salary manually from the employee's profile when the time is right.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

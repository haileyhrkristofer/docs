Create your benefit types to begin managing employee benefits in Hailey!

## What are benefits?

Benefits are employee compensations provided in addition to salary, such as wellness allowances, company cars, and other taxable or non-taxable benefits.

## Why use benefits?

Does your organization offer different types of employee benefits -such as a wellness allowance or a phone allowance? Benefits are a great way to manage and differentiate various forms of compensation. They also make it easier for both you and your employees to track these perks whenever needed.

## How do you create benefits?

To create benefits, navigate to **Settings - Compensation - Benefits** and click **Add new - Benefit.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2026-07-26-35-2298-AM.png?width=670&height=278&name=image-png-Aug-28-2026-07-26-35-2298-AM.png)

Enter a name for the benefit and a description if you wish. Then choose whether the benefits frequency is **Monthly** or **Yearly**. When you’re ready, click **Save**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-28-2026-07-27-56-1193-AM.png?width=670&height=388&name=image-png-Aug-28-2026-07-27-56-1193-AM.png)

## Edit or delete benefits

If you want to edit the information for a benefit or delete it, click on the **three dots to the right** and make the changes needed.

You can not delete a benefit that is being used on one or more employees profile.

Once you’ve created your benefits, the next step is to assign them to your employees. Read more about how to **Manage benefits** i the guide [HERE](/benefit-change).

How do you update employees' time & time off settings?

Once your time & time off settings have been configured in **Settings - Time & time off**, they need to be assigned to the relevant employees' employments. You can do this either from an individual **employee's profile** or in bulk from the **Employee list**.

- **Update settings in bulk from the employee list -** mark the employees whose settings you want to update and click **Action - Update time & time off settings**. Choose your settings and click **Save**.

The settings will be updated on the **active** employment. If there is no active employment, the settings will apply to the employment that was last terminated or the employment that is planned to start.

<Note>
  **Note** that all four settings are included in the bulk update. If a field is left blank, its existing value will be **removed** from the selected employees' profiles.
</Note>

- Update settings directly on the **employee’s profile** - click on the employment you want to update and click on **Action - Edit time and time off - Save.**

## Rights

- **Employment** **-** **View & edit** allows you to edit time and time off settings on an employee's employment.
- **Action - Bulk update organizational fields** allows you to update these settings for multiple employees at once from the employee list.

<Note>
  Please note that these rights also give access to update other fields within the employment.
</Note>

Read more about Roles & rights in the guide [HERE](/roles-and-rights).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="One of our employees is about to get a new employment and will need to start reporting time and/or time off. How do I do this?">
    Time and time off settings are linked to an **employment**, not to the employee as a person. If an employee changes role or receives updated employment terms that result in a **new employment** in Hailey, the time and time off settings can be updated in the **Add New Employment** workflow.

    Read more about this process in the guide [**HERE**](/employment-wizard).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to update the work hours for an entire team. How do I do that?">
    Go to the **Employee list**, filter the list by the team you want to update, select all employees, and choose **Actions - Update time & time off settings**.

    **Keep in mind** that all Time & time off fields of the employment will be updated. If you only want to change the **Work hours**, you still need to fill in the other fields with their current values. If you leave a field empty, the current value will be removed from the employees' employment.

    Tip: If possible, update employees who share the same time & time off settings in the same bulk update. This makes it easier to fill in all fields correctly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

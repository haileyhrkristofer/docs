## Settings

Your organization can decide how employees sign in to Hailey. To manage your organization's sign-in methods, go to **Settings** - **Security** - **Log in**. You can choose from the following sign-in methods:

- Microsoft (SSO)
- Email login
- Google (SSO)
- Okta (SSO)

Select the sign-in method(s) you would like to make available for your organization. You can have mutliple sign-in methods active at the same time if needed!

<Note>
  If you plan to switch to another sign-in method, **we strongly recommend** testing the new method before disabling the current one. This helps prevent the risk of accidentally locking yourself out from Hailey. To test a new sign-in method: Enable the new sign-in method, sign out from Hailey, sign back in using the new method and confirm that everything works as expected. Once you've verified that it works, you can disable the old one.
</Note>

## Email login

Email login is the default sign-in method in Hailey. To sign in using Email login, **enter your email address and password** on the login page. For additional security, we recommend activating Two-Factor Authentication. This provides an extra layer of security for your account by requiring both your password and a one-time verification code when you sign in.

Navigate to **Settings** - **Security** - **Email Login** - **toggle** **on** **two-factor authentication** to enable.

When this is activated, all users are required to enter a verification code sent to their email address after signing in with their email and password.

## Logging in with SSO

**Single Sign-On (SSO)** allows users to sign in to Hailey using **their existing company login credentials**. When SSO is enabled, employees do not need separate login credentials for Hailey. This saves time and makes login easier, and more secure.

Click the links below to find more information about our different **SSO options**:

- [**Microsoft SSO**](/sign-in-with-microsoft)
- [**Google SSO**](/sign-in-with-google)
- [**Okta SSO**](/sign-in-with-okta)

## Multiple logins with the same email

If you are part of a group of companies or work with subcontractors who use Hailey, it may be useful to have multiple Hailey accounts connected to the same email address. The multiple logins feature **allows a user to have multiple Hailey accounts linked to the same company email address**. If a person's company email address is connected to more than one account, the user will be prompted to choose which account they would like to login to during sing-in.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-16-2024-02-00-50-9616-PM.png?width=554&height=326&name=image-png-May-16-2024-02-00-50-9616-PM.png)

If the organizations connected to your email do not have the same sign-in methods activated, you will not be asked to choose an account. Instead, you will be signed in directly into the organization that supports the sign-in method you used.

## How to invite employees

Before employees can sign in to Hailey, they must have an email address entered in the **Company email** field on their employee profile, and they must have **received an invitation** to the platform. To avoid any issues, make sure to add a company email to your employees' profiles before sending invitations!

There are two ways to invite employees to Hailey, either via the Employee list or the Employee profile.

1. Go to Employees in the main menu, click **Action** - **Invite employees to Hailey.**
2. Open the Employee profile, click **Action** - **Invite employee to Hailey.**

<Tip>
  Need to invite multiple employees at once? Send out inivtations from the employee list! This makes it quick and easy to get everyone set up.
</Tip>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-14-2026-09-38-22-0987-AM.png?width=670&height=349&name=image-png-Jul-14-2026-09-38-22-0987-AM.png)

Once an employee has been invited, an invitation email is automatically sent to the address listed in the employee's **Company email** field. To access Hailey, the employee simply needs to open the invitation email, click **Go to Hailey**, and log in using the sign-in method enabled by your organization.

<Tip>
  Not sure which employees have already been invited? Go to **Employees** in the main menu, select **Other Filters** and filter by **Account Status**. This way you can see who has been invited.
</Tip>

## FAQ's

Below you will find a list of frequently asked questions that both **Employees** and **Admins** can use to troubleshoot login issues.

Please note that Hailey does not have access to your account and cannot see which sign-in method you're expected to use or which email address is registered on your employee profile.

If you need information about which sign-in method or email address to use, we recommend reaching out to your organisation's administrator.

**Question 1:** How do I know I've been invited, and which email should I use to log in?

When an admin invites you, an email is sent from [**noreply@haileyhr.com**](mailto:noreply@haileyhr.com). The email is sent out to the email address entered in the **Company email** field on your employee profile, and this is the same email address you should use whn logging in to Hailey.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-11-2026-11-34-17-8741-AM.png?width=670&height=315&name=image-png-May-11-2026-11-34-17-8741-AM.png)

**Question 2**: Our administrator has invited me, but I can't find my invitation?

The invitation is sent to the email address entered in the **Company email** field on your employee profile in Hailey. Please double-check if:

- The invitation have been sent to your personal email, work email, or another one of your inboxes?
- An invitation email appear if you search for the sender [**noreply@haileyhr.com**](mailto:noreply@haileyhr.com) in any of your inboxes?

If the answer is no, we recommend contacting your Hailey administrator so they can confirm that you've been invited and verify which email address the invitation was sent to.

**Question 3:** I get an error message when logging in with SSO. What does this mean?

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-01-16-03-6794-PM.png?width=670&height=352&name=image-png-May-04-2026-01-16-03-6794-PM.png)

If you receive the error message _"The email address associated with your external identity provider doesn't match any registered users in Hailey HR"_, it is usually caused by on of the following:

- SSO is not enabled as a sign-in method method in your organization.
- You are trying to log in with a different email address than the one added in the Company email field on your Hailey profile.
- The email address on your Hailey-profile does not match the email address used to sign in to your Microsoft, Google or Okta account.

We recommend either trying to log in using **Email** + **Password** or asking your Hailey administrator to double-check that the correct email address is entered on the **Company email** field on your profile.

**Question 4:** It get "_Invalid email address or password_" when I log in. What does this mean, and what do I do?

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-01-17-00-7894-PM.png?width=670&height=350&name=image-png-May-04-2026-01-17-00-7894-PM.png)

This is likely because you've entered an incorrect email address or password, or because **Email + Password** is not activated as a sign-in method in your organisation. Please double-check that you are:

- Logging in with the email address entered in the **Company email** field on your profile in Hailey.
- Entering the correct password.
- Using a sign-in method that your organisation has activated.

If you're unsure of your password, you can always click **Don't remember your Hailey HR password** to reset it. Once reset, a new password will be sent to the email address entered in the **Company email** field on your Hailey profile.

If you're unsure which sign-in method is activated for your organization, we recommend reaching out to your Hailey administrator.

**Question 5:** I'm unable to log in. The page turns white and keeps loading when I select **Log in**. Why?

This is usually caused by a firewall or network restriction within your IT-environment that is blocking the connection to the service.

Please try the following:

- Check that the URL is spelled correctly.
- Perform a **hard refresh** of the page.

If the issue persists:

- Try connecting through a different network.
- Try using a different web browser.
- Clear your browser's cache and cookies.

If you are still unable to sign in, please contact your IT department and ask them to whitelist the following domains:

- **hhr.app**
- **haileyhr.app**

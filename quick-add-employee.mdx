Quick add lets you create a profile in Hailey without going through the full hiring process. In this guide you'll learn when to use it, how it works, and how it differs from Initiate hire.

## When to use Quick add?

You should use **Quick add** when you need to add someone to Hailey without going through the full hiring process. It's a faster way to create an employee profile for those who need access to the platform but don't need a full employee setup (_no signings, salaries, workflows and so on_).

Quick add is best suited for:

- External payroll parties fetching **Reports** from Hailey
- Service accounts used for activating integrations or retrieving access tokens
- Anyone who doesn't need a full employee record in Hailey – such as board members or external consultants

<Note>
  For most new employees, we recommend using **Initiate hire**, as it gives you the full benefit of Hailey's onboarding and employer branding features. Read more [**HERE**](/iniate-hire).
</Note>

## How to quick add an employee?

Go to **Employees** and click **Add employee** in the upper right corner. After that, select **Quick add**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-07-2026-07-59-29-5399-AM.png?width=670&height=384&name=image-png-Aug-07-2026-07-59-29-5399-AM.png)

In the next step, fill in the employee's information and click **Add new** to create the profile. You can always go back to the employee's profile to update or add more information later if needed.

<Tip>
  The fields marked with an orange asterisk (\*) are mandatory to fill in. The rest is optional. That said, we always recommend filling in as much relevant information as possible from the start.
</Tip>

## Q&A

Below you will find answers to some of the most frequently asked questions about Quick add employee.

**Question:** What is the difference between **Quick add** an employee and **Initiate hire**?

The main difference is that **Quick add** creates a profile with only the essential information needed to give someone access to Hailey, while **Initiate hire** is a structured process where you collect information securely and send and sign contracts directly in Hailey.

**Initiate hire** is meant for giving new employees the full hiring and onboarding experience including contracts, employment details, salary information, approvals and other pre- and onboarding activities.

In short: if you're hiring someone, use **Initiate hire.** If you just need to give external partners or service accounts quick access to the platform, **Quick add** is the way to go.

**Question:** I can't find the employee in the **Employee list** after doing a **Quick add**?

This is probably because of how you've filtered the list. For example, if you've added the employee without a **Start date**, they'll have the employment status **Unknown**, which may not show up in your current filter.

Check your active filters and the employee should appear. If you're unsure, just clear all filters and the employee will pop up in the list.

**Tip:** You can also **Super search** for an employee by pressing Ctrl + K on your keyboard.

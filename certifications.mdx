## What is certifications?

Certificates are uploaded documents that shows that an employee has a certain competence, education, or authorization. They can work as proof that the individual meets specific requirements for, for example, a title.

## How do I set up the approval principles?

When a certificate is added for an employee, a task will go to a verifier who needs to verify the certificate. To set up who your verifiers should be, navigate to **Settings - Security - Approval principles - Certificates.** Your options for choosing the verifier are:

- **Managers**
- **Team Leads**
- **Head of organization**
- **Head of department**
- **Specific employee(s)**

## How do you create certifications?

Navigate to **Settings - Skills - Certifications** and click on **Add new**. Give the certificate a name and description.

Toggle on if you want a **notification** to be sent out when the certification is about to expire. Choose who to notify, the **employee** to whom the certification is added on, their **manager** and/or a **Task force**. Also, set the number of days before the certification expires that the notification should be sent out. The notification is sent by email.

<Note>
  Note that a notification is only sent when the certificate is **required** from the employee.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-08-20-04-9634-AM.png?width=669&height=337&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-08-20-04-9634-AM.png)

If a certificate should be visualized as **required** on employees profile, you can choose which **Legal entities**, **Locations**, **Departments**, **Teams**, **Titles** and/or **Specific** **employee(s)** to select, click **Save.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-18-2026-08-20-31-8885-AM.png?width=670&height=335&name=image-png-Jun-18-2026-08-20-31-8885-AM.png)

All created certificates will be shown in the overview list including the scope for each certificate.

## Rights

- **View** on your own profile makes it possible to see all information under tab **Certification** on your own profile.
- **View** on other employees makes it possible to see all information under tab **Certification** on the employee profiles + access to **Skills - Certification** in the main menu for employees who is scoped.
- **View & edit** education gives you the right to view, add and delete certificates.
- **Settings - Create** education gives full access to create new and manage already added certifications under **Settings**.

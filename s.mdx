## What is salary proposal approval for a manager's manager?

Salary proposal approval is the process where a manager reviews and approves salary proposals submitted by managers who report to them. Managers can review proposed salary changes, read and add comments, and approve salary proposals before they move to the next step in the salary review process.Salary proposal approval is the process in which a manager reviews and approves salary proposals submitted by managers reporting to them. Managers can review proposed salary changes, read and add comments, and approve the proposals before they are forwarded to the final approval stage.

## How do I approve proposals as a manager's manager?

Once the salary proposals have been submitted, you as the proposing manager’s manager will receive an email prompting you to “Go to Hailey.”

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-01-2025-01-39-26-0313-PM.png?width=550&height=326&name=image-png-Jul-01-2025-01-39-26-0313-PM.png)

On your dashboard, an update card will appear under **My Updates** for approval, where you will see the employees whose salaries you need to approve.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-38-25-1750-PM.png?width=670&height=92&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-38-25-1750-PM.png)

Once inside the review, you as the manager’s manager can **go through the proposals and read any comments related to the changes** – provided that a comment has been added. Any comments on salary changes can be found under the heading “Latest Comment.” If a date appears in this column, it means a comment is available and shows when it was added.

**Salary adjustments can only be made by the proposer.** You can use the comment field to explain which adjustments you believe should be made. An email will be sent to the proposer once you have added a comment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-39-59-7960-PM.png?width=670&height=338&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-39-59-7960-PM.png)

When you are ready to approve the proposals, simply select the employee or employees whose salary proposals you want to approve, and then click **Approve salary changes** in the top-right corner.

<Note>
  If you have multiple managers reporting to you, this view displays all employees whose salary proposals you are responsible for approving. Use the filters on the left to view specific groups of employees.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-41-14-8537-PM.png?width=670&height=337&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-41-14-8537-PM.png)

When approving salary changes, a message will appear showing which employees you are approving the salary changes for, who the proposer is, the target increase, the proposed increase for the affected employees, their current salaries, and their new salaries after the adjustment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-41-47-4996-PM.png?width=670&height=489&name=image-png-Jan-07-2026-02-41-47-4996-PM.png)

After you press **Approve,** the overview view for the review will close.

You can easily return to the salary review via **My updates** under the update card _Approve salary changes_ to view the salary review overview and see which employees' salary changes have been approved. Approved salary changes will be marked with a turquoise checkmark under the "Status" column.

<Note>
  The update card will remain under **My Updates** until the salary review is complete and until the date on which salaries are updated.
</Note>

## Key Terms

Here you will find brief explanations of terms that may be helpful when reviewing and approving salary proposals.

- **Average target:** The average salary increase target for the employees included in the salary review. It is displayed as both a percentage and an amount, allowing you to compare the submitted salary proposals against the review target.
- **Proposed**: The average salary increase based on the salary proposals submitted for approval. It is displayed as both a percentage and an amount and provides an overview of the proposed increase across the employees included in the review.
- **Proposer:** The manager who submitted the salary proposal for approval. The proposer is shown in the salary review overview and can receive comments and feedback on submitted proposals.
- **Status:** Shows whether a salary proposal is awaiting approval or has already been approved. Approved salary changes are marked with a turquoise checkmark in the salary review overview.
- **Comments:** Comments can be added to salary proposals to provide feedback or suggest adjustments. Once a comment is added, the proposer receives an email notification. The Latest comment column shows the date of the most recent comment related to a salary proposal.
- **Approve salary changes:** The action used to approve salary proposals submitted by managers. Once approved, the proposals move forward in the salary review process.

FAQ

**Question:** Can I edit a salary proposal before approving it?

No. Salary adjustments can only be made by the manager who submitted the proposal. If changes are needed, you can add a comment explaining which adjustments should be made. The proposer will receive an email notification when a comment is added.

**Question:** What happens after I approve a salary proposal?

Once a salary proposal is approved, it moves forward in the salary review process and can proceed to the next approval step.

**Question**: What happens if a salary proposal is changed after I have already approved it?

If a salary proposal is changed after it has been approved, the previous approval is removed and the status changes back to **Waiting for proposal**. The updated proposal must then be reviewed and approved again before it can move forward in the salary review process. Salary proposals are not locked after approval and can still be updated by the proposing manager.

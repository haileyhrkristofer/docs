## What is the HR-hub?

The Hr-hub is a place where we gathered seven overviews of different HR-processes. These seven overviews are:

- **Workflows**
- **Tasks**
- **Hirings**
- **Signings**
- **Salary reviews**
- **Employee documents**
- **Pay surveys**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-23-30-1734-PM.png?width=669&height=339&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-01-23-30-1734-PM.png)

<Tip>
  **Tip!** In overviews with filtering options, use the filtering menu on the left to quickly find the information you need.
</Tip>

## Workflows

The overview of **Workflows** view all running workflows. Seven columns are presented:

- **Type** - the title of the workflow.
- **For** - who the workflow is running for.
- **Current steps** - the step(s) that are running right now.
- **Waiting for** - the employee(s) that the workflow is waiting for to take action.
- **Started by** - if an employee is shown, the workflow is started manually by that employee. If a gear is shown, it means that an automatic trigger started the workflow. Read more about workflows [HERE](/workflows-tasks).
- **Start date** - the start date for the workfloa.
- **Progress** - shows how far the workflow has made the progress.

![image-png-Aug-05-2026-12-56-40-5817-PM](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-05-2026-12-56-40-5817-PM.png?width=670&height=340&name=image-png-Aug-05-2026-12-56-40-5817-PM.png)

When you click on a workflow process, you get a more detailed view on each task in the workflow. Hoover over the check-icon to see who and when the task was completed.

## Tasks

The overview of **Tasks** shows all running tasks for the employees. The task overview does not view finished tasks. The columns are:

- **Task** - the title of the task.
- **Assigned to** - who has gotten the task on their dashboard.
- **Category** - what type of process the task comes from, for example a **Quick task**.
- **For employee** - the employee who the task is running for.
- **Deadline** - the deadline for the task.

Depending on your rights, you can mark some tasks as done for someone else. Click on the check box to the left and mark as done by clicking on the check-ikon in the top right corner.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-51-26-8861-AM.png?width=667&height=379&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-51-26-8861-AM.png)

## Hirings

The overview of Hirings shows all hiring processes, both running and finalized.

The columns are:

- **Being hired** - the employee being hired.
- **Type** - the type of employment, for example rehiring.
- **Current step** - current step of the process.
- **Waiting for** - the employee who has the current task in the process.
- **Started on** - the start date for the process.
- **Progress** - shows how far the hiring has made the progress.

When you click on the hiring process, you get a more detailed view on each task in the process. You can also view the contract by clicking on **View contract** in the bottom left corner.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-52-05-7550-AM.png?width=669&height=384&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-52-05-7550-AM.png)

You can also change the owner of the hiring process. Click on the **three dots - edit** and change the owner **- Save.** Read more about how the hiring process works in the guide [HERE](/iniate-hire).

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-52-40-5850-AM.png?width=669&height=379&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-52-40-5850-AM.png)

## Signings

The overview of **Signings** shows you all running signings. The overview shows following columns:

- **Employee** - the employee for whom the signing is intended. The signed document will appear in this employee's profile.
- **Template** - the title of the document being signed.
- **Reviewer** - the reviewer(s) of the document.
- **Signers** - the signing parties.
- **Send date** - the date the document was sent out.
- **Progress** - shows how far the signing has made the progress.

If you would like to delete a signing, do so by marking the **Check box** - click on the **Trash can** in the top right corner - **Yes I am sure.**

## Employee documents

The overview of employee documents shows a list of all employee documents.

You can see following columns:

- **Name** - name of the document.
- **Folder** - name of the folder the document is placed in.
- **Employee** - the employee to whom the document is placed on.
- **Upload** - the date the document was uploaded on the employee profile.
- **Disposal settings** - Yes or No. Hoover over to see more information.
- **Disposed date** - date when the document will be deleted from Hailey. <img src="https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-54-19-2045-AM.png?width=612&height=347&name=image-png-Aug-06-2026-08-54-19-2045-AM.png" alt="" />

## Rights

- **Enable** gives you get the right to either view or view and edit that overview of the HR-hub on targeted employees. Hoover over the information-icon to read more detailed information.
- **View** gives you the right to view the overview on targeted employees.
- **View & edit** gives you the right to view & edit the overview on targeted employees.

Read more about Roles & Rights in the guide [HERE](/roles-and-rights).

## Common questions

**Question**: Our managers can't see the the workflow overview of their employees, why is that?

You need to give the right to view the workflow overview to employees on others in order for the overview to be visible. Read more about Roles & rights [HERE](/roles-and-rights).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I mark one-to-ones as done for one of my managers in the task overview?">
    It is only possible to mark tasks as done for someone else if the task comes from a workflow. Other type of tasks needs to be handled by the owner of the task or the process needs to be deleted.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

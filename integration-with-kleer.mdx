Eliminate unnecessary duplicate work and free valuable time with Hailey’s integration with Kleer, where time off registration and employee creation happens automatically!

<Tip>
  First time activating one of **Hailey's standard integrations**? Start by reading [**THIS**](/haileys-standard-integrations) guide to know what to keep in mind before you get started.
</Tip>

---

## What does the integration do?

During each synchronization, **included data** is automatically updated via **API transfer** in Kleer to match the values in Hailey, as **Hailey serves as master.**

<Note>
  **Do you also want to sync salary and employee data?** - See our guide [**HERE**](/integration-with-kleer-salary-and-employee-data) for integration with Kleer – Salary and employee data.
</Note>

## How does the integration work?

With the API transfer, a synchronization between Hailey and Kleer takes place **once an hour based on changes.** This means that:

- If any updates are made to an employees included data in Hailey, the information will be automatically updated in Kleer.
- When an employee’s time off is approved in Hailey, the time off will be updated in Kleer.

## What data is included?

Employee data:

| **HAILEY** | **KLEER** |
| --- | --- |
| Company email | Email |
| First name, Last name | Name |

Time off synchronization:

| **HAILEY** | **KLEER** |
| --- | --- |
| Time off | Time off |

What is required to be included in the integration?

For an employee to be included in the integration, the following is required:

- Company email

If an employee in Hailey has a company email that **does not yet exist in Kleer**, a new user will be **created** in Kleer. Therefore, it is important that all new employees are always created in Hailey first.

Make sure that the **company email** for your existing employees **is identical in both Hailey and Kleer** and **matches their existing profile in Kleer** before the integration is activated.

<Note>
  If an employee’s **company email is changed in Hailey,** the integration will **create a new profile in Kleer** with the updated email.
</Note>

For an employee to be included in the **time off** sync, the following information must be available on their profile in Hailey:

- Company email
- First and last name
- Swedish personal identity number

Employees with the following **employment statuses** are included in the integration:

- Unknown
- Will join
- Being hired
- In service
- Leaving
- Has left

**Important notes** regarding **time off** sync **:**

- If an employee reports **time off in percentage (%)**, they must have **working hours** specified in their profile in Hailey. This allows the system to understand the extent of the time off.
- If an employee reports **time off in hours,** working hours are **not** required in their profile.
- Time off for **vacation** must always be reported as a **full day**, as Kleer does not allow vacation time off that is shorter than a full working day.

**Follow the instructions below to complete the activation of the integration.**

Before activating the integration, it is important that you **contact your contact person at Kleer**, who can provide the **required API key** for the setup. The person setting up the integration between Hailey and Kleer must also have access to an **administrator** **account** **in both systems.**

To activate the integration in Hailey:

- Go to **Settings.**
- Click **Integrations** at the bottom of the menu bar.
- Select the **Payroll** section.
- Click on **Kleer** and then **Add integration.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-08-30-11-4965-AM.png?width=670&height=312&name=image-png-May-04-2026-08-30-11-4965-AM.png)

Paste the **company number** and the **access token** that you **received from Kleer.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-08-30-53-6204-AM.png?width=670&height=297&name=image-png-May-04-2026-08-30-53-6204-AM.png)

When activating the time off sync, you must **select a start date for when you want time off data to be transferred from Hailey to Kleer.**

Ensure the following before activation:

- **To synchronize time off** you need to **map** out your **Time off reasons** in Hailey to the corresponding reason in Kleer. You find your time off reasons in Kleer from Payroll - Monthly Reports.
- If you add new activities in Kleer, the activity must **have a unique name** in addition to the standard activities for the integration to work.
- If an activity **doesn't exist in Kleer** or is spelled wrong the admin at your company will receive an email with information that the time off synchronization has been **inactivated**.
- The activities from Kleer must be filled out in **Swedish** in the configuration of the integration.
- If an activity doesn't exist in Kleer or is spelled wrong the admin at your company will receive an email with information that the time off synchronization has been inactivated.
- The time off reason **vacation** must always be reported for a **full day** since Kleer doesn't allow vacation to be reported shorter than a working day.
- Once the time off has been **attested**/**approved in Kleer,** it can only be corrected there.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-08-31-31-8154-AM.png?width=670&height=583&name=image-png-May-04-2026-08-31-31-8154-AM.png)

When all steps of the integration setup have been completed, click **Save**.

- _Does the integration not apply to the entire company? -_ Read the next step in the guide about scope **before** **you click save.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-04-2026-08-32-57-0136-AM.png?width=670&height=1072&name=image-png-May-04-2026-08-32-57-0136-AM.png)

Now that the integration is enabled, Hailey will automatically read in all users from your Kleer account who meet the requirements for the integration.You can see which/how many integrations your company has active under the Active tab in Integrations.

## Does the integration not apply to the entire company?

Then it is possible to add a **scope** for the integration!

- Setting a scope means you can choose to include only **employees assigned to a specific workplace and/or legal entity in the integration.**
- This must be configured when setting up the integration. It is **important** to do this at the initial setup, **as the synchronization starts immediately.** This ensures that only the correct employees are synced from Hailey to Kleer.

If these fields are left empty, all employees with a company email address will be included in the integration.

### If you want to set up multiple integrations with different Kleer accounts.

- If your company has multiple workplaces and/or legal entities in Hailey, it may be beneficial to create separate integrations with Kleer for different/multiple Kleer accounts.

Repeat the same process described above. In other words, navigate to the **Payroll** section and follow the same steps as when you configured the first integration.

You can view which and how many integrations your company has active under the **Active** tab in the Integrations section.

## Common questions

**Question**: What do I need to have in mind if I use time off report in procentage in Hailey?

If an employee reports time off in percentage, they must have **working hours** specified in their profile in Hailey. This allows the system to understand the extent of the time off.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can I double-check if an employee’s time off is not syncing correctly?">
    Make sure that the activities in Kleer match the time off reasons in Hailey and that there are no duplicate activities with the same name in Kleer. Also, double-check that the employee’s time off has been approved in Hailey.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An employee cannot report time off in Kleer, what should I double-check?">
    Check that the employee has a valid employment contract registered in Kleer. A valid employment contract is required for the employee to be able to report time off.

    _If you have any questions regarding the integration, please contact Hailey support._
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

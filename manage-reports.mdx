In this guide, you will learn how to manage reports in Hailey. We will go through how to filter and save views, share reports with colleagues in your organization, and export reports when needed.

## What are reports in Hailey?

Reports are a feature in Hailey that help you compile, analyze, and export data from the platform. Hailey includes a range of predefined reports covering areas such as employee data changes, employment data, payroll, time, absence, and audit logs. Reports provide a clear overview of your information, making it easy to track changes, share relevant data with the right people, or export it for use in other systems. This helps you work more efficiently, save time, and ensure that the right information is available whenever you need it.

<Note>
  Looking for a guide to a specific report in Hailey? Click [**HERE**](/reports) to find all our guides for individual reports!
</Note>

## Share reports

If you would like employees to access specific reports for particular areas of the company, you can choose to **share reports**.

All reports have a **Share button** in the top-right corner. Click the button and name the shared report. Select **who you want to share the report with**, as well as **who should be included** in the report based on organizational belonging. You can define this scope based on _legal entity, workplace, department, cost center, team, job title, and reports to._

If you enter values in multiple fields, **the report will include employees who meet all specified criteria**. This allows you, for example, to create a report that only includes employees who belong to the Sweden legal entity and whose workplace is Stockholm.

All reports that you have shared with others in the organization can be found under **Reports and the Shared tab.**

<Tip>
  Please note that a shared report **does not** take into account the rights of the employee with whom the report is shared. This means that the person receiving the shared report may be able to view information that they normally would not have access to on the employee card.

  **Example:** Employee A does not have the right to view the salaries of any of their colleagues according to their access rights. Employee A receives the _Joiners_ report shared with them. Since this report contains salary data, the employee can now see this information in the report.
</Tip>

## How do I filter and save views?

To the left of the reports, there are filters to sort by group or specific change. Use the calendar selector at the top right to choose a specific period to view. In the report **f**ilters can be saved by clicking "Save" at the bottom of the filter menu. You can find the saved view under **Views** in the filter menu.

## Export

A report can be transfered to relevant systems through file transfers or manually. Export a CSV file from Hailey using the Export button located at the top right corner, or work with the information directly in Hailey. The exported data will match what you filtered using the calendar selector and the filters on the left. If you need to reformat the CSV file to Excel, you can find instructions on how to do this [**HERE**](/export-data-from-hailey#manage_csv_file).

## Rights

- **View** enables users to view, share, and export all reports available under the **Reports** tab in the main menu. **Note:** This right allows users to view all changes made to employee profiles, including salary data, regardless of any other right assigned to the role.

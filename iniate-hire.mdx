## What is initiate hire?

Initiate hire helps you gather and structure all the information needed when a new employee is starting. The process ensures a smooth and consistent experience for both the person hiring and the new employee.

<Tip>
  Would you like to know how to add an employment for an existing employee, or how to rehire a employee?

  - Read more about adding an employment for an existing employee [**HERE**](/employment-wizard).
  - Read more about rehiring an employee [**HERE**](/rehiring-wizard).
</Tip>

## Before you get started

Before initiating hire in Hailey, it may be beneficial to ensure that certain functionalities are already set up. We recommend reviewing the following features before proceeding with the hiring process:

- **Contract Templates:** Read more [HERE](/create-contract-templates).
- **Employee Folders:** Read more [HERE](/employee-folders).
- **Hiring Templates:** Read more [HERE](/hiring-templates).
- **Employee Templates** (optional)**:** Read more [HERE](/employee-templates).

## How do you initiate a hire?

The first step in the process when you initiate an employment in Hailey is to enter the candidate’s information and organizational information. After that, the candidate will (with the help of the hiring template) provide all the necessary information required to create the contract.

### Step 1 – Initiate hiring

Start by:

- Go to the Employee tab from the menu on the left - click on **Add employee**
- Select **Initiate hire**

or,

- Go to your home page in Hailey.
- Click on **Initiate hire**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-14-44-7877-AM.png?width=670&height=407&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-14-44-7877-AM.png)

Choose whether you want to initiate a Regular or a Casual employment.

<Note>
  Would you like to learn more about Casual employments in Hailey? Click [**HERE**](/casual-employments).
</Note>

You will now be guided through the hiring wizard. In the first step, you choose whether you want to create the hiring using an **Employee Template** or continue without one.

![Employeetemplate](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Employeetemplate.png?width=665&height=431&name=Employeetemplate.png)

If you have created **Hiring templates** in Hailey, you will be able to choose which template you want to use.

- The hiring template will be used to collect the necessary information from the candidate during the hiring process.

![hiring template](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/hiring%20template.png?width=670&height=444&name=hiring%20template.png)

Fill out the basic information about the candidate.

<Tip>
  Note that you need to have the option **Do you need personal identification?** activated if you want to **sign with BankID.**
</Tip>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-29-50-2912-PM.png?width=670&height=470&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-29-50-2912-PM.png)

Then fill in the information about where the candicate will be working. Once all information has been completed, click the turquoise **Arrow**.

- After clicking the turquoise **Arrow**, the employee’s profile will be created in Hailey, and an **email** will be sent to the candidate informing them that you have requested their information in order to complete the contract.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-30-47-0296-PM.png?width=670&height=661&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-30-47-0296-PM.png)

#### Step 2 – Gather information from the candidate

In the email, the candidate is asked to provide their personal information and approve that you have the right to use the information to generate the hiring contract.

<Note>
  Would you like to see more of what the hiring template looks like from a candidate’s perspective? Watch the video at the top of this guide or click **[/hiring-templates](/hiring-templates)HERE.**
</Note>

How do you create and send a contract for signing?

#### Step 1 – Create the contract

Once the candidate has submitted their information, you will receive a task in your **My tasks** list to create the contract.

- Click the task to be guided through the contract creation process.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-32-39-3456-PM.png?width=670&height=94&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-32-39-3456-PM.png)

Select one of your created contract templates from the drop-down list.

- If the template contains optional sections or attachments, you can easily choose which ones to include in the contract by selecting the relevant sections.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-33-51-5588-PM.png?width=670&height=659&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-33-51-5588-PM.png)

If the contract template is of the **Manual upload for each signing** type, you can upload a PDF document by clicking **Click or drop a file here to upload**.

<Note>
  When using a manually uploaded contract, the salary must be created separately outside the hiringwizard.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-36-58-2464-AM.png?width=670&height=431&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-36-58-2464-AM.png)

Choose your signing method: **Digital autograph** or **BankID**.

In this step, you can also select who will review and sign the contract, and in which order by setting up your signing steps.

- If you select a reviewer, the contract will be sent to the reviewer for approval before it is sent out for signature.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-34-42-7414-PM.png?width=670&height=526&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-34-42-7414-PM.png)

Fill out in the employee’s **employment terms** and any **custom employment fields** (optional).

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-40-25-7809-AM.png?width=670&height=425&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-40-25-7809-AM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-41-45-2119-AM.png?width=670&height=555&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-41-45-2119-AM.png)

Choose whether the candidate should report **Time** and **Time off**. If so, complete the relevant **Time** and **Time off** sections.

- This step is optional and is only displayed if your organization has enabled **Time** and **Time off** settings in Hailey.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-35-57-4139-PM.png?width=670&height=457&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-35-57-4139-PM.png)

If the contract template contains salary variables, you will create and enter the candidate’s salary in this step.

- The new salary will then be registered on the candidate’s profile.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-37-01-6173-PM.png?width=670&height=428&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-37-01-6173-PM.png)

In this step, you can see the information sent in by the candidate via the candidate portal, and adjust if needed.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-37-41-1609-PM.png?width=670&height=589&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-37-41-1609-PM.png)

Before you send the contract to potential reviewers or signing parties, you get a summary of the contract’s content. Here you can also preview the completed contract by clicking on **Preview Contract.**

- Click on the **Arrow** when you want to send the contract.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-51-03-3966-AM.png?width=670&height=498&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-51-03-3966-AM.png)

#### Step 2 – Review and sign the contract

If you have selected one or more reviewers, they will now receive a task to review the contract. Once the contract has been reviewed and approved, it will be sent to the relevant parties for signing according to the signing order.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-56-40-5337-AM.png?width=670&height=406&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-11-56-40-5337-AM.png)

The candidate will receive the contract by email and can sign it by clicking the link in the email.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-23-21-4365-PM.png?width=670&height=452&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-23-21-4365-PM.png)

the company's signer will receive a task and an email notifying them that the contract is ready for signing.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-20-46-0268-PM.png?width=670&height=123&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-20-46-0268-PM.png)

Once the contract has been signed by the relevant parties, it will automatically be added to the employee’s profile under the **Docs** tab.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-41-53-4795-PM.png?width=670&height=316&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-41-53-4795-PM.png)

#### Step 3 - Finalize hiring

Once all parties have signed the contract, you will receive a task to **Finalize hiring.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-42-45-9624-PM.png?width=670&height=136&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-42-45-9624-PM.png)

In this step, you can choose to start a manual **Workflow** for the new employee, which is optional.

Finally, you can choose to send a **Preboarding portal** from the hiring template to the employee to collect any remaining information needed before the start of the employment.

- Click the turquoise **Checkmark** to finalize the hiring.

## Where can you follow the hiring process in Hailey?

If you want to, you can follow the entire hiring process. You can do this either through **My updates** on the home page in Hailey, or via a **hiring overview** in Hailey that collects ongoing and completed hiring flows.

If you want to view the **hiring overview** and its upcoming steps, you can find it in the main menu under:

- **HR Hub - Hirings**

Here you can follow the entire hiring process - from start to finish.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-50-56-3205-PM.png?width=670&height=311&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-50-56-3205-PM.png)

If you click on a hiring process in the list, you will get an overview of all the steps included in that specific hiring.

- For example, you can see whether the candidate has submitted their information for the contract, whether the contract has been created, and whether the final step of the hiring process has been completed.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-53-40-7493-PM.png?width=670&height=528&name=image-png-Jun-30-2026-12-53-40-7493-PM.png)

In the overview view, you as the person who started the hiring process, or as an Admin, can also **change the owner of the hiring process.**

- You do this by clicking the three dots next to the current workflow – Edit – and then selecting a new owner from the dropdown list.

<Note>
  To be selected as the new owner of an hiring process, the person must either have right to initiate the same type of hiring as that the process relates to, or be an Admin.
</Note>

When ownership is transferred, the new owner takes over the current steps and tasks in the hiring process.

- The new owner will see the hiring process as a card under My updates on their homepage.
- The hiring process and the tasks connected to the process will be removed from the previous owner’s homepage.

## Key terms

- **Optional sections:** The sections available in your contract templates that you can choose to include in the contract.
- **Reviewer:** The person responsible for reviewing the contract before it is sent to the relevant parties.
- **Company signatory:** The person representing the company in the signing process.
- **Signing step:** The order in which the contract is sent for signing, for example first to the candidate and then to the manager.

## Rights

**Initiate hire**

As an admin in Hailey, you always have the right to initiate hire. If you want someone else, for example managers, to have the right to initiate hire, you can set this under **Actions** and grant the right to **Initiate hire**.

If you want to give right to **Initiate hire with employee template**, you can grant it from the same place.

**Signing**

Being able to act as a **Signing party for the company** is a right that must be set up, both for admins and other employees. Click **Other rights** and grant the right **Can be company signing party.**

<Note>
  [**HERE**](/roles-and-rights) you can read more about Roles & rights.
</Note>

## Common questions

#### Hiring process

**Question:** I want to delete/cancel the hiring process. How do I do that?

If a hiring process has been started but not completed, the employee will receive the status **Being hired** in Hailey. To remove this status, you first need to cancel the hiring process and then, if needed, delete the overall process.

To remove the **Being hired** status, you need to:

- Go to the employee's profile.
- Click **Action - Cancel hiring process**.

Once the hiring process has been cancelled, the **Being hired** status will be removed. The employee will instead receive another employment status, such as Will join, In service, Leaving, or Garden leave. Which status is displayed depends on the start and end dates registered on the employee's employment.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-54-50-3827-PM.png?width=609&height=410&name=image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-54-50-3827-PM.png)

If you also want to delete the hiring process, such as the contract signing or other steps in the hiring flow, do the following:

- Go to **HR Hub - Hiring process**.
- Click the three dots to the right of the relevant process.
- Select **Delete**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-55-28-0018-PM.png?width=651&height=163&name=image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-55-28-0018-PM.png)

If the employee should no longer remain in the system, you can permanently delete them by going to the employee's profile. Click **Action - Delete employee**. Please note that if you delete the employee, they will be permanently removed from Hailey and the information cannot be restored.

<Note>
  If you do not select a hiring template, Hailey’s default hiring template will be used. [**HERE**](/hiring-templates) you can read more about what the default template includes.
</Note>

**Question:** Can I resend the Candidate portal if the candidate cannot find the original email?

Yes! You can do this by going to the Homepage and opening the ongoing hiring process under **My updates**. Then copy the Candidate Portal link and send it manually to the candidate.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-38-45-5041-PM.png?width=670&height=422&name=image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-38-45-5041-PM.png)

**Question:** The candidate cannot find the Preboarding Portal in their email inbox. Can I resend it?

Yes, If you want to resend the Preboarding Portal to the employee, you can copy the link found under the employee’s name on the employee profil and send it manually.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-41-01-9341-PM.png?width=670&height=279&name=image-png-Jul-03-2026-12-41-01-9341-PM.png)

**Question:** Can I change the owner of a hiring process?

Yes! Go to **HR Hub - Hiring processes**. As the person who initiated the hiring or as an admin, you can change the owner of the process by clicking the three dots next to the relevant process and selecting a new owner from the dropdown list.

To be eligible as a new owner, the person must either have the right to initiate the same type of hiring as the process concerns, or be an admin.

**Example**: If a hiring has been initiated using an employee template, the owner can only be changed to someone who has right to initiate hirings using the same template.

### Send contract

**Question:** How do I know which fields are mandatory for the contract?

A general rule; any fields marked with a red asterisk, as well as any variables included in the contract template, are mandatory to fill in before the contract can be sent.

**Question:** I receive an error message when sending the contract. What could be the reason?

This usually happens because required information is missing or the contract.

Start by checking that all mandatory fields are completed. Contract templates contain merge codes that gathers information from the hiring and employee data. If any of this information is missing or incorrect, the contract cannot be sent.

Then review the contract template structure and ensure all variables are correctly configured. If you do not have right to edit contract templates, an admin can help review the setup.

**Question:** Can I recreate the contract?

Yes! Go to the Homepage in Hailey, click the hiring process under **My updates**, select **Recreate contract**, and follow the steps. Note that you will need to fill in the fields again if you recreate the contract.

### Signing

**Question:** Which countries support BankID signing?

BankID signing is only supported in Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland in Hailey.

**Question:** What happens if a reviewer rejects the contract?

If the reviewer rejects the contract, they can add a comment explaining the reason. A task will then be sent to the person who initiated the hiring, who can either cancel the signing or send out a new, updated contract.

**Question:** Can I resend the contract?

Yes, as the person who initiated the hiring, you can resend the contract if needed. This is done via **HR Hub - Signings**. Click the relevant signing and choose the recipient to resend the contract to.

**Question:** I can’t see the signed contract on the employee’s profile. Why?

Document folders are right-based. If you cannot see the document, it may be because you do not have access to that specific folder.

Check with your admin to ensure you have the correct rights. If you are an admin, make sure you are included in the correct access categories for the folder.

[**HERE**](/employee-folders) you can read more about employee folders.

### Rights

**Question:** Why can’t I see HR Hub?

Access to HR Hub and the hiring overview is controlled via Roles & rights. If you lack right to view the overview but can initiate hirings, you will only see the hirings you have initiated.

**Question:** Why can’t I select a contract reviewer?

The ability to act as a contract reviewer must be enabled under Roles & rights - Other rights - Can be contract reviewer.

**Question:** Why can’t I select a company signing party?

The ability to act as a company signatory must be enabled under Roles & rights - Other rights - Can be company signing party.

title: "Integration with Kivra"
---

With Hailey’s integration to Kivra, the signed employment contract is automatically sent both to Hailey and the new employee’s private Kivra inbox. Smooth, secure, and appreciated – by both HR and the employee.

<Tip>
  First time activating one of **Hailey's standard integrations**? Start by reading [**THIS**](/haileys-standard-integrations) guide to know what to keep in mind before you get started.
</Tip>

## What does the integration do?

The integration automatically sends signed employment agreements to Kivra for newly hired employees who are existing Kivra users **.** When an agreement is signed in Hailey's employment workflow, the employee's personal identity number is matched against Kivra's user registry. If the employee is registered with Kivra, the agreement is delivered both to the employee's profile in Hailey and to their private Kivra inbox.

When an employment agreement is sent to Kivra through the integration, Hailey is displayed as the sender, not the employer.

## How does the integration work?

Every time you sign an employment agreement through the **initiate hire** process in Hailey, the agreement will automatically be sent to the new employee’s Kivra inbox.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/Sk%C3%A4rmbild%202025-05-23%20103237-png.png?width=505&height=494&name=Sk%C3%A4rmbild%202025-05-23%20103237-png.png)

## What data is included in the integration?

Only agreements signed as part of the hiring process for a new employee through Hailey's employment workflow **(Initiate hire)** will be sent to Kivra.

What are the requirements to be included in the integration?

To be included in the integration, the following is required:

- A personal identity number entered as a 12-digit number in the following format: **YYYYMMDD-XXXX**.

## How do you activate the integration?

Go to **Settings** - **Integrations** and click on **Kivra** - **Add integration**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-23-2025-08-34-41-5460-AM.png?width=508&height=309&name=image-png-May-23-2025-08-34-41-5460-AM.png)

Click the **Active** button **,** click **Save,** and you're all set!

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-23-2025-08-35-19-2675-AM.png?width=302&height=352&name=image-png-May-23-2025-08-35-19-2675-AM.png)

## Common questions

**Question 1:** What happens if the employee does not have a personal identity number registered in Kivra?

The signed agreement will only be sent to the employee via email and stored in the employee’s designated document folder in Hailey.

**Question 2:** Why didn’t the employee receive their agreement in Kivra?

Only agreements that are sent and signed through the **Initiate Hire** process in Hailey are being sent to Kivra.

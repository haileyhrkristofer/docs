## What is a job ad?

A job ad in Hailey’s recruitment module is a publicly visible ad where you present an open position. It includes information about the role, the company, and how to apply.

The job ad is linked to a recruitment and is published on your Career Page as well as selected external channels.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-33-04-6956-AM.png?width=670&height=424&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-33-04-6956-AM.png)

## Before you start

**Before creating a Job Ad**, you need to set up a **Career Page** and have an active **Recruitment** in place.

If you have not completed these steps yet, follow the instructions in the following guides:

- **Create a Career Page – [HERE](/career-page)**
- **Start a Recruitment – [HERE](/start-and-manage-recruitment)**

Once these steps above are completed, you can create your **Job Ad.**

## Create job ad

To create a job ad, start by **navigating to the recruitment you want to link the ad to**. Open the recruitment and then click **Actions - Create job ad** in the top right corner.

<Note>
  When you've created a job ad for your recruitment, the button **Create job ad** will change to **Edit job ad** to make it possible for you to come back to make changes.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-35-37-0372-AM.png?width=670&height=400&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-35-37-0372-AM.png)

Start of by setting up the outline of the job ad. **Hailey will pick your standard logotype along with your standard cover image.** You can easily change both by clicking on each and select another file. Additional to this: **set a title for your job ad and any subtitle wanted**.

<Note>
  For logotypes and cover images to be selectable, they must first be uploaded under **Organization - Branding**. Read more about that **[HERE](/admin-get-started#Branding).**
</Note>

The next step is to **choose whether the department, location and possibly salary should be displayed** in the job ad by toggling each option. The department, location and salary displayed are those selected when the recruitment was created.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-39-26-8901-AM.png?width=670&height=429&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-39-26-8901-AM.png)

Now let's get into the fun stuff - writing the **job description!** Hailey's job ad offers a rich text editor, meaning that you can use e.g. bold headings, bold text and bullet points to **structure the outcome of your job description**.

In addition to this, one can also **use AI to help generate the job description**. To do this, you need to enable AI in Recruitment. For instructions on how to do this, click **[HERE](/ai).**

In this part of the setup, you can also choose the **contact person** from your company. Hailey automatically selects an admin, but you can easily change this by clicking the name field and selecting an employee from the dropdown menu.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-47-06-2248-AM.png?width=670&height=425&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-47-06-2248-AM.png)

## Application form

The next part of your job ad is deciding on **how you want the candidates to apply**. The application form is edited by selecting **Application** in the menu to the left in the job ad settings. Hailey has **four standard information requirements**:

- First name
- Last name
- Private email
- Private phone

In addition, there are **custom application fields**, where **CV** and **Cover letter** are set as standard. You choose whether the custom application fields should be mandatory by toggling on/off **required**.

The order of the custom application fields can be **adjusted using the arrows**. They can also be deleted by using the trash bin icon.

<Tip>
  It is possible to rename the already created custom application fields if desired.
</Tip>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-53-35-5659-AM.png?width=670&height=368&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-09-53-35-5659-AM.png)

If you want to **add additional custom application fields** to your application, click the **plus sign** and choose the field type you want to use. You also need to decide whether these fields should be mandatory or not. The available field types are:

- Text field
- PDF upload area
- Yes/No
- Short text (maximum of 80 characters)
- Decimal
- Selection
- Multiple selection

The field types determine how candidates can respond to the form, ensuring that the information is collected in the format you need.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-02-03-01-4038-PM.png?width=670&height=404&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-02-03-01-4038-PM.png)

## Publish the job ad

Now it's time to publish your job ad!

As mentioned earlier, a career page must exist before you can publish a job ad. Click **HERE** for instructions on how to create your career page(s).

To publish the job ad, click **Publish** in the right-hand side menu. Here, you can choose which career page the job ad should be published on and specify the dates during which it should be visible.

If you leave the publication dates blank, the job ad will be published immediately and will remain visible until it is unpublished manually.

<Note>
  Note that when you publish a job ad, it will be visible on your public career page.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-02-11-35-3121-PM.png?width=670&height=373&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-02-11-35-3121-PM.png)

Once the job ad is published, it will appear on your career page. From there, you can easily share the job ad to LinkedIn, via email, or copy the link to the ad.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-13-2026-02-12-48-1420-PM.png?width=670&height=400&name=image-png-Aug-13-2026-02-12-48-1420-PM.png)

## Unpublish the job ad

To unpublish the job ad, go to **Recruitment** in the main menu - **Job ads** - click the relevant **job ad** - **Unpublish** - **Yes, I'm sure**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-11-2026-07-08-07-1477-AM.png?width=670&height=387&name=image-png-Aug-11-2026-07-08-07-1477-AM.png)

## Rights

As an Admin in Hailey, you automatically have the access to **Recruitments** right, including to **Create Job Ads.** Read more about **Roles & right** in [**THIS**](/roles-and-rights) guide.

- **None** - The Recruitment section is **hidden** from the main menu.
- **View** - Allows the user to **view** all content within the **Recruitment section** in the main menu, including **Career Pages, Recruitments,** and **Job ads.**
- **View & Edit** - Gives the user **full access** to manage the **Recruitment module**, including the ability to create, edit, and delete **Recruitments, Career Pages, and Job Ads.**

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I have set an **To-date** for my published job ad, but I would now like to extend it so that the ad is published on my career page for a longer period. How is this done?">
    To change the **To-date** of your published job ad, you first need to **unpublish** the ad and then **publish it again** with the new dates. Unpublishing the job ad will **not** remove candidates who have already applied for the position. This means that the information for each candidate will remain in the recruitment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add screening questions to my application form?">
    You add screening questions in your job ad by adding one or several **custom application fields**.

    If you use the types **Yes/No**, **Selection**, or **Multiple Selection**, you’ll be able to **filter and sort candidates** later in the recruitment based on their responses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I have finished advertising my job posting. Do I need to actively remove the job ad?">
    Yes. To stop receiving applications for your job ad, you need to **unpublish** the ad. This will end the advertisement of your job ads on your careers page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to have a careers page in order to publish my job posting?">
    Yes. In order to publish the job posting you have created, there must be a careers page where the posting can be published. You can read more about how to create your careers page[**HERE.**](/career-page)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

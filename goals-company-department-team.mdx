## What are organizational goals?

Organizational Goals help align efforts across the company by creating clear objectives for companies, departments, and teams. By tracking progress through measurable key results, it becomes easier to follow development, stay focused on priorities, and work towards shared outcomes.

Department and team goals can also be linked to Company goals, creating a clear connection between local initiatives and overall organizational objectives.

## How do you add organizational goals?

If you have this right to add a Company, Department or Team goal you can find the goals overview by navigating to **Performance - Goals** in the main menu. A goal is created by clicking **Add new**.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/01.png)

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/02.png)

A wizard will now guide you through the process. Keep reading this guide to get an overview of each step!

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/03.png)

In the first step, you decide what type of goal to add. Is it a **Company goal**, **Department goal** or **Team goal**? When you have made the decision, click on the right arrow.

<Tip>
  **For Company goals:** if you have more than one legal entity, you get the option to select which legal entity the goal should belong to.
</Tip>

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/04.png)

Then, you set the title of the goal. When complete, click on the right arrow.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/05.png)

The next step is to set potential **key results** for the goal. At this point, you also decide how to measure the progress; in percentage or not done/done. You also get the option to set deadlines for each key result. When done, click on the right arrow.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/06.png)

The final step in the setup is deciding a deadline for the goal. This is optional. When done, click on the right arrow.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/07.png)

If the goal should improve the progress of another goal at your company, then now you can connect them.

<Note>
  Only **Department-** and **Team goals**, as well as **Employee** goals are able to be connected to a **Parent goal.**
</Note>

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/08.png)

You will now see a summary of the setup on your goal. If you are satisfied, click on the check mark to launch the goal.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/09.png)

## How do you follow up on goals?

All of your Company, Department or Team goals are visible in the Performance tab, **for everyone who has the right to view the goals**. In this view you can also see employee goals, if you have the rights to do so. Therefore, you are able to filter on Company, Department, Team, Employee and/or Goal type.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/10.png)

It is also possible to use Organization filters. If you filter by department, you will see both the department's goals and the employees who have goals that belong to the department.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/11.png)

With the right to edit goals, you can do follow ups from this view by clicking on the Goal - **Follow up.**

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/12.png)

In this view you are able to **change status and deadline** for the goal. Along with this, you can also **write a comment** and **adjust the progress** on key results. Click Save when you are done.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/13.png)

The updates will now show in the overview and when clicking on the Goal you will also see the **follow up**.

<Note>
  You can delete the **latest** follow up on a company, department or team goal. Deleting a follow up will remove both the comment and the progress entered, reverting to the state before the follow up.
</Note>

When you want to complete a goal, you do so through a new follow up where you adjust the status and the progress on the key results. When clicking on Save, your goal will be completed.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/14.png)

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/15.png)

## Edit goal

If needed, you can edit your company, department or team goals. This is done via the Goals tab and by clicking on the three dots on the right of the goal.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/16.png)

Here you can change the title and and the deadline of the goal. Additional to this, you can also adjust your key results and parent goal, as well as add new ones.

![](/images/goals-company-department-team/17.png)

## Rights

As an Admin in Hailey, you automatically have full access to manage all goals within the organisation. Read more about Roles & Rights in the guide [HERE.](/roles-and-rights)

- Rights for goals can be set separately for **All company goals**, **All department goals** and **All team goals**.
- **None -** Goals are hidden from the user.
- **View -** Allows the user to view goals at the selected level (company goals, department goals or team goals).
- **View & Edit -** Allows the user to view, create, edit and follow up on goals at the selected level (company goals, department goals or team goals).

<Warning>
  Please note that the **View** or **View & Edit** rights refer **to all the goals** set for that specific part of the organisation.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **View & Edit** is required to add and follow up Company/Department/Team goals.
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do my rights affect which goals I can follow up on?">
  Yes, you can only view and follow up on the goals that you have the rights to access, based on your administrator's settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can all employees see team and department goals?">
  No, the goals that are visible depend on the user's rights and which goals the organization has chosen to make available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I see the company goals?">
  This is usually due to your rights. Contact your administrator if you believe you should have access to the company goal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

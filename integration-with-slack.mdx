title: "Integration with Slack"
---

Get updates in Slack for you organization and enable your employees to have their tasks in Slack.

<Tip>
  First time activating one of **Hailey's standard integrations**? Start by reading [**THIS**](/haileys-standard-integrations) guide to know what to keep in mind before you get started.
</Tip>

## Why integrate Hailey with Slack?

This integration allows your employees to both receive and view tasks, report sick leave, see who is absent and get contact info. By connecting Hailey and Slack you can provide your employees an easy way of administrating and staying up to date.

## Slack App: Admin set up

Let's start in Hailey. Go to **Settings** - **Integrations** and choose Slack. Click **Connect to Slack**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-08-2026-03-50-13-3012-PM.png?width=670&height=352&name=image-png-May-08-2026-03-50-13-3012-PM.png)

You will be sent to this page where you have to choose which workspace in Slack you want to connect. Then click **Allow**!

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-27-2023-01-23-47-7152-PM.png?width=345&height=332&name=image-png-Nov-27-2023-01-23-47-7152-PM.png)

If you want your employees to be able to report sick leave in Hailey through Slack, this needs to be set up in Hailey. This is done from **Settings** - **Integrations** - **Slack**. If you have multiple reasons for sick leave, you will need to set which one can be reported from Slack.

Once this is done, the setup between Hailey and Slack is completed!

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-27-2023-01-16-57-3589-PM.png?width=636&height=340&name=image-png-Nov-27-2023-01-16-57-3589-PM.png)

<Note>
  💡 If you want to use the Slack-bot, you also have to activate the **Bot**. You do this from **Settings** - **Security** - **AI**, in Hailey. Once you have activated the Bot, the employees will be able to start interacting with the Bot!
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-08-2026-03-51-07-2579-PM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-May-08-2026-03-51-07-2579-PM.png)

## Slack App: Employee set up

Now that the company accounts are connected, each employee can log into Slack and connect the Slack-bot for themselves. They will now find Hailey HR under **Apps**.

<Note>
  Note that the app that supports the latest version of the integration is the one with the black Hailey HR logotype.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-08-2023-09-52-01-0801-AM.png?width=395&height=134&name=image-png-Dec-08-2023-09-52-01-0801-AM.png)

To connect their Hailey account to the Slack-bot, open the app and type **connect**. Once they get the answer, they should click the Connect button that appears.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Nov-27-2023-01-30-17-9573-PM.png?width=576&height=231&name=image-png-Nov-27-2023-01-30-17-9573-PM.png)

This will redirect you to Hailey. If you're not already logged into Hailey, you will need to log in in order to connect Hailey to Slack.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-May-05-2026-09-13-13-8875-AM.png?width=670&height=353&name=image-png-May-05-2026-09-13-13-8875-AM.png)

Congratulations, the Hailey Slack App is now activated!

## The integration is live - now what?

The employee is now connected and will receive their tasks in Slack and can start asking the **Slack-bot** to help them in the following matters:

- Reporting themselves sick today (regarded that this is set up)
- Getting ones tasks
- Providing contact details
- Inform about who is absent today

All tasks will be shown in Slack when the bot is asked.

Regardless if the bot is active or not, the employee will receive tasks automatically to Slack if the integration is active. The tasks that will be sent out **automatically** to Slack relate to:

- Salaries
- One-to-ones
- Custom workflows
- Signing of contract
- 360° review

<Note>
  💡 Note that your settings under Roles and Rights in Hailey also apply in the Slack-bot. This means that the chatbot will never give out people data that your employees do not have the right to see in Hailey.
</Note>

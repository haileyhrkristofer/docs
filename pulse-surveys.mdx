## What are pulse surveys?

Pulse surveys in Hailey help you regularly gather feedback about employees' workplace experience and well-being. The results provide valuable insights that support a healthy work environment, strong leadership, and engaged employees.

Hailey's pulse surveys supports the systematic measurement of several key factors, such as work environment, leadership, workload, and motivation. The question framework was developed in collaboration with HR strategist and researcher Patrik Reman, and is based on research in HR analytics, employee engagement, and organizational development.

By using the included set of questions, you can:

- **Identify early signs of workplace issues** before they become bigger challenges.
- **Track progress over time** and measure the impact of improvement initiatives.
- **Make informed decisions** based on employee feedback and data.
- **Support continuous improvement** in leadership, communication, and employee well-being.

The questions are designed to enable meaningful comparisons over time and provide insights at both team and organizational levels.

## How do you create a pulse survey?

To create your pulse survey, start by navigating to **Performance** in the main menu and click on **Pulse surveys**.

![Picture71](/images/pulse-surveys/01.png)

From here, click **Create** **pulse** **survey** and follow the wizard that will guide you through the set up. Give the pulse survey a **name** and choose who should receive the pulse survey. If you leave the organization fields empty - meaning no scope is defined - the survey will be **sent to all employees** in your account.

<Note>
  If you want to exclude **consultants** or **interns** from the pulse survey, you have the option to scope it based on **employment type**. If you also want the pulse to always exclude employees who are on **long‑term leave**, that option is available as well.
</Note>

![Picture109](/images/pulse-surveys/02.png)

Choose the **start date** for the first survey to be sent, then **specify how often** (in weeks) the survey should be repeated. The weekday on which the pulse's start date occurs will be the weekday on which the pulse repeats, according to the weekly frequency you have set.

![Picture104](/images/pulse-surveys/03.png)

In the next step, you'll see the **categories Hailey's pulse survey is based on**. Then, you'll review each category and select questions.

![Picture101](/images/pulse-surveys/04.png)

Each category consists of **several questions**, which are included in the survey by default. However, you can **manually select** which questions to keep and uncheck the ones you don’t want to include. All questions will be answered on a 1-10 Likert scale.

<Note>
  Each question includes an explanation of why it's asked, which you can view by hovering over the information symbol to the right.
</Note>

![Picture102](/images/pulse-surveys/05.png)

In the next step, you'll choose **how many questions** employees should answer **in each survey send out**. If the number of included questions exceeds the selected number per send-out, the questions will be randomised.

![Picture107](/images/pulse-surveys/06.png)

In the next step, you can enable a feature that allows all managers to view the pulse survey results of their reportees. For managers to be able to view results, they must have at least **five direct reportees** included in the pulse survey to see a widget on their homepage.

Read more about how to view results as a manager further in this guide.

![Picture106](/images/pulse-surveys/07.png)

The final step in the wizard displays **the settings** you have configured. If you're satisfied, check the box and launch your pulse survey!

<Note>
  You can change the settings you configured in the wizard after launching the survey.
</Note>

![Picture105](/images/pulse-surveys/08.png)

## How to answer a pulse survey

When a pulse survey is sent out, the included employees will receive an **email**, from where they can access the survey. All included employees will also receive a task under under **My tasks** in Hailey.

![](/images/pulse-surveys/09.png)

By clicking on **Go to Hailey**, employees will be directed to the pulse survey in their browser. Respondents will see an estimate of how long the survey will take to complete and will be informed that the survey is anonymous.

![](/images/pulse-surveys/10.png)

The questions will appear one by one, and each question is answered using a 1-10 Likert scale.

![](/images/pulse-surveys/11.png)

## How to view pulse survey results

When viewing the pulse survey results, you'll see the **real time results** from the most recent survey sent. The pulse must have at least five responses before the results can be viewed. The Pulse category shows the **avarage overall pulse survey score** and the trend chart shows development over time. By clicking on the individual categories you can see the avarage score per category and development over time.

It is also possible to **filter** the results by gender or by organizational filters if you click on the filter icon shown in the top right corner.

<Note>
  The arrows in each category show the trend based on previous results compared to the latest one.
</Note>

![Picture15](/images/pulse-surveys/12.png)

![Picture16](/images/pulse-surveys/13.png)

If you click on **Action** in the top right corner, you are able to:

- **Update questions**: Here, you can always edit the number of questions, as well as which questions will be included in the pulse.
- **Update frequency:** Here, you can **pause** the pulse survey, change the start date and update the frequency. When paused, no pulses will be sent out and the pulse will show the status **Paused**. All previous scores remain visible even while paused, and once you activate it again, the pulse will resume.
- **Update pulse survey:** Here you can update which employees should be included in the pulse, as well as decide whether you want to share the results with managers.
- **Delete the pulse:** Here, you can delete the pulse and all data contained in it.

![Picture108](/images/pulse-surveys/14.png)

## How to view results as a manager

If manager access to view results has been enabled, managers can view the pulse survey results of **their** **reportees** directly on their homepage. A widget displays the results from the most recent closed pulse and shows the avarge scores of their reportees.

<Note>
  The results can only be viewed **to managers who have at least five reportees** included in the most recently closed pulse. In addition, **at least three** of the reporting employees must have responded for the manager to be able to view the results, to secure anonymity.
</Note>

![Picture61](/images/pulse-surveys/15.png)

The overall pulse score is presented on the first page, following by a trend chart showing the development of the **six recent pulse send-outs**. Results for individual categories are displayed only when there are **at least three responses** within each category, and includes a brief explanatory text to provide context.

![Picture55](/images/pulse-surveys/16.png)

![Picture60](/images/pulse-surveys/17.png)

## Rights

As an admin in Hailey, you always have the right to manage pulse surveys. It is possible to grant other users the right to manage pulse surveys by **enabling** the right to **Manage all pulse surveys,** by creating a custom role.

<Warning>
  Please note that this provides **full** **access**, including the ability to start, update and delete pulse surveys, as well as view all results.
</Warning>

## FAQ's

**Question 1:** Can I change the minimun number of responses required to view results?

No. The minimum response threshold is fixed and cannot be changed. At least five responses are required for pulse survey results to be displayed.

**Question 2:** I can't see any pulse survey results. Why?

There can be several reasons why results are not displayed. Fewer than five employees may have responded to the survey, the survey may not have received any responses yet, or you may not have permission to view the results. If you are a manager, results are only visible if manager visibility has been enabled and the required thresholds have been met.

To resolve the issue, check that the survey has received at least five responses, that you have the correct permissions, and that manager visibility has been enabled if you are trying to view results as a manager.

**Question:** Can I edit a pulse survey after it has been launched?

Yes. You can update the included questions, survey frequency, participants, and manager visibility settings after launch. Do this by opening your pulse survey and click **Action**. Note that any changes made will be set for the next survey send out and will not affect closed ones.

**Question:** Employees are not receiving tasks to answer the pulse survey. What should I check?

If no task is available, make sure the survey has been launched, that the employee is included in the survey scope, and that the start date has passed. The employee also needs to be **invited** **to** **Hailey** to be able to receive a task to answer the pulse. Also make sure the employee has not been excluded due to employment type or long-term leave settings.

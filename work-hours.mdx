## What is Work hours?

When your employees are supposed to report time in Hailey, they need to have **Work hours** on their **Time and time off settings** under their profile. Work hours represent the hours an employee is expected to work and therefore, needs to report time for.

## Create Work hours

To get started, click on **Settings - Time and time off - Add new - Work hours.** Add a name and procentage of fullt time. The work hours is by default set to 8 hours a day, Monday to Friday. You can change this to your organizations needs. Then click **Save**.

<Note>
  For employees to be able to report time, it requires **Work hours combined** with a **Reporting policy** of the type Time and Time off. Read more about Reporting policy [HERE](/create-reporting-policy)
</Note>

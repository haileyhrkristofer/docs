## What are time off balances?

Time off balances allow you to visualize and track employees' remaining leave days in Hailey. Each employee has their Time off balance visualized on their profile under the **Time tab**.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-07-2026-01-43-29-9643-PM.png?width=668&height=364&name=image-png-Aug-07-2026-01-43-29-9643-PM.png)

## Before you get started

Time off balances are only needed if you want to track and display employees' remaining leave balance in Hailey.

Before creating a Time off balance, make sure that the relevant **Time off reasons** have been created. Read more about creating **Time off reasons** in the guide [HERE](/time-off-reason).

## How do you create a Time off balance?

Navigate to **Settings - Time & Time Off - Add new - Time Off Balance.** Start by adding a name for the balance. We recommend choosing a name that clearly reflects the agreement or reason it is connected to.

Then configure the balance settings:

- Which **Time off reasons** should reduce the balance.
- When the balance should be **refilled**.
- Any **carry-over limit** (if left empty, no limit on days will be applied).
- Whether negative balances should be prevented. Click **Save** when done.

## Rights

- **Time & time off - View** - gives access to the **Time** **tab** on the employee profile.
- **Time & Time Off - View & edit** - also allows adding automatically approved time off and deleting time and time off.

Read more about how **Roles & rights** work in the guide [HERE](/roles-and-rights).

## Common questions

**Question**: Why can't I create a Time off balances?

To create a Time off balance, you need the **View & edit** permission for **Settings - Time & Time Off**. You can manage this right under **Settings - Security - Roles & rights**.

Note: Access to these settings is typically reserved for administrators.

**Question**: Can I change a Time off balance after it has been created?

Yes, you can edit the Time off balance settings under **Settings - Time & time off** if changes are needed.

You can also adjust employees' balances manually or through an **import**. Read more how to **Import Time off balances** in the guide [HERE](/import-time-off-balance).

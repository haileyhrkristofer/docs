## What are standard fields?

**Standard fields** are predefined, built-in fields in Hailey that store information about employees. Some of the standard fields can be deactivated or activated. The same fields can also be scoped to a specific parts of the organisation. This helps ensure that employee profiles contain the right information and supports different organisational and international needs.

The standard fields that can be deactivated or deactivated are:

- Relationship (Personal - Emergency contact)
- Account number (Compensation - Bank details)
- Clearing number (Compensation - Bank details)
- IBAN (Compensation - Bank details)
- Bic (Compensation - Bank details)
- Swift (Compensation - Bank details)
- Contracted hours (Job - Contracted period)
- Contracted scope (Job - Contracted period)
- Notice period (Job - Terms)
- Vacation days (Job - Terms)

<Note>
  By default, the fields Relationship, Account number, Clearing number, Notice period and Vacation days will be **active**. The remaining fields (IBAN, Bic, Swift, Contracted hours & Contracted scope) are **inactive** by default, but can be easily activated in **Settings** - **Customisation**.
</Note>

## How to manage standard fields

To activate or deactivate standard fields, navigate to **Settings** - **Customisation**. Choose the tab Standard fields.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-27-2026-11-21-14-8290-AM.png?width=670&height=333&name=image-png-Jul-27-2026-11-21-14-8290-AM.png)

Click the three dots on the right for the field you wish to edit. You can then choose to activate or deactivate the field in question by clicking the toggle "**Active**" and then **Save**.

## Scope standard fields

You can also scope these standard fields to different parts of your organization. To scope a standard field, navigate to **Settings - Customisation - Standard fields**. Click on the three dots to the right of the field you wish to scope. Fill in the Departments, Legal entities or Locations you want to include in the scope. Once saved, this field will only be visible on the employee profiles matching the scope you have filled in.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-14-2026-09-54-53-6187-AM.png?width=670&height=387&name=image-png-Aug-14-2026-09-54-53-6187-AM.png)

## Rights

Only users with the Admin role can view and edit standard fields. This right cannot be assigned to other roles.

Read more about Roles & Rights in Hailey [**HERE**](/roles-and-rights).

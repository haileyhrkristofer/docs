## About sending documents for signature to several employees

Streamline your work by sending contracts, policies, and other important documents for signature to several of your existing employees at the same time.

## Before you get started

To be able to send a document for signing, ensure that the following information is registered on the employees profile:

- **Company Email**
- **Personal identity number** and **Issuer of the personal identity number** (for signing with BankID)

Also, make sure that you have a **Contract** **template** or a **Signable document** ready. Read more about setting them up [**HERE**](/create-contract-templates).

## How do you send a document for signing?

To send documents to several employees, first navigate to the **Employees** tab in the main menu.

**Select the employees** who are going to sign the document, click **Action** and select **Send document for signature.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-32-08-7664-PM.png?width=670&height=339&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-32-08-7664-PM.png)

- Select the **Contract template** you want to send for signing.

<Note>
  Note that only contract templates marked as available for **Signing with many employees** will appear as options.
</Note>

- Choose **Signing method** for the document - **BankID** or **Digital Autograph.** Please note that signing with BankID is only available in Sweden, Norway, Denmark, and Finland.
- Set a **Deadline** for signing the document.
- Under **Signing order,** choose who should sign the contract and in which order by adding **Signing steps.** For example, if you want the employee to sign the document first and then the employee's manager.
- If the contract template contains **Optional sections** or **Optional attachments**, select the parts that should be included in the document. Also fill in possible **Input fields** manually.
- Click **Preview** to verify that everything looks correct in the document, and then click **Send.** The chosen **signing order determines** whether the document is sent out to **all** **signing parties directly** or if there are **multiple steps**.

<Note>
  **Please note** that any **Variables** included in the contract template will retrieve the current information that is filled in on each employee’s profile. **Input fields** will be filled in with the same value in all contracts in the same mailing.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-40-50-3658-PM.png?width=670&height=452&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-40-50-3658-PM.png)

When the documents have been sent, you can **follow the signing process** in: **HR Hub - Signings.**

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-42-27-3316-PM.png?width=670&height=144&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-42-27-3316-PM.png)

<Tip>
  **If the signing needs to be canceled**, click on the **three dots to the right of the signing** and **Cancel**. Then it is no longer possible for the signing parties to sign the document and the signing process is deleted.
</Tip>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-43-01-6689-PM.png?width=670&height=150&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-43-01-6689-PM.png)

## How do you sign a document?

When a document is sent for signature, the employee will receive both a **task in Hailey,** and **an email** with this information. The document can be signed from both places.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-44-43-0408-PM.png?width=670&height=139&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-44-43-0408-PM.png)

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-43-51-1766-PM.png?width=670&height=535&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-43-51-1766-PM.png)

When all the **parties involved have signed the document,** it will be stored under the **Docs** tab in the employee's profile.

<Tip>
  Read more about **Employee folders** and settings for the **Docs** tab **[HERE](/employee-folders).**
</Tip>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-45-39-7931-PM.png?width=670&height=354&name=image-png-Apr-17-2026-12-45-39-7931-PM.png)

## Rights

To **Send documents for signature**, the right must be enabled under **Roles & rights** - **Actions.**

To access the signing overview in the HR Hub, the right to either **View** or **View & edit** has to be enabled in **Roles & rights** - **HR Hub - Signings.**

<Note>
  You can read more about Roles & rights [**HERE.**](/roles-and-rights)
</Note>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I find the signing in the HR Hub overview?">
    If the signing cannot be found in **HR Hub → Signings**, it may be because a filter is currently applied in the overview. Make sure that no filters are active.

    Another possibility is that the signing process has already been completed. Completed signings are no longer displayed in the **Signings** overview. In such cases, the signed document can instead be found under the **Docs** tab in the employee’s profile.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

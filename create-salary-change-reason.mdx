## What are salary change reasons?

A **Salary change reason** is the reason displayed when an employee's salary is changed. It helps organizations track and understand why salary changes are made.

Hailey includes two standard reasons by default: **New employment** and **Salary review**. These cannot be edited, archived, or deleted. The two standard reasons are automatically assigned when a salary change is made as part of a new employment or a salary review.

In addition, Admins in Hailey can create custom salary change reasons as needed.

## How do you create Salary change reasons?

To create Salary change reasons, navigate to Settings - Compensation - Add new - Salary change reason.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-24-2026-04-35-11-5335-PM.png?width=670&height=370&name=image-png-Aug-24-2026-04-35-11-5335-PM.png)

Enter a **Name** for the salary change reason and **Save** when done.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-24-2026-04-36-27-8483-PM.png?width=670&height=373&name=image-png-Aug-24-2026-04-36-27-8483-PM.png)

## When can you edit, archive or delete?

**Custom salary change reasons can be edited, archived or deleted:**

- **Edit** is always available. If you want to change the name, description, scope or add a translation click on the three dots to the right and edit and make the changes needed.
- **Archive** is always available. Archived reasons will no longer appear in dropdowns but remain visible on historical salary changes.
- **Delete** is available if the reason never has been used on a salary change.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Aug-24-2026-04-37-01-1119-PM.png?width=670&height=374&name=image-png-Aug-24-2026-04-37-01-1119-PM.png)

## Rights

To **create** a new salary change reason, you must have Admin access in Hailey.

To **use** a salary change reason, you must have permission to view and edit compensation for the relevant employee.

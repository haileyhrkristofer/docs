## What is approving and changing time off?

As a manager or an admin, you are able to approve, change or reject time off that have been requested by your employees. When an employee requests time off, you will receive a task in Hailey. An email will also be sent to the email adress registered on your profile.

## Before you get started

Before you can get started with approving and changing the time off of your employees, **Time and time off Settings** need to be set up in your Hailey account. Make sure you have the following set up:

- [Reporting policies](/create-reporting-policy)
- [Time off reasons](/time-off-reason)
- [Non-working days](/non-working-days)
- [Time off balances](/time-off-balances)
- [Work hours](/work-hours)

Each bullet point above takes you the the specific guide related to the topic.

## How do you approve time off?

When an employee registers time off the request is sent for approval. The approver will receive an **email notification** with a link to the Hailey start page.

To review the request, go to **My tasks** and open the time off approval task. From there, you can approve or reject the request.

If the employee has applied for multiple periods, you can also choose to approve only specific periods by unticking the ones you do not want to approve.

You can also approve time off via **Time** in the main menu by choosing the employees whose time off should be approved and click **Approve**.

<Tip>
  **Tip!** You can filter by team, manager, or other organizational criteria.
</Tip>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-07-25-5506-AM.png?width=664&height=227&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-07-25-5506-AM.png)

## How do you reject time off?

From the **Approve time off** -task under **My tasks**, you can also **reject** time off. A window will appear where you need to add a message to the employee.

The employee will receive an **email** notifying them that their time off request has been rejected, along with your message.

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-07-51-1490-AM.png?width=670&height=372&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-07-51-1490-AM.png)

## How do you delete employees' time off?

To delete an employee’s time off, go to the employee’s profile. You must be an admin or have the required [rights](#rights).

Employees can delete their own **unapproved** time off.

Click the **bin icon** in the top right corner of the calendar and select the day or days you want to delete.

The time displayed reflects what has been reported for the current month. To delete time off from a previous or future month, use the arrows on the left to navigate to the relevant month.

You can delete both future and past time off, including approved time off.

Are you using **time cycles**? Time off cannot be deleted if it falls within a period that has been approved through a time cycle. Only ones with the additional right to Edit approved time cycle can do this.

How do you add time off for employees?

As an admin, or as an employee with the [rights](#rights), you can also add new time off for employees.

To do this, go to the employee’s profile and click **Actions - Add time off**. Select the dates, enter a reason, and click **Save**.

<Note>
  By doing this, the time off is registered and approved immediately.
</Note>

![](https://docs.haileyhr.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-09-13-7925-AM.png?width=670&height=442&name=image-png-Jul-16-2026-08-09-13-7925-AM.png)

You can also add time off directly in the overview under **Time** in the main menu.

Use the fliters to find the employee for whom you want to add time off for, **click on the day** you want to register the time off on, and do the same procedure as above.

## How do you approve time cycles?

Learn more about time cycles and how to set them up [HERE.](/time-cycles)

Employees with **time cycles activated in their reporting policy** will receive a **task** on the first of each month to submit their time/time off for the previous month, regardless of whether any time off was taken.

Approvers will receive a **task** to approve time/time off displaying all employees requiring approval (e.g. managers' reportees), along with a summary and status of their time cycle.

Approvers can approve both submitted and unsubmitted time cycles.

Once the time cycle is approved, and the employee has not submitted their time cycle, the task disappears. This means employees **no longer have the possibility to register any time off during that month.**

<Note>
  Note that once a time cycle is approved, you can only **add** or **delete** time off for the period in question if you have the right to **Edit approved time cycle.**
</Note>

## Overview

Click **Time** in the main menu to open a time off **overview** of all employees you have right to view. This view shows all time off reported in Hailey, including the time off registered in a holiday plan. Read more about holiday plans [HERE](/plan-your-holidays-in-hailey).

<Tip>
  **Tip!** Use the **filters** on the left to display the employees or parts of the organization you want to see. You can also filter by time off details, such as status or reason, and save your filtered views for easy access.
</Tip>

## Rights

To be able to **approve** an employee's time off, you need to be their **Time & time off approver.** Read more about it [HERE](/create-reporting-policy).

The following rights may be useful when **changing** time off:

- **Time & time off - View:** gives access to the **Time** tab on the employee profile and the **Time overview**.
- **Time & time off - View & edit:** gives the right to delete approved time off and add automatically approved time off.
- **Time & time off - Edit approved time cycle:** gives the right to add or delete time off within an approved month.

If you want to read more about how **Roles & rights** work in Hailey, click [HERE](/roles-and-rights)!

## Common questions

Here are answers to some frequently asked questions that can help you when approving or managing time off.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can access the Time overview, but I can't approve an employee's time off. Why?">
    To approve an employee's time off, you must be assigned as their **Time & time off approver**.

    When you are assigned as the approver, you will receive both an **email notification** and a **task** in Hailey whenever an employee submits a time off request for approval.

    The approver is defined in the employee's **Reporting policy**. Read more about reporting policies [HERE](/create-reporting-policy).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I approve time off for multiple employees at the same time?">
    Yes. You can approve time off requests for multiple employees at once from the **Time overview** in the main menu.

    When you approve a request from the Time overview, the corresponding approval task is automatically removed from your **task list**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I approved time off by mistake. Can I undo it?">
    Once time off has been approved, it cannot be rejected. However, you can **delete** the time off.

    To do this, go to the employee's **Profile** and **Time** tab. In the top-right corner of the calendar, click the **trash can** icon to delete the time off.

    Note: You may need additional rights to delete approved time off.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add or delete time off for multiple employees at the same time?">
    You cannot add or delete time off for multiple employees at the same time.

    Time off can be added individually from the employees profile by clicking **Actions - Add time off**. You can also add time off directly from the **Time overview** by clicking a day in the employee's row. You can register for a single day or a period.

    You can delete time off from the employee's profile from the **Time** tab. Click the **trash can** icon in the top-right corner of the calendar and choose the days you want to delete.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I add or delete time off for an employee for a previous month?">
    If you're using **time cycles**, the month may already have been approved. Once a time cycle has been approved, only users with the **Edit approved time cycle** right can make changes to that month. Read more about time cycles [HERE](/time-cycles).

    If you're not using time cycles and you normally have permission to add and delete time off, you should also be able to make changes to time off in previous months.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

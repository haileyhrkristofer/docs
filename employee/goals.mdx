## What is employee goals?

Employee Goals help employees and managers set clear objectives, track progress, and stay aligned on priorities. By breaking goals down into measurable key results, it becomes easier to follow achievements, identify areas that need attention, and support ongoing development.

Employee Goals can also be linked to company, department, or team goals, creating a clear connection between individual contributions and broader organizational objectives.

## How do you add goals?

To Add a employee goal, go to the profile of the employee whom the goal will be added to and click **Actions** – **Add a goal.**

![](/images/employee/goals/01.png)

Hailey will then, step-by-step, guide you through the entire process. Keep reading this guide for a walkthrough of each step!

![](/images/employee/goals/02.png)

In the first step you need to **set a title** that shortly describes the purpose of the goal.

![](/images/employee/goals/03.png)

Once the title is set, it's time to **add key results**. The key results defines what needs to be done to achieve the goal, and makes the goal measurable. Add as many key results as you like!

For each key result, set whether progress should be measured **in percent (%)** or as **Not Done / Done**. If needed, you can also set a deadline for each key result.

![](/images/employee/goals/04.png)

The next step is optional, but in order to guide and motivate the employee, it could be a good idea to **set a deadline** for when the goal is expected to be achieved.

If you don't feel the need for a deadline - leave the field blank and skip over to the next step.

![](/images/employee/goals/05.png)

If the employees progress have impact on any of the company, department or team goals that are set up in your system, you can **connect the employee goal to a parent goal** in this step. If you ***don’t*** want to connect the employee goal to a parent goal - skip this step!

To connect an employee goal to a parent goal, there must be company, department and/or team goals set up to connect it to. Click [here](/goals-company-department-team) if you want to read more about how to add these types of goals.

<Note>
  You need the right to **View** or **View & edit** Company, Department or Team goals to connect an employee goal to a parent goal.
</Note>

![](/images/employee/goals/06.png)

In the final step, you get a summary of the goal and everything you've set up in previous steps.

If you need to make adjustments, click the left arrow. If you're happy with the settings, click the green check mark to add the goal to the employee's profile!

![](/images/employee/goals/07.png)

There you go! The goal is now added and saved under the **Goals** tab on the employee's profile.

![](/images/employee/goals/08.png)

## How do you follow up on goals?

Follow-ups can either be done via **Performance** – **Goals** in the main menu or from the **Goals** tab on the employee's profile. Regardless of where you choose to follow up on the goal from, the follow up process looks the same.

To follow up on a goal, start by clicking the goal and then click **Follow up**.

![](/images/employee/goals/09.png)

From here, you can adjust the status, change the goal's deadline, and set a follow-up date.

Progress is tracked by either marking a key result as **Done** or by moving the progress bar in the right direction. Use the comment section to further document the progress!

When the follow up is completed, click **Save**.

![](/images/employee/goals/10.png)

To get an overview of **comments** that has been added during previous follow ups, start by clicking the goal and then click the **Follow up** heading.

![](/images/employee/goals/11.png)

When key results are marked as done or increased in percentage and Saved, the progress bar will increase and the follow-up is saved.

To see progress of each key result, click on the heading Key result.

![](/images/employee/goals/12.png)

## Edit goal

To edit an employee goal, click the three dots to the right of the goal and select the **Edit** option.

<Note>
  The three dots will only appear if you have the right to **View & Edit**. If you _don't_ have the right to View & edidt, you _won't_ be able to edit an employee goal.
</Note>

![](/images/employee/goals/13.png)

From this view you can adjust deadlines, change the goal's title, and add, remove or edit the content of any key results. Don't forget to click **Save** when you're done editing!

![](/images/employee/goals/14.png)

## Overview of goals

Depending on your rights, there might be a Goal Overview avaliable for you to use to get an overview of the different goals in your organization.

If you have the right to **View/View & edit** goals on other employees, or the right to **View/View & edit**  Company, Department or Team goals you will fint the overview under **Performance** – **Goals** in the main menu.

In the overview, you can see for whom the goals are set, the goal names, deadlines, progress, status, and whether the goal is linked to a parent goal or not.

<Note>
  Use the **Filtering** function to sort and customize your goal overview.
</Note>

![](/images/employee/goals/15.png)

## Rights

The ability to view, add and follow up on employee goals is managed through **Settings** - **Security** - **Roles & rights**. Read more about Roles & Rights in the guide [**HERE.**](/roles-and-rights)

Rights for goals can be set separately for On **all employees**, On **Employees below you** and On **Your own profile**.

- **None -** Employee goals are hidden for the user.
- **View -** Allows the user to view employee goals at the selected level (all employees, employees below you or their own profile).
- **View & Edit -** Allows the user to view, add, edit and follow up on employee goals at the selected level (all employees, employees below you or their own profile). This right also allows the user to delete goals.

## Common questions

  **Question:** Why don't I see the **"Link goal to a parent goal"** step when adding an employee goal?
